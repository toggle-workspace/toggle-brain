# KMU — Rebranding Pitch Plan

_Prospect · Civil engineering & infrastructure · Malaysia · Drafted 2026-06-22_

> **Purpose.** Working plan for Toggle's pitch to KMU Group of Companies. Theme: **modernising the company's image.** This is the internal map — angle, narrative, scope, proof, gaps, and open questions — that the deck and proposal will be built from. Not the client-facing document yet.

---

## 1. The one-line angle

> **KMU has built RM700M+ of the infrastructure beneath Greater KL for 27 years. The brand doesn't show it. We modernise the image to match the company that actually exists.**

The gap is the pitch: a serious, proven, technically advanced contractor (German trenchless tech, 150+ projects, government-grade) presenting itself through an early-2010s website and dated identity. The rebrand closes the distance between **reputation earned** and **image projected**.

## 2. Why now — the business case (not just "it looks old")

A rebrand for a B2B contractor only sells if it ties to money and risk. Lead with these, confirm in discovery:

- **Credibility in tenders.** For government and large private bids, a dated brand quietly undercuts a strong technical submission. The brand should add confidence to a bid, not subtract it.
- **Talent & succession.** Engineering talent judges employers partly on how modern and stable they look. A 27-year firm likely facing a generational handover needs an image that recruits, not one that signals "old guard."
- **Partnerships & financing.** German tech partners, JV partners, and financiers all read the brand as a proxy for how the business is run.
- **The story is already strong.** This is a *re-expression*, not an invention. Lower risk, faster payoff — we're packaging proof that already exists.

### The room — decision dynamics (confirmed 2026-06-22)

- **Decision-maker:** the **Group Founder & Managing Director** — built KMU over 27 years. Almost certainly emotionally invested in the brand *he* created. Treat the current identity as his legacy, not a mistake.
- **Champion / our way in:** the founder's **son, currently in Business Development** — our introducer and the likely internal advocate for modernising (next-gen, growth-minded).
- **The dynamic:** classic founder + next-gen succession. We must win **two audiences in one room** — arm the son to sell modernisation internally, *and* frame the rebrand to the founder as **securing the legacy / "earning the next 27 years,"** never as fixing something broken.
- **Implication for the pitch:** open on the founder's achievements before any critique of the brand; carry the "why now" on the son's forward-looking BD agenda (new pipeline, bigger / private-sector bids); scrub any language that reads as dismissing the past.

## 3. Diagnosis — current state (verify, don't assume)

From public research (kmu.com.my):
- Website reads early-2010s: basic layout, dated type and palette, weak project storytelling despite an impressive portfolio.
- Strong raw material underused: RM700M+ portfolio, 150+ projects, named flagship works (D46/D47/D52), specialist trenchless capability via Great Tunnel.
- Multi-entity structure (KMU + Great Tunnel + property arm) with no clear brand architecture visible.

**To capture in discovery / audit:** current logo files, brand guidelines (if any), color/type usage, collateral (bid docs, profiles, signage, vehicle livery), org/brand-architecture intent, who owns the brand internally.

## 4. Proposed rebrand scope (modular — let KMU choose depth)

Framed as phases so KMU can buy a strategic core and expand. Pull service definitions by path; don't inline.

1. **Brand strategy & positioning** — discovery, stakeholder interviews, competitive scan, positioning platform, messaging, brand architecture for the group (KMU / Great Tunnel / property). → [brain/services/branding.md](../../../brain/services/branding.md)
2. **Visual identity system** — logo (refresh vs redraw), palette, typography, grid, photography/iconography direction, brand guidelines. → [brain/services/branding.md](../../../brain/services/branding.md) + [brain/services/creative-production.md](../../../brain/services/creative-production.md)
3. **Corporate website rebuild** — the single highest-visibility proof of "modern"; project-portfolio-led, credibility-first. → [brain/services/web-development.md](../../../brain/services/web-development.md)
4. **Collateral & applications** — company profile / capability deck, bid/tender templates, signage, vehicle & site livery, LinkedIn presence.
5. **Launch / rollout** — internal alignment, sequence for replacing assets, announcement.

> **Recommended pitch posture:** sell Phases 1–2 as the committed core (strategy + identity), present 3–5 as the natural rollout. Phase 3 (website) is the most tangible "before/after" and likely the emotional hook — consider a teaser concept in the pitch.

## 6. Suggested pitch flow (deck skeleton)

1. **The company KMU already is** — open on their proof (RM700M+, 27 years, trenchless, flagship projects). Earn the room.
2. **The brand that's representing it** — show the current site/identity honestly. Name the gap.
3. **What that gap costs** — tenders, talent, partnerships (Section 2).
4. **The idea** — "modernise the image to match the company." One memorable line.
5. **The work** — phased scope (Section 4), with a website/identity teaser concept.
6. **Why Toggle** — differentiators + adjacent proof (Section 5).
7. **How it runs** — process, phases, rough timeline, investment band.
8. **The ask** — discovery workshop as the committed first step.

## 7. Open questions

**For KMU (discovery call):**
- What's triggering the rebrand *now* — a bid, an anniversary, succession, a partnership?
- Equity vs revolution: refresh the existing logo/name or a fuller reinvention?
- Brand architecture: one master brand, or KMU + Great Tunnel + property as distinct?
- ✅ **Decision-maker (confirmed):** Group Founder & MD; introduction via his son in Business Development (our champion). Still to confirm: anyone else who signs off (board / co-owners)? Names + titles of both.
- Budget band and timeline expectations.
- Any sacred cows — colours, logo elements, name — that must survive?

**For Toggle (internal, before finalising the pitch):**
- Do we have any unpublished branding / website rebuild work to show as proof? (Closes the Section 5 gap.)
- Who leads/staffs this — is there a senior brand/design lead for the pitch and the work?
- Deliverable format for the pitch: **deck**, or **written proposal**, or both? (Affects what I build next.)
- Pricing approach for branding/web — `brain/pricing/` is performance-marketing oriented; do we have rate-card lines for brand/identity/web, or is this bespoke-quoted?
