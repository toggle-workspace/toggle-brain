# SignWell — Style Pack

> Base: `prompts/style-packs/toggle-default.md`. This file overrides only what differs.
> Status: prospect. Everything below is read from their live site on 13 September
> 2026, not from a brand guide. Replace with their real guidelines if Toggle wins the
> account.

## Voice overrides
- Tone: plain, practical and unfussy. They sell simplicity against DocuSign's bloat,
  and the copy matches. Short claims with a number attached.
- Words they use: eSignature, documents, agreements, compliant, audit trail, zero
  setup, no account needed, legally binding, sandbox, embedded signing.
- Words to avoid: enterprise, platform, suite, transformation, agreement
  intelligence. Those belong to DocuSign and PandaDoc, and using them hands SignWell's
  positioning to the companies it competes with.
- One number to settle before any copy ships: the homepage claims documents get
  signed 40% faster, `/docusign-alternative/` claims 60%. Ad copy has to pick one.

## Visual overrides
- Primary colors: not sampled. Take from their brand guide before producing creative.
- Typography: Proxima Nova, served through Adobe Typekit (`use.typekit.net/qpg6mlr`).
- Logo usage rules: unknown. Request the brand kit.
- Motion: unknown.
- Reference images: none held.

## Platform tone
- TikTok: not applicable. Neither SignWell nor any competitor runs TikTok ads.
- Meta: uncontested ground. DocuSign sells enterprise AI there and PandaDoc sells
  revenue operations, so the simple switching message is open. Write to an office
  manager holding a renewal quote, not to a procurement committee.
- LinkedIn: compliance and customer stories. SignNow proves the format works with 52
  ads built entirely from DocuSign switcher stories in clinical research.
- Google Search: their own live copy is the best reference. "No Account Needed to
  Sign, Free and Takes Under a Minute" and the API set, "Integrate document signing
  in days, not months. No bloated SDK, just clean."

## Examples of yes and no
- ✓ This works: "No account needed to sign. Free, and it takes under a minute."
  Concrete, checkable, and no competitor with a paywalled free tier can copy it.
- ✓ This works: "SOC 2, HIPAA and eIDAS on the free plan." Falsifiable, and true only
  of them in this price bracket.
- ✗ This doesn't: "Streamline your agreement workflows." Every competitor could paste
  that line unchanged, which is the test it fails.
