# SignWell discovery questions

Forty five minutes with a hard stop. You will not ask all of these. The starred ones
are the five that change what Toggle would actually do, so ask those first and let
the rest go if time runs out.

---

## 1. Goals and economics

- ⭐ **What counts as a qualified app signup?** A free account, an account that sends
  a first document, a paid conversion, or an API key? *(Why it matters: a $12 seat
  and a $275 API account cannot share one conversion action. Whatever they say
  decides the entire bidding setup.)*
- ⭐ **What is your cost per conversion today, and what would good look like?**
- How many qualified signups do you get a month, across all channels?
- What is the free to paid conversion rate?
- What is the average revenue per paid account?
- Of seats, API and enterprise, which revenue line matters most to you this year?
- Is there a payback period or a CAC ceiling you work to?

## 2. Audience and market

- ⭐ **You have eleven industry pages. Can you tier them?** Which are tier one by
  revenue today, which are tier two, and which are aspiration? *(Why it matters:
  eleven industries at $20,000 a month means none of them get a real budget.)*
- Which countries are you focused on? The Mexico NOM 151 page and eIDAS coverage
  suggest more than the US.
- Who is the buyer, and is it the same person as the user?
- Do you see a different buyer for the API product than for seats?
- Which integrations bring you the most customers? Clio, Close, QuickBooks, Xero,
  Jack Henry and Mambu all point at different verticals.

## 3. Journey and product

- ⭐ **Where is the biggest drop off in the journey today?** *(Ask it open, then
  offer the map: landing to signup, signup to first document sent, first document to
  the three document ceiling, ceiling to paid.)*
- Walk me through what happens when someone clicks talk to sales or requests a demo.
  Who picks it up, how fast, and what happens next?
- Is the self serve path really just Google login, or is there an email verification
  or onboarding step I did not see?
- Do you run email nurture or in app notifications to bring people back?
- What does activation mean to you inside the product?
- How many free users hit the three document ceiling in a month?

## 4. Tracking and data

- What is tracked today, and what do you know is missing?
- ⭐ **You write the GA4 client ID into a `ga_client_id` cookie with a two year
  expiry. Where does that go after someone signs up?** *(Why it matters: if it
  reaches your database, offline conversion import is a short job. If it was built
  and never wired up, that is the highest leverage fix available.)*
- Does HubSpot hold the signup record, or does that live somewhere else?
- Do you want free signups and paid conversions tracked as separate conversions with
  values attached?
- The call to action that appears after someone finishes drawing a signature is
  injected by JavaScript. Does that click fire an event?
- You have two GTM containers firing on the demo request page. Was that deliberate?
- What do you want to be able to see in twelve weeks that you cannot see now?

## 5. The last agency and the migration

- ⭐ **How were the campaigns built, and what exactly comes across?** *(Why it
  matters: campaigns do not move between Google Ads accounts with their history.
  Conversion history, learning and Quality Score stay behind. Somebody has to say
  this out loud and it should be you.)*
- When would Toggle get admin access to new accounts?
- Do the old accounts keep running during the transition, or is there a hard cut?
- Do you get any historical exports, or does that data disappear with them?
- What went wrong with the last agency, in your words?
- Is there anything in those accounts you want kept rather than rebuilt?

## 6. Channels and history

- Which channel drives the most qualified signups today?
- What have you tried on paid social before, if anything?
- What past campaigns worked, and what did not?
- Your free tools pull real traffic. Do you know what share of signups start there?
- What is your main pain point in converting more users?
- The homepage says 40% faster and the DocuSign page says 60% faster. Which is the
  number you stand behind?

## 7. Commercials and process

- Is the $20,000 fixed, or does it move if the numbers justify it?
- What reporting cadence do you want, and who reads it?
- What does the decision timeline look like?
- Who else is involved in the decision besides the two of you?
- What would make Toggle the obvious choice over the other two?

## Things to state rather than ask

Say these even if nobody asks, because the other two agencies probably will not.

1. **The migration will cost you performance for a few weeks.** No agency can move
   campaign history between accounts. Anyone who promises a clean lift and shift is
   either wrong or about to disappoint you in month two.
2. **Six of your six comparison pages have no signup button.** Only Log in. That is
   where the most expensive clicks in your category land. Pull one up on screen.
3. **We will not forecast until we see the account.** Five numbers are missing and
   guessing at them in a pitch would be dishonest. Five working days after access
   and you have a real one.
