## What SignWell sells

Five price points on one platform. Free at 3 documents a month. Light at $12 a user
monthly. Business at $36 a user monthly. An API plan at $275 a month base, then
$0.85 falling to $0.20 per document. Enterprise is custom and gated behind sales.

The API plan runs 8 to 23 times a seat plan, which makes "qualified app signup"
ambiguous as a bidding target until Henry defines it.

Claimed proof: 65,000 plus businesses, 20 million plus documents signed, 4.9 on
Capterra, 4.8 on G2. SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, GDPR, eIDAS, ESIGN, UETA and Mexican
NOM 151 compliance, included on every plan including free, which is their stated
wedge against competitors who gate compliance behind higher tiers.

17 employees, growing 36.4% year over year as of March 2026 per Crustdata. A third
party snapshot put revenue near $5M ARR in 2024, which is unconfirmed and should not
be repeated to the client as fact.
