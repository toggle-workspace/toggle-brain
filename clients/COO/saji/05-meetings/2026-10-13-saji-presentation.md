## Campaign direction: Low Key Raya

- **The question behind the idea:** does Low Key Raya mean celebrating less, or finding more meaning in how we celebrate?
- **Three pillars:**
  - Connect: the brand film.
  - Celebrate: content.
  - Convert: sales.
- **Competitors:** they read as glam and typical. That leaves Saji room to own a more values-led, intimate position.
- **Saji's response:** the client said the direction works for them both personally and commercially.

## Brand film

- **Title and tagline:** *Nilai diwarisi, tersaji dari hati*.
- **The protagonist:** a mother juggling work, family and Raya prep. She doesn't need advice; she needs someone to notice her. The key beat is a thank-you, or a moment of recognition from her family.
- **Changes agreed:**
  - Add a subtle male presence, either a father or the late husband. He observes and doesn't express much, which reinforces the values passed down through the generations. Saji agreed.
  - Keep the conflict internal (self-doubt, juggling) rather than external.
- **Length:** about 3 minutes. Saji noted that past films ran long.

## Contest mechanic

- **Dates:** the contest runs 30 January to 11 April 2027, about 10 weeks. The landing page goes live by 15 January.
- **Prizes, from the deck's prize slides:**
  - Main prizes of RM10,000, RM5,000 x 3 and RM3,000 x 5.
  - RM100 x 10 winners every week, plus 100 RM50 brand vouchers.
  - A year of SAJI products for first place.
  - TerSAJI Raya Collection packs.
- **The two options:**
  - Option 1 was Mata points.
  - Option 2 was buy RM25, answer a weekly question and upload the receipt.
- **Saji's decision: a hybrid of both.**
  - A simple entry is the baseline.
  - Receipt uploads and event attendance earn extra Mata.
  - The points balance must be visible to the shopper.
  - Event bonus points must not disadvantage shoppers outside KL.
- **The full design is in** `01-strategy/hybrid-contest-mechanic-2026-10-14.md`.
