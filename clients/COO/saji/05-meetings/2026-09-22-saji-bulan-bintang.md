# Saji x Bulan Bintang joint meeting

**Date:** 22 September 2026
**Present:** Saji (Delima Oil Products), Bulan Bintang, COO
**Outcome:** Bulan Bintang put a price on the collaboration. Saji took it away to review.

---

## Saji brand context, recorded because it had not been written down before

- FGV subsidiary, 93% FGV owned, formerly public listed. The biggest FGV subsidiary.
- Targeting RM1B revenue. Consumer products run at about RM2B annual revenue, 70% domestic.
- About 100 consumer SKUs. A 25 person marketing team across five sub-departments.
- Flagship oil brand holds 50%+ household penetration and about 60% market share.
- Monthly volume about 3M bottles. The stated goal is to push household penetration toward 93%.
- 93% of customers are Malay. The target is families with children up to 12.
- Ramadan and Raya is the biggest campaign period every year.
- **No TV or radio spend at all. Digital first, with growing organic reach.** Last year ran about
  50M views across digital platforms, mostly organic.
- The Raya film runs five to seven minutes and launches pre-Ramadan every year.

That 50M mostly organic number matters for the plan. Saji has reach and no identity layer
underneath it.

## What Bulan Bintang asked for

**A title sponsorship with a floor of RM1.5 million in value**, split 40% product and 60% monetary,
which works out to roughly RM300,000 cash and RM200,000 product at the tier discussed in the room.
Saji took it away to review internally.

Saji's own framing was that this is a **collaboration and not a sponsorship**, so it has to be a
mutual value exchange.

| Side | What they bring |
|---|---|
| Saji | Retail visibility across 80 to 100% of key accounts, product sampling, digital content featuring Bulan Bintang |
| Bulan Bintang | Traffic, audience access, roughly 400,000 unique data entries, media value |

## Mechanics discussed

Three options were tabled for the joint campaign:

1. **Buy and win.** Purchase both brands, submit receipts, enter the weekly draw.
2. **Unlock Raya reward.** Buy selected Saji products, unlock a Bulan Bintang voucher or discount,
   auto-qualify for the weekly prize.
3. **Raya shopping checklist.** Complete a multi-SKU checklist across both brands to qualify.

Prize structure discussed: a 10 week campaign, four weekly winners per week, and **one grand prize
golden ticket per week**. That last point does not fit the prize pool and is carried as an open item.

**The pop-up concept was agreed in principle.** A Saji kiosk outside Bulan Bintang stores, which
reduces the dual-receipt friction by putting both purchases in one visit, drives foot traffic with an
exclusive mystery gift, and captures proof of spend at Bulan Bintang.

Digital entry runs through a landing page, with a QR scan at the pop-up for instant participation.

**Saji committed 300,000 product sampling packs**, budget around RM500,000, for inclusion in Bulan
Bintang packaging. This is double the 150,000 packs assumed in rev2.

Saji is open to featuring collaboration partners in the Raya film and in PR activations.

## Open items this created

1. **The RM1.5M floor.** Saji has to decide whether any tier is acceptable. This is the item that
   decides whether the pop-up mechanic survives.
2. **One golden ticket per week does not fit the prize pool.** Ten at RM29,000 would be RM290,000
   against a pool of RM145,000 at the time.
3. **The sampling pack number changed.** 300,000 packs at about RM500,000, against the 150,000
   assumed in the rev2 deck.
4. Both sides to revert internally on partnership structure before confirming next steps.

## What was produced

Nothing. This was a commercial conversation and it fed the 24 September COO internal.
