# COO sit-down, Saji RaRa 2027: the rev2 revisions

**Date:** 15 September 2026
**Present:** COO and Toggle
**Outcome:** rev2 of the proposal. One budget, a kitchen prize idea, and an access checklist.

> Contains the working media and service fee split. Internal to Toggle and COO.

---

## Decision 1: one budget at RM145,000 all in

The campaign budget is fixed at **RM145,000**, covering ad spend, production fees, service fee and
other marketing fees. The RM160,000 tier from rev1 is dropped and the deck presents one number.

Inside it:

| Component | Amount |
|---|---|
| Working media | RM100,000 |
| Toggle and COO service fee | RM45,000 |
| **Total** | **RM145,000** |

**The forecast is built on RM100,000, and the deck presents the outcomes against RM145,000.** The
media and fee split does not appear in the deck, in the slides or in the speaker notes, and the deck
is built so that no reader can derive it. Cost per entry, blended CPM and every per-line ringgit
figure are absent for that reason: any one of them makes the split solvable. Allocation appears as
percentages only.

**Two separate RM145,000 figures now run through this campaign and they are not the same money.**
The contest prize pool is RM145,000, funded by the client. The campaign investment is another
RM145,000. Total client outlay is RM290,000. Every slide that names either one says which it means,
and slide 13's speaker notes carry the line to say out loud if anyone looks confused.

### What RM100,000 delivers

| | Brief target | This plan, paid |
|---|---|---|
| Contest entries | 4,000 | 4,000 |
| Paid reach | 15M | 13.91M |
| Video views | 40M campaign wide | 15.06M |
| Impressions | Not set | 25.74M |
| Entries per 1M reached | Not set | 288, against 273 in 2026 |

Cost per entry lands at **RM10.00** on the contest line, which is a 3.6% landing page conversion
against 111,111 contest sessions. rev1 planned 2.0% and RM18, deliberately not crediting the pop-up.
rev2 credits it, and the argument is the two things 2026 did not have: a landing page with a pixel,
and both receipts in the shopper's hand at the counter.

**The cost of that, recorded plainly. There is no cushion left on entries.** 3.6% sits at the
confident end of the credible band rather than the middle, and if the real rate comes in at 3.0%
entries land at 3,333 with no budget headroom to buy the difference. The mitigation is measurement
speed rather than money: the page reports daily, so we will know which row we are on inside the
first two weeks of a ten week contest and can move weight from video into contest while it still
matters.

### The reach trade that was declined

**RM105,000 of working media hits 15.00M paid reach exactly**, clearing every briefed KPI on paid
alone. It costs RM5,000 of the service fee, taking it to RM40,000.

The decision in the room was to **hold the fee at RM45,000 and accept the 7.2% reach shortfall**, on
the basis that the 15M KPI is campaign wide and the non-paid contribution closes it. Recorded here
so the trade is visible if the client pushes back on reach.

## Decision 2: the kitchen redesign goes in as a prize idea

New slide 14, offered **alongside** the RM29,000 Golden Ticket rather than replacing it. Uncosted,
not in the plan, and presented as a question for the client to react to.

It is the client's own idea coming back to them. A free kitchen renovation was brainstormed in the
11 September discussion, alongside gold hidden in product and the Saji for a year framing.

The case: Saji lives in a kitchen and a cash prize leaves no trace of that. A cheque handover is one
photograph; a kitchen reveal is a series the campaign otherwise has to invent.

What has to be answered before it goes anywhere: a contractor quote range, a build window finishing
before the 17 April fulfilment date, and liability if a build goes wrong in a winner's home. If the
client bites, it either converts a block of the 40 weekly prizes or it needs a trade sponsor.

**Our recommendation is unchanged: 40 weekly winners plus the RM29,000 Golden Ticket.**

## Decision 3: the deck closes on an access checklist

New slide 29, in three columns: platform access, page and domain and data, and brand and partner and
legal. Meta Business Manager and ad account, TikTok Business Center and Ads Manager, Google Ads
manager linking, YouTube channel access, GA4 and Tag Manager, the contest domain and DNS, the entry
database and PDPA controller, brand assets, the SKU list, the Bulan Bintang agreement and the permit
owner.

**The critical path is two items: pixel access and the domain.** Without them the page cannot report
a conversion, and the conversion event is what every measurement argument in the deck rests on,
including the mitigation for the 3.6% risk above.

## What did not change

The mechanic. RM25 of Saji plus RM150 of Bulan Bintang across two receipts, with the pop-up counter
inside all 14 boutiques removing the friction. Slides 9 to 13 carry over from rev1 untouched apart
from slide 13's headline, which now says "prize pool" so the two RM145,000s cannot be confused.

**The landing page is unchanged and rev2 does not create a duplicate.** Nothing in this revision
touches it: the prize pool is still RM145,000 with 40 weekly winners and the RM29,000 Golden Ticket,
the kitchen redesign is only a suggestion, and neither the budget nor the access checklist belongs
on a consumer page. Slides 25 and 26 keep pointing at
`../02-creative/tersaji-raya-2027-contest-landing-page-rev1.html`.

## Open items this created

1. Confirm COO and the client are both clear that the prize pool and the campaign investment are
   separate money, and that total outlay is RM290,000.
2. If the client insists on 15M reach from paid alone, the answer is RM105,000 of working media, not
   a re-cut of this plan.
3. 3.6% conversion has no safety margin. Agree now what happens if weeks 1 and 2 read 3.0%.
4. The kitchen redesign needs a price before it can be more than a slide.
