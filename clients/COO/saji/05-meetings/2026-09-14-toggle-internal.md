# Toggle internal, Saji RaRa 2027: how people participate

**Date:** 14 September 2026
**Present:** Toggle team
**Outcome:** rev1 of the proposal. The mechanic stays as briefed and the friction moves.

---

## The decision

rev0 recommended changing the client's mechanic. One purchase would qualify, both brands would earn
five entries plus the only route into the grand prize tier. The team took the opposite position on
14 September.

**Keep the mechanic exactly as the client wrote it.** RM25 of Saji, RM150 of Bulan Bintang, two
receipts. Improve the customer experience by changing where the shopper stands when she buys, not
what she is asked to buy.

Three problems were named in the room, and each got an answer.

## Problem 1: customer experience

**The answer is a Saji pop-up counter inside every Bulan Bintang store.**

Both qualifying purchases then happen in one trip, in one location, with the two receipts printed
minutes apart instead of days apart. The nine step journey in the brief becomes six, and nothing in
the client's mechanic has to change.

It is accessible and exclusive at the same time. Anyone can walk into a boutique, so nobody is
locked out, but the campaign rewards only exist at the counters, so the people who do walk in get
something the grocery shopper cannot.

It also produces data Saji does not currently hold. Saji is perceived as a B40 brand. Every pop-up
receipt pairs RM25 of Saji with RM150 or more of apparel, which is first-party basket evidence for a
higher income shopper.

**What we will and will not claim.** Bulan Bintang sells at roughly RM289 a piece through 14
boutiques and skews suburban. That supports M40. It does not support T20, so the deck says M40 and
flags T20 as unproven rather than asserting it.

## Problem 2: novelty

Three devices, all gated to stock sold at the pop-up counters.

1. **A mystery gift** for a spend above the RM25 entry floor. RM50 is Toggle's proposed threshold,
   double the floor. The client sets the real number.
2. **Campaign-specific packaging**, a Saji x Bulan Bintang pack sold only through the counters.
3. **A Golden Ticket**, hidden at random in pop-up stock, winning a grand prize outright.

## Problem 3: the prize

Solved by the client's own RM145,000, which does not move. What moves is the shape, so the contest
finally has a headline prize rather than 50 identical ones.

| Tier | Count | Value each | Subtotal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly winners, 4 a week across 10 weeks | 40 | RM2,900 | RM116,000 |
| Golden Ticket grand prize | 1 | RM29,000 | RM29,000 |
| | | **Total** | **RM145,000** |

The Golden Ticket is worth exactly ten weekly prizes. The one trade is the winner count, 50 down to
40. The fallback, if the client will not give up ten winners, is to keep all 50 and ask Bulan
Bintang to fund the Golden Ticket.

## The media consequence

Cost per entry rises from RM9 to **RM18**, a 2.0% landing page conversion instead of 4.0%. 2027 is
the first year of this contest to require two purchases and two receipts, so there is no Saji data
point and nothing to borrow from the Malaysian benchmarks. Halving the rate is a safety margin.

At RM18 the contest line has to carry RM72,000 instead of RM45,000, and the budget options move with
it.

| | Contest line | Entries | Paid reach | Paid views |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RM135,000, recommended | RM72,000 | 4,000 | 15.1M | 14.7M |
| RM160,000 | RM72,000 | 4,000 | 20.0M | 20.1M |

The RM90,000 discussed with COO on 11 September returns 2,083 entries and 11.4M reach at RM18, so it
clears neither target and comes out of the deck.

## What this costs that nobody has budgeted

Counter build, stock, staffing, the mystery gift and the Golden Ticket packaging change are all
on-ground and client costs. Not a ringgit of either media option pays for them, and the deck says so
on the caveats slide.

## Open items this created

1. Bulan Bintang has to agree to host a competitor category in all 14 boutiques for 10 weeks. The
   whole plan rests on a partner decision Toggle does not control.
2. A Golden Ticket cannot go inside a bottle of cooking oil. A neck tag, under-cap or carton insert
   adds packaging lead time, so it locks in mid December with the permit.
3. The weekly draw and the Golden Ticket are two games of chance. Counsel should confirm whether one
   permit covers both.
4. The 30/70 KL and Selangor geo split was set against Bulan Bintang's suburban skew. Now that the
   boutiques carry the mechanic, it should be set against the real store list, which we do not have.
5. Does a Bulan Bintang online purchase qualify, or does entry require a boutique receipt? The
   landing page currently says the boutique.
