## Campaign shape

Saji RaRa 2027 runs on five pillars: brand content (hero video), the Buy and Win contest, digital
amplification across Meta, TikTok, YouTube and Google, on-ground activation, and Saji Seikhlas Hati.
**The digital team takes pillars 2 and 3 only.**

The client is Saji under FGV and Delima Oil Products, with sister brands including Seri Pelangi,
Premeo, Adela and Gula Prai. The collaboration partner is Bulan Bintang, described in the room as a
mid-range baju raya brand aimed at suburban and outskirt markets.

## Timeline agreed in the room

- 25 January: campaign launch, the FGV umbrella event
- 1 February: digital campaign and Buy and Win contest start
- 8 February: first day of Ramadan
- 24 February: brand video launch, roughly two weeks before Raya
- 10 March: Raya
- 5 April: everything ends, a 10 week contest window

Chinese New Year falls on 6 February, inside the first week of the contest.

## Brand video deliverables

One master video at 6 minutes, 3 to 5 short form cutdowns for Shorts and social, 2 teasers before
launch, social adaptations for Facebook and Instagram, and static key visuals in multiple sizes
handled internally. The Bulan Bintang collaboration appears at the end of the brand video. A director
is engaged externally and the agency oversees production.

## The contest, and the problem with it

**Mechanics as briefed:** purchase RM25 of Saji products, purchase RM150 of Bulan Bintang baju, so
RM175 total to enter. Prize pool RM145,000 across 53 winners, each receiving RM500 cash plus Saji
products for a year. Target 4,000 entries.

(COO's working deck says 50 winners, and 50 multiplied by RM2,900 is what actually produces
RM145,000. The 53 in these notes looks like a slip and needs confirming.)

**Participation has been falling:** 11,000 in 2022 down to roughly 3,700 in 2026.

**The friction COO named:** participants must buy from two separate brands at two separate locations.
QR codes were flagged as too many steps against a direct landing page click, and a landing page was
preferred over a QR code for contest entry. Website readiness was raised as a prerequisite, since
Saji's site must absorb the traffic during the contest period. A gamified checklist landing page was
floated, prompting users to complete both purchase steps.

**Three options to put to the client:**

1. Current mechanics, RM25 Saji plus RM150 Bulan Bintang
2. Buy Bulan Bintang only, receive a Saji voucher
3. Buy Saji only, receive a Bulan Bintang voucher or free gift

COO's own view was to present all three with the strategy rationale and to flag the UX friction of
the current one.

## Digital amplification as discussed

Meta and TikTok carry the contest, YouTube and Google carry video views. Two objectives: awareness
pushing the brand and Raya video, and conversion driving contest entries.

**Geo split agreed:** KL and Selangor as one campaign tier, other states as a separate tier, so
budget does not concentrate in KL.

**Budget estimate in the room:** roughly RM90,000 for 10 weeks to hit 45 million impressions at a
RM2 to RM3 CPM. At a RM1.50 CPM the same impressions cost about RM67,500.

Chinese New Year on 6 February was flagged as a cost and noise problem. Influencer briefing focuses
on Instagram and TikTok, not Threads.

**KPIs from the brief:** 40 million video views, 15 million reach, 3% engagement rate, 4,000 contest
entries.

## Bulan Bintang collaboration

The stated rationale is to drive Bulan Bintang's audience toward buying Saji products. Discussed in
the room: a limited edition Saji x Bulan Bintang pack, an in-store QR on Saji products, and inserts
in Bulan Bintang packaging as entry triggers.

Prize ideas brainstormed: gold hidden in product as a treasure hunt, a free kitchen renovation, and
the "Saji for a year" framing, with Nestle's salary-for-a-year campaign at a RM4M prize pool used as
the reference point.

## What Toggle did with this

All five action items are answered in
`../01-strategy/media-plan-and-findings-2026-09-13.md` and the accompanying deck. The two findings
worth flagging back to COO:

- **No Malaysian contest was found that required purchases from two separate brands at two separate
  retailers for one entry.** Every benchmark either puts both brands in one receipt at one store, or
  makes the partner brand the prize or the reward. That absence is the strongest argument for
  changing the mechanic.
- **Bulan Bintang is running its own contest right now**, "Terrrpaling Raya Finale", a QR scan for a
  RM400,000 cash pool, verified on their own site. The RM145,000 two-brand contest would sit next to
  a bigger and simpler one run by the collaboration partner.
