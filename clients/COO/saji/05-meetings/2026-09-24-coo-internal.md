# COO internal, Saji RaRa 2027: the pivot to a point system

**Date:** 24 September 2026
**Present:** COO and Toggle
**Outcome:** The pop-up mechanic is dropped. Build a point system with an online to offline flow.

---

## Decision 1: Saji narrowed what they want

Saji's stance, as recorded in the room:

- They do not need the full package. They want to **push new product sampling**.
- Not interested in T-store. The focus is **ground activation**, meaning bazaars and roadshows.
- **Not committing to guaranteed sales numbers.**
- Collaboration with Bulan Bintang is **likely minimal**, so the mechanic has to be flexible enough
  to work with or without them.
- Saji is unlikely to take the Gold tier at 500k (40% product, 60% cash). Probably Silver or Bronze.
- A minimum budget of 50k was floated. Full cost breakdown still needed for logistics, packaging and
  manpower.
- Cost split principle: Bulan Bintang bears their own costs, the team bears its own portion.

**This kills the rev2 mechanic.** rev2 said every part of its argument rested on Bulan Bintang
hosting a Saji counter in all 14 boutiques. That yes is now unlikely, and the 22 September meeting
priced it at a RM1.5M floor.

**The 50k needs qualifying.** It reads as the Bulan Bintang sponsorship tier rather than our campaign
budget, and the decks continue to be built on the RM145,000 campaign investment. Carried as an open
item.

## Decision 2: a point system replaces the pop-up

Yang's proposal, as tabled:

- Receipt value converts to points. Higher spend means a higher chance at the grand prize.
- Works as a leaderboard, so a gamified contest rather than a single draw.
- **Flexible: it can incorporate Bulan Bintang receipts or run Saji only.** That flexibility is the
  whole reason it was chosen.

## Decision 3: an online to offline flow

- Run digital ads to drive awareness and sign-ups through a landing page.
- Roadshow and bazaar stops where participants scan a QR code for bonus points.
- An offline spin-the-wheel activation at the roadshow, for exclusive on-site Saji merchandise.
- Weekly bonus QR codes to sustain engagement, similar to the HWC weekly promo mechanic.

**The landing page now needs more than a form.** Participant registration, receipt scanning and a
live leaderboard. Customer data capture is a secondary benefit, following the Media Prima approach.

## Decision 4: ground activation shape

- Promoters at selected high-revenue Saji stores plus popular bazaar spots.
- Number of trucks to confirm, and bazaar space to negotiate. Noted as expensive.
- **Preference is to keep it simple and avoid over-reliance on human promoters.**

## What did not change

The campaign window, the brand film timing and the KPIs. The landing page argument from rev2 also
survives untouched, and the 24 September discussion strengthened it: a page is now required for
registration, receipt scanning and the leaderboard, not only for entry.

## Open items this created

1. Saji to confirm the Bulan Bintang collaboration, yes or no, and at what level.
2. Finalize the mechanics before costing. Logistics, manpower and fulfilment all depend on the
   mechanic.
3. Check logistics cost and bazaar space rates, to confirm whether a 50k minimum is viable.
4. Clarify whether 50k is the campaign budget or the Bulan Bintang tier.

## Next step

submission from the rev2 timeline did not close, and became a revision cycle instead.
