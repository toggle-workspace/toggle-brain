# Saji, Ramadan Raya 2027 (via COO)

Toggle works through COO on this account. The end client is Delima Oil Products Sdn Bhd, a wholly
owned subsidiary of FGV Holdings Berhad, so every deliverable here ships to COO rather than to the
brand.

Saji is Malaysia's number one refined cooking oil at roughly 45.6% market share. Delima also owns
Seri Pelangi, Premeo, Adela and Gula Prai, and all of them ride along in the Ramadan Raya campaign.
The 2027 campaign runs with a collaboration partner, **Bulan Bintang**, a baju Melayu and baju
kurung brand founded in 2016 by Azzim Zahid Azmi, selling through 14 boutiques at roughly RM289 a
piece and skewing suburban rather than urban.

## Scope

The campaign has five pillars: brand video, Buy and Win contest, digital amplification, on-ground
activation, and Saji Seikhlas Hati (CSR).

- **COO owns:** the client relationship, the contest mechanics and the brand video.
- **Toggle owns:** the digital advertising portion, which is pillars 2 and 3.

COO's working deck asked Toggle two questions directly on its slide 20, "Digital Marketing Support?"
and "Viral Portal budget portion?", and left the Digital Amplification section (slides 27 to 31)
empty apart from "KOL?" and "Thread?". The proposal in `01-strategy/` fills that gap.

**The current proposal is rev2.** Three revisions so far. rev0 recommended changing the client's
contest rules. rev1, after the Toggle internal on 14 September 2026, keeps them exactly as briefed
and removes the friction with a Saji pop-up counter inside every Bulan Bintang boutique. rev2, after
the COO sit-down on 15 September, fixes one budget at RM145,000 all in, adds a kitchen redesign as a
prize idea, and closes the deck on a client access checklist. Earlier revisions stay in place as the

## The five year trend that drives everything

Contest entries have fallen every year since 2022 while media output climbed. The last column is the
one the proposal is built on.

| Year | Impressions | Views | Reach | Engagement | Entries | Entries per 1M reached |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 15.06M | 3.40M | 4.46M | 0.94M | 11,834 | 2,653 |
| 2023 | 40.03M | 13.81M | 15.61M | 17.35M | 8,464 | 542 |
| 2024 | 50.40M | 10.43M | 15.66M | 6.85M | 7,576 | 484 |
| 2025 | 51.60M | 11.21M | 20.12M | 1.88M | 4,179 | 208 |
| 2026 | 51.00M | 38.82M | 13.80M | 0.04M | 3,765 | 273 |

Hold 2026's rate of 273 and it takes 14.7M reached to land 4,000 entries, which is why the brief's
15M reach KPI is internally consistent. It stops being consistent once the cost of entry rises from
RM25 in one receipt to RM175 across two brands.

## The brief as received

The Buy and Win contest asks for RM25 of Saji products plus RM150 of Bulan Bintang baju raya, so
RM175 total across two retailers, with both receipts uploaded. Prize pool RM145,000 across 50
winners at RM500 cash plus RM2,400 of Saji products each. Target 4,000 entries.

Digital ad buy KPIs: 40 million video views, 15 million reach, 3% engagement rate, and increased
brand awareness and recall. The client also requires Kak Ji, the mascot introduced in 2026, to
appear in the 2027 content plan.

## What Toggle recommends (rev1)

1. **Keep the mechanic, move the shop.** RM25 of Saji plus RM150 of Bulan Bintang across two
   receipts stays exactly as briefed. A Saji pop-up counter inside every one of the 14 Bulan Bintang
   boutiques collapses the nine step journey into six, in one location, in one visit, with the two
   receipts printed minutes apart rather than days apart. It is accessible to anyone and exclusive
   at the same time, since the campaign rewards exist only at the counters, and it gives Saji
   first-party basket evidence for an M40 shopper it does not hold today.
2. **Give people a reason to walk in.** A mystery gift above a RM50 counter spend, a Saji x Bulan
   Bintang campaign pack sold only at the pop-ups, and a Golden Ticket hidden at random in pop-up
   stock that wins RM29,000 outright.
3. **Reshape the prize inside the same RM145,000.** 40 weekly winners at RM2,900 across ten weekly
   draws, which is RM116,000, plus the RM29,000 Golden Ticket. The total is untouched. The one trade
   is the winner count, 50 down to 40.
4. **Land entries on a landing page, not a QR code in the ad.** A QR code in a mobile in feed ad
   cannot be scanned by the device showing it, and it returns no click, no pixel, no conversion
   event and no cost per entry. QR codes stay for offline surfaces, each with its own tracking tag,
   and the pop-up counter code is now the most valuable of them.
5. **Fund the contest and the brand video separately.** Two engines, two budgets, two measurement
   sets, so the cheap outcome cannot eat the expensive one the way it did in 2026.
6. **Ask whether Bulan Bintang will join the prize.** A partner funded RM600 baju set per weekly
   winner is worth raising, since the collaborator is in the mechanic, the pop-up and the Golden
   Ticket but not in what a winner receives.

## The media model, rev2

**Internal, and the reason the deck reads the way it does.** The campaign investment is RM145,000
all in, of which **RM100,000 is working media and RM45,000 is the Toggle and COO service fee**. The
forecast is built on RM100,000 and the deck presents the outcomes against RM145,000.

**The split appears nowhere in the deck, including the speaker notes**, and the deck is built so
that no reader can derive it. Cost per entry, blended CPM and every per-line ringgit figure are
absent, because any one of them makes the split solvable. Allocation shows as percentages only.

| | Brief target | This plan, paid |
|---|---|---|
| Contest entries | 4,000 | 4,000 |
| Paid reach | 15M | 13.91M |
| Video views | 40M campaign wide | 15.06M |
| Impressions | Not set | 25.74M |
| Entries per 1M reached | Not set | 288, against 273 in 2026 |

Cost per entry is **RM10.00** on a RM40,000 contest line, which is a 3.6% landing page conversion
against 111,111 sessions. rev1 planned 2.0% and RM18 without crediting the pop-up. rev2 credits it,
on the strength of the two things 2026 did not have: a landing page with a pixel, and both receipts
in hand at the counter.

**There is no cushion left on entries.** 3.6% sits at the confident end of the band rather than the
middle. At 3.0% entries land at 3,333 with no headroom to buy the difference, so the mitigation is
measurement speed: the page reports daily and we will know which row we are on inside the first two
weeks of a ten week contest.

**The reach trade that was declined.** RM105,000 of working media hits 15.00M paid reach exactly and
clears every briefed KPI on paid alone, at the cost of RM5,000 of fee. The 15 September decision was
to hold the fee at RM45,000 and accept the 7.2% shortfall, treating the 15M KPI as campaign wide.

**Nothing in the RM145,000 covers pop-up cost.** Counter build, stock, staffing, the mystery gift and
the Golden Ticket packaging change sit with on-ground and with the client.

## Open items

- **Is the 40M views KPI paid only or campaign wide?** The whole media model turns on this. 2026
  reported 38.82M views against 51M impressions, a 76% view rate that no paid buy across three
  platforms reaches, so that number almost certainly includes KOL and organic. Paid only roughly
  doubles the budget.
- **What counts as a view, and what is engagement measured against?** 2026 reported 42k engagements
  and 2025 reported 1.88M. Those cannot be the same measurement.
- **Bulan Bintang runs its own contest.** "Terrrpaling Raya Finale", a QR scan for a RM400,000 cash
  pool, was live on their site on 13 September 2026. Our RM145,000 two brand contest would sit next
  to a bigger and simpler one. Confirm whether the windows overlap.
- **Contest licensing.** A pure random draw sits under the Common Gaming Houses Act 1953 and needs a
  permit. Adding a tiebreaker question is the usual route. Allow three to four weeks.
- **Two conflicts in the client's own slides.** The launch event is 25 January on the campaign house
  slide and 28 January on the project timeline slide. The winner count is 53 in the meeting notes
  and 50 in COO's working deck, and 50 is the number that multiplies out to RM145,000.
- **The 2025 winner count needs confirming.** The weekly table reads 4,179 participants against
  2,000 winners, close to a one in two chance. The PDF column alignment is loose, so check it against
  the source before quoting it in the room.
- **The landing page runs on placeholder data.** The entry count, the winner count and the four
  named winners are all hardcoded. Every one of them must read from the live database before launch.
  Numbers invented on a live contest page are a Trade Descriptions problem, not just a credibility one.
- **The landing page form posts nowhere.** Validation, the receipt progress meter and the thank-you
  state all work client side, but the submit handler needs wiring to a real endpoint, along with
  receipt storage and the Meta and TikTok conversion events that the whole media argument depends on.

### Opened by rev1

- **Will Bulan Bintang host a Saji counter in all 14 boutiques for 10 weeks, and on what terms?**
  The entire rev1 argument rests on a partner decision Toggle does not control. If the answer is no,
  the mechanic reverts to the nine step journey and the 2.0% planning conversion is optimistic
  rather than cautious.
- **Who funds and staffs the pop-ups?** Counter build, stock, staffing, the mystery gift and the
  packaging change are on-ground and client costs. Neither media option carries a ringgit of them.
- **Golden Ticket feasibility.** A ticket cannot go inside a bottle of cooking oil. It has to sit
  under the cap, on a neck tag or in the outer carton, which is a packaging line change with its own
  lead time, so it locks in mid December alongside the permit.
- **Permit scope.** The weekly draw and the randomized Golden Ticket are two games of chance.
  Counsel should confirm whether one permit under the Common Gaming Houses Act 1953 covers both.
- **Does the client accept 40 winners instead of 50?** The Golden Ticket is carved from the same
  RM145,000, so ten weekly winners pay for it. The fallback is to keep all 50 and ask Bulan Bintang
  to fund the ticket.
- **Where are the 14 boutiques?** The 30/70 KL and Selangor geo split was set against Bulan
  Bintang's suburban skew. Now that the boutiques carry the mechanic, it should be set against the
  real store list, which we do not have.
- **The mystery gift threshold.** RM50 is Toggle's proposal, double the entry floor. The client sets
  the real number.
- **Does a Bulan Bintang online purchase qualify?** The rev1 landing page says entry needs a
  boutique receipt, because the Saji counter is inside the store. The client has not ruled on it.
