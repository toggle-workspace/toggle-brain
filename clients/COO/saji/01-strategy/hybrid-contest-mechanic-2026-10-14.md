# Hybrid contest mechanic, 14 October 2026

At the 13 October presentation, Saji liked both contest mechanics and could not choose between them.
- **Option 1 (collect Mata)** suits contest fans.
- **Option 2 (buy RM25, answer, upload)** suits shoppers who want one easy chance without committing to collect points.

The hybrid uses Option 2 as the way in and Option 1 as the reason to keep buying. It replaces the
two option slides (63 to 65) in COO's final deck.

## The rules

1. **Entry (Option 2).** Buy at least RM25 of SAJI. Then scan the contest QR on the pack or the shelf, or tap an ad. Answer one question, register and upload the receipt. That gives you 1 entry.
2. **The question.** It is the same for all 10 weeks, and you answer it once, when you register. After that, you only scan and upload.
3. **Commitment (Option 1).** Every RM1 of SAJI is 1 Mata, and every 25 Mata is 1 more entry. The first RM25 receipt is the first 25 Mata, so it counts once, not twice.
4. **Four ways to earn Mata:**
   - A SAJI receipt: RM1 = 1 Mata. This is required.
   - The product of the week: 2x or 3x Mata.
   - A bonus QR code: 25 to 50 Mata.
   - Buying each week, or referring a friend: 25 Mata.

   Every route pays in steps of 25, so "25 Mata = 1 entry" is the only number a shopper needs to remember.
5. **The draws.**
   - **Weekly draw:** only that week's entries count, so shoppers buy again each week to stay in.
   - **Grand prize draw:** every entry since 30 January counts. It covers the top 3 prizes on the poster (1x RM10,000, 3x RM5,000, 5x RM3,000) and never resets.
6. **The balance.** After each upload, the contest page shows the shopper's Mata, their entries so far, and how much more SAJI gets the next entry. Shoppers can also check it any time by logging in with their phone number.
7. **Fairness and guardrails.**
   - Bonus QR codes are on special SAJI packs and at SAJI events in every state, and event codes pay the same as pack codes. This answers Saji's point about shoppers outside KL.
   - Each receipt counts once, and Mata per receipt is capped so a bulk buyer cannot take over the draw.

**The goal, as written on slide 1:** make joining easy so more shoppers enter, then reward every
repeat purchase from 30 January to 11 April.

## Decisions Jordan made on 14 October

- **Save your place.** A shopper who sees the ad at home can answer and register first, then upload the receipt after shopping. The entry goes live when the receipt arrives.
- **25 Mata = 1 entry,** with RM25 as the minimum for the first receipt.
- **The two draws:** the weekly draw counts only that week's entries, and the grand prize draw counts everything.
- **"Top 3 prizes" means the poster's three main tiers,** not a leaderboard for top collectors.
- **WhatsApp is dropped.** Reminders, the WhatsApp balance message and WhatsApp receipt entry are all out, because nobody knows yet whether a WhatsApp Business setup is possible. The balance lives on the contest page instead.
- **English slides with no audience age groups on them.**

## Internal audience reasoning (not on the slides)

The COO and Toggle whiteboard scored four age groups on IT knowledge, willingness, commitment,
interest and experience. Boomers and Millennials came out as the main audience, Gen Z second and
Gen Alpha third.

The key tension is that boomers have the most interest (100%) and the least IT knowledge (20%).
That is why the entry has to be simple. It is also why the offline help matters:
- a promoter at the sampling booth;
- a flyer with the steps;
- children or grandchildren scanning for their parents, which matches Aisyah and Nenek in the brand film.

## How the slides were checked
