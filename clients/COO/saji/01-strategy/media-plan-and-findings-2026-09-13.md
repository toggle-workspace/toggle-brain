# Saji x Bulan Bintang, RaRa 2027: media plan, model and findings

Prepared for COO, 13 September 2026. This document carries the working behind
`saji-bulan-bintang-digital-advertising-proposal-2026-09-13.pptx`. Every figure in the deck can be
reconciled from here.

## 1. The diagnosis

Contest entries have fallen five years running while media output climbed.

| Year | Impressions | Views | Reach | Engagement | Entries | Entries per 1M reached |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 15.06M | 3.40M | 4.46M | 0.94M | 11,834 | 2,653 |
| 2023 | 40.03M | 13.81M | 15.61M | 17.35M | 8,464 | 542 |
| 2024 | 50.40M | 10.43M | 15.66M | 6.85M | 7,576 | 484 |
| 2025 | 51.60M | 11.21M | 20.12M | 1.88M | 4,179 | 208 |
| 2026 | 51.00M | 38.82M | 13.80M | 0.04M | 3,765 | 273 |

Source: RARA 2025 deck (per platform actuals and the weekly entry table) and RARA 2026 deck (the
2022 to 2026 series).

In 2026 the campaign delivered 3.5 times the video views of 2025 and posted the lowest entry count
on record. Media was bought against impressions, and impressions is what it returned. Entries were
never the optimization target, because entries arrived by WhatsApp receipt photo, which neither Meta
nor TikTok can see.

**The 2027 tension.** The brief asks for roughly 6% more entries than 2026 while raising the cost of
entry from RM25 in one receipt to RM175 across two brands, two retailers and two shopping trips.

## 2. The rate card, and what is actually known

### Derived from the 2025 deck (hard)

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Google | 13,171,108 | not reported | 1,644,866 | 34,898 | n/a | 12.5% | 0.26% |
| Meta | 26,373,035 | 14,170,818 | 2,310,408 | 34,400 | 1.86 | 8.8% | 0.13% |
| TikTok | 11,523,177 | 5,957,220 | 7,258,989 | 1,224,367 | 1.93 | 63.0% | 10.6% |

calculable from them. These are planning estimates for Malaysian auction prices with festive

| Line | Platform | Objective | CPM | Freq | Per 1,000 reached | View rate | Per view / CPC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Contest | Meta | Conversions | RM22.00 | n/a | n/a | n/a | CPC RM0.45 |
| Contest | TikTok | Conversions | RM15.00 | n/a | n/a | n/a | CPC RM0.30 |

Meta's observed 8.8% view rate in 2025 reflects a mostly static reach buy, so the 55% Reels figure
is a different buy rather than a contradiction.

**The single question that fixes this column:** what did the 2026 campaign spend on digital, by
platform. One answer turns most of these assumptions into facts.

### Entry conversion, bracketed rather than asserted

In 2025, Meta delivered 34,400 link clicks against 4,179 total entries, so **12.1% is the ceiling**
if Meta carried the digital route. Counting TikTok's 1,224,367 clicks drops it to **0.33%**, but
TikTok counts taps that are not link clicks. The band is that wide because there has never been a
landing page to measure against.

The plan uses **4.0%** and shows the sensitivity rather than a false point estimate.

| Conversion | RM90,000 plan | RM135,000 plan | Cost per entry (contest line) | Read |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.5% | 2,604 | 3,125 | RM14.40 | Friction stress case, not a forecast |
| 4.0% | 4,167 | 5,000 | RM9.00 | What the plan is built on |
| 6.0% | 6,250 | 7,500 | RM6.00 | Upside if the mechanic lands well |

**The 2.5% row is a stress case, not a modelled rate.** No Malaysian contest in the research
required purchases from two separate brands at two separate retailers for one entry, so there is no
observed rate to borrow. It is what a third of conversion lost to friction would look like.

### The view definition problem

2026 reported 38.82M views against 51M impressions, a **76% view rate**. No paid buy across Meta,
TikTok and YouTube reaches 76%. That number almost certainly includes KOL and organic views from the
50 influencers and the dance challenge.

40M **paid** views on TikTok alone would cost about RM124,000 at RM0.0031 per view, before the
contest engine exists. This plan therefore treats 40M as a campaign wide target that the paid buy
contributes roughly 20M toward, and flags the assumption.

## 3. The two budget options

### Option 1: RM90,000 (the internal estimate)

Google RM15,000. Meta and TikTok share RM75,000, split 50% contest / 35% video and reach / 15%
engagement.

| Line | Spend | Delivers |
|---|---|---|
| Contest, Meta | RM18,750 | 41,667 sessions |
| Contest, TikTok | RM18,750 | 62,500 sessions |
| Video, TikTok | RM17,000 | 5.48M views, 4.07M reach, 7.73M impressions |
| Video, Meta | RM9,250 | 1.45M views, 1.47M reach, 2.64M impressions |
| Engagement, TikTok Spark | RM11,250 | 2.81M views, 2.40M reach, 4.33M impressions |
| YouTube | RM15,000 | 1.26M views, 2.32M reach, 3.95M impressions |

**Totals:** 104,167 sessions, **4,167 entries** at 4%, **11.0M paid views**, **11.6M paid reach**,
20.8M impressions. Cost per entry RM9.00 against the contest line, RM21.60 against the full budget,
close to the RM22.50 the team already assumed.

**Verdict:** hits entries only if the mechanic changes (2,604 if it does not), misses the 15M reach
KPI by 23%, and reaches a quarter of the 40M views on paid alone.

### Option 2: RM135,000 (recommended)

Google RM20,000. Meta and TikTok share RM115,000, split 39% contest / 50% video and reach / 11%
engagement.

| Line | Spend | Delivers |
|---|---|---|
| Contest, Meta | RM22,500 | 50,000 sessions |
| Contest, TikTok | RM22,500 | 75,000 sessions |
| Video, TikTok | RM38,000 | 12.26M views, 9.09M reach, 17.27M impressions |
| Video, Meta | RM20,000 | 3.13M views, 3.17M reach, 5.71M impressions |
| Engagement, TikTok Spark | RM12,000 | 3.00M views, 2.56M reach, 4.62M impressions |
| YouTube | RM20,000 | 1.68M views, 3.10M reach, 5.26M impressions |

**Totals:** 125,000 sessions, **5,000 entries** at 4%, **20.1M paid views**, **19.5M paid reach**,
35.4M impressions. Engagement lands around 2.3% on impressions and 4.2% on reach.

**Verdict:** clears entries with 25% cushion against friction risk, clears reach by 30%, and gets
paid halfway to 40M views with KOL and organic closing the gap.

### Why the split moves

The contest line saturates around RM45,000, because you can only convert people who will actually
buy. At RM90,000 the contest needs half the money. At RM135,000 it does not, and forcing it there
would buy entries we cannot convert. That is the answer to the proposed 50/30/20 split: correct at
the lower budget, wrong at the higher one.

### The non-paid remark

Both forecasts are **paid media only**. They exclude KOL and organic views from the 30 influencers,
both brands' owned channels, entries walked in from 320 sampling days across 120 outlets and 96
roadshow days across 32 outlets, POSM and on pack QR codes, the Sampul Raya, and earned media (2026
got 10 publications at campaign launch and 10 more at the music video launch). Actual outcomes should
land above these numbers.

### Geo

The contest runs as **two separate campaigns**, not two ad sets, so budget cannot leak to the
cheapest inventory. KL and Selangor take 30%, the other states take 70%. This matches Bulan Bintang's
suburban skew and the roughly 73% of Malaysians who live outside KL and Selangor. Inside a single
campaign the algorithm spends where conversions are cheapest, which is KL, and the outer states
starve. The brand video runs national.

## 4. Mechanics research

Five verified Malaysian benchmarks, checked 13 September 2026.

| Campaign | What they did | The lesson |
|---|---|---|
| HeroMarket x Tyson x First Pride, 1 Aug to 30 Sep 2026 | Buy any two packs of either brand in a single receipt | Two brands works when both sit in the same basket |
| Guardian x BRAND'S x Christy Ng, 1 Sep to 31 Oct 2025 | Spend RM100 on BRAND'S, win a Christy Ng bag, 20 winners | The partner brand can be the prize rather than a second purchase |
| 100PLUS x SHALS, 17 Jan to 15 Apr 2026 | Limited edition baju raya by a Malaysian label, awarded to top spenders in TikTok Live | FMCG and fashion works when the baju is the reward |
| AEON Merdeka Shop and Win, 30 Jul to 31 Aug 2026 | RM80 single receipt, scan the QR at checkout, 69 prizes over four weekly draws | The QR lives at the till, and weekly draws hold a long window |
| Bulan Bintang Terrrpaling Raya Finale, live now | QR scan for a RM400,000 cash pool | Our partner already runs a bigger and simpler contest |

**The finding is the absence.** No Malaysian contest found required purchases from two separate
brands at two separate retailers for one entry. Every example either puts both brands in one receipt
at one store, or makes the partner the prize or the reward.

### The three options for the client

| | A, as briefed | B, weighted entries (recommended) | C, voucher chain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry cost | RM175, both brands | RM25 or RM150, either brand | RM25 or RM150, either brand |
| Retailers | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| Steps to enter | 9 | 5 | 5 |
| Who can enter | One audience of three | All three | All three |
| Saji revenue | Capped by Bulan Bintang buyers | Every entrant buys, 5x pulls the rest | Every baju buyer gets sampled |
| Bulan Bintang revenue | Guaranteed RM150 per entry | 5x entries plus grand prize tier is the pull | Voucher drives the boutique trip |
| Tracking | Two receipts to verify | One or two, weighted | Unique code, cleanest |
| Malaysian precedent | None found | HeroMarket model | BRAND'S and 100PLUS model |

**Option B in one line:** "Satu beli, satu peluang. Dua beli, lima peluang." Buy RM25 of Saji for one
entry, RM150 of Bulan Bintang for one entry, or both for five entries and the only route into the
grand prize tier.

Both B and C keep the RM25 and RM150 thresholds intact where they apply, so the client's commercial
targets survive the change.

### The prize

The briefed prize is RM500 cash plus RM2,400 of Saji products, 50 winners, RM145,000. Bulan Bintang
appears nowhere in it, which is odd for a collaboration contest and leaves the partner with no reason
to co-fund.

**We have not moved the client's number.** RM145,000 across 50 winners at RM2,900 each is what the
deck leads with and what the landing page is built on. Every budget figure in this document assumes
it.

**Optional, and framed as a question rather than a recommendation:** adding a RM600 Bulan Bintang
family baju set to each prize would take it to RM3,500 per winner and RM175,000 total, with Saji's
RM145,000 untouched and Bulan Bintang funding the RM30,000. It is worth asking because the partner
currently appears in the mechanic, the 5x multiplier and the where to buy block, but contributes
nothing to what a winner actually receives. The client and the partner decide, not us.

**Structure:** five grand winners a week across the 10 week window, 50 in total, so no week passes
without a visible winner. COO's working deck proposes four draws, which would leave six of the ten
weeks with no winning moment.

The reason for many winners rather than few: in 2025 the contest had 2,000 winners against 4,179
entries, close to a one in two chance, and entries held up better that year than any since. Visible
winning is what keeps people entering. Confirm the 2025 figure against the source deck before
quoting it, since the PDF column alignment is loose.

## 5. QR code versus landing page

Over 90% of Meta and TikTok impressions in Malaysia are mobile in feed. **A QR code in that ad cannot
be scanned by the device showing it.** The viewer has to screenshot it and find a scanner that reads
from the gallery, or find a second device.

What a QR in the ad returns: no click, no pixel, no conversion event, no retargeting pool, no cost
per entry. The platforms never learn who converts, so they keep serving people who never enter. This
is precisely why 2026 bought views rather than entries.

What a landing page returns: the click is recorded, the pixel fires, the conversion event trains the
algorithm, a retargeting pool builds, and cost per entry becomes reportable daily. It also lets us
chase the people who started an entry and never finished.

**QR codes keep their place offline**, where the shopper is looking at a physical surface: shelf
talkers, neck tags, A-boards, block displays, posters and on pack stickers (the 2025 campaign printed
10,000 shelf talkers, 7,000 posters and 40,000 neck tags), Bulan Bintang boutique counters and
packaging inserts, the 150,000 Saji sample packs, the launch at Wisma FGV, the sampling and roadshow
days, and the Sampul Raya. Every code carries its own tracking tag so we can report which touchpoint
produced entries. This is the AEON model.

## 7. Caveats

1. Is the 40M views KPI paid only or campaign wide? The model assumes campaign wide. Paid only
   roughly doubles the budget.
2. What counts as a view? 2026's 76% view rate implies a 2 second definition, not 30 seconds.
3. Engagement measured on impressions or on reach? The same campaign reads 2.3% or 4.2%.
4. The 2026 spend is unknown, so every CPM in section 2 stays an assumption until the client shares
   it.
5. Chinese New Year on 6 February inflates the auction in contest weeks 1 and 2. A 20 to 40% CPM
   lift in that window is outside our control.
6. A pure random draw sits under the Common Gaming Houses Act 1953 and needs a permit. A tiebreaker
   question is the usual route. Allow three to four weeks from brief to launch.
7. Bulan Bintang runs its own QR contest with a RM400,000 cash pool. Confirm whether it overlaps.
8. The client's own slides disagree on the launch date (25 or 28 January) and the winner count (50
   or 53).
9. Website readiness. The landing page must absorb a weekly announcement spike, so we need to own or
   co-own the hosting and the load test.
10. COO's working deck proposes four weekly winner draws inside a 10 week contest window, which
    leaves six weeks with no winning moment. The recommendation here is 10 weekly draws.

## 8. Questions for the client

1. Are the three KPIs for paid media alone, or for the whole campaign including KOL and organic?
2. What did the 2026 campaign spend on digital, by platform?
3. Who owns the landing page, the domain, the hosting and the entry database, and who is the PDPA
   data controller?
4. Would Bulan Bintang co-fund a RM600 baju component per winner, on top of the briefed RM145,000?
5. Can the mechanic be locked by mid December so the permit and the page are ready in time?
6. Do we get Meta and TikTok pixel access on Saji properties, plus customer lists for lookalikes?
7. Which Saji SKUs count toward the RM25 threshold?
8. Who verifies receipts and fulfils prizes, and what turnaround do they commit to?
