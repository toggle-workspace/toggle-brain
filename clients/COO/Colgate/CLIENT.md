## The platform

The idea is **Share-Share**, not Kongsi Raya. COO rejected "Gong Xi Raya" as
superficial and culturally risky, then pivoted phonetically from Gong Xi to
Kongsi to Share-Share, which also echoes Xie Xie (谢谢). Kongsi Raya was only
ever the client's own reference term, so do not use it as the platform name.

- Regional platform: Make More Smiles. Malaysian localization: Share-Share.
- Roadmap: 2027 Foundation, 2028 Amplification, 2029 Culmination.
- Values: Senyuman, Kebersamaan, Keikhlasan.
- Brand film: a six-episode web series, "Share-Share Senyuman", three episodes
  for Chinese New Year and three for Ramadan and Raya, shot as one production.
  Talents are Hun Haqeem and Arena Wan, both still placeholder casting.

## Why 2027 to 2029

Chinese New Year and Hari Raya do not fall together in 2027. They land about a
day apart in 2029. What collides in 2027 is Chinese New Year on 6 February and
the first day of Ramadan on 8 February.

| Year | Chinese New Year | Ramadan begins | Hari Raya |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2027 | 6 February | 8 February | 9 March |
| 2028 | 26 January | 28 January | 26 February |
| 2029 | 13 February | 16 January | 14 February |

Aidilfitri dates are projections confirmed by moon sighting, so they may shift
by a day. The three-year shape holds either way.
