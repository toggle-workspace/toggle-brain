## Visual system

Deliverables for this account use the **bru-hwc design system**, COO's house
style, so Toggle work sits beside COO work without looking foreign. The full
spec is in `design-systems/bru-hwc/README.md` and `tokens.css`, both carried
over from `clients/COO/HWC x Media Prima/`.

Everything in that system holds: the three page modes, the two font pairs, the
card grid with colored top rules, the inset footer bar, the panel pair, the
timeline, and the 56px page margins on a 1000 x 563 slide.

### The Colgate accent shift

The bru-hwc palette was sampled from a coffee brand. For Colgate's festive work
the structural colors stay and the accents move toward Chinese New Year and
Raya. Keep the canvas and the purples exactly as documented.

| Token | bru-hwc | Colgate variant | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--cream` | `#FCE5CD` | unchanged | Canvas |
| `--card` | `#FDF2DF` | unchanged | Card fill |
| `--purple` | `#351C75` | unchanged | Statement text |
| `--purple-2` | `#3B2478` | unchanged | Headlines, card body |
| `--purple-muted` | `#746096` | unchanged | Kickers, running headers |
| `--ink` | `#1C120B` | unchanged | Footer bars |
| `--maroon` | `#660000` | unchanged | Text on gold fills |
| `--brick` | `#B5330F` | **`#C1272D`** | Headline accent, card rule 1, alerts |
| `--orange` | `#F6B26B` | **`#E8B04B`** | Panel fills, badges |
| `--orange-rule` | `#F2A25C` | **`#D9A441`** | Card rule 3 |
| `--highlight` | `#EEFF41` | **`#F5C518`** | Footer bar lead-in word |

The red stays deliberately deeper than Colgate's own brand red so Toggle pages
do not compete with the key visuals COO drops in beside them.

**One exception to the system.** Gold `#D9A441` is too light for small text on
the card fill. Where a card rule is gold, its number takes maroon `#660000`
instead, which is the pairing the system already specifies for gold.

## Naming, and the words that matter

- The platform is **Share-Share**. Write it with the hyphen and both words
  capitalized.
- **Never write "Kongsi Raya" or "Gong Xi Raya" as the platform name.** Kongsi
  Raya was the client's own reference term and COO has pivoted away from it.
  The only correct use is naming the thing being rejected, or describing the
  genuine calendar event in 2029.
- Malay phrases the platform owns: Share-Share Rezeki, Share-Share Senyuman,
  Share-Share Kebahagiaan, Share-Share Kegembiraan.
- The web series is **Share-Share Senyuman**.
- Colgate's regional platform is **Make More Smiles**. Share-Share is its
  Malaysian localization, not a replacement.

## Creative guardrails

Carried from the diagnosis. Dual-festival work in Malaysia has a history of
going wrong.

**Do:** treat the two celebrations as neighbours rather than one blended
festival, give the fasting household and the feasting household equal weight,
give the product a real job in both rituals, make halal status visible without
making it the whole message, and write the Malay-market cut in Bahasa Malaysia
first.

**Avoid:** merging religious observance into one generic unity message, any
frame that mixes Ramadan devotion with Chinese New Year feasting, claiming Raya
and Chinese New Year fall together in 2027 (they do not), apologizing for the
brand or referencing the boycott, and positioning against Darlie.

## Audience

- **Primary:** Malay and Bumiputera households, with religious sensitivity
  treated as a hard constraint rather than a tone note.
- **Secondary:** the broad Malaysian audience, reached through shared values
  rather than a blended festival.
