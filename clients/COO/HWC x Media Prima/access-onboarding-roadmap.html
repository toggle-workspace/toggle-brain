A checklist of exactly what we need from your side to build and run the campaign. Nothing here needs passwords or personal logins — every platform below is connected through partner / agency access that you approve and can revoke anytime.

01 — How this works

Partner access, not shared logins.

We connect to your accounts as an approved partner agency. You keep full ownership and admin control; you simply grant our business IDs access to the specific assets we run.

01

We send IDs, you approve

We give you our Meta / Google / TikTok business IDs. You add them as a partner and assign the assets — ad accounts, pages, pixels. No credentials change hands.

02

You stay the owner

All accounts, pages and data remain owned by Media Prima. Our access can be adjusted or removed at any time from your Business Settings.

03

Three platforms first

The priority is the three ad platforms — Meta, Google, TikTok. Tonton data, deep links and the daily sync come alongside. Creative / CI runs through COO.

02 — The three ad platforms · priority

Meta · Google · TikTok.

For each platform we need partner access to the ad account, and where noted, the social pages and tracking pixel. Steps are written for whoever holds admin on your side.

Meta · Business Suite

Ad account · Pages · Pixel

  1. In Business Settings → Partners, choose “Add partner to share assets,” and enter our Business ID.
  2. Assign the Ad Account (advertise + analyse permission).
  3. Assign Pages access — not just the ad account — including the new BRU page (see §03).
  4. Share the Meta Pixel (or create one and grant us access) — required for retargeting & lookalikes.

03 — New brand pages · your side to create

The dedicated BRU accounts.

Part of the strategy is giving BRU its own social presence — separate from the main Tonton feeds — so the IP builds its own fanbase and lives on after the finale. These accounts are created by Media Prima, then shared with us to run.

What to create
  • New Facebook Page for BRU
  • New Instagram account for BRU
  • New TikTok account for BRU
Then
  • Add these pages to your Business Manager / Business Center
  • Grant our partner access to them (as in §02)
  • We handle setup, bios, linking and content from there
Ownership: Media Prima owns these accounts and the IP throughout — we operate them as your partner. Once created, a quick chat on handles / naming and we take it from there.
04 — Tonton

Deep links, audience data & the daily sync.

Three things from Tonton turn the campaign from “reach” into a measurable, optimisable engine.

01

Tonton deep links

A deep link is a URL that opens the Tonton app directly on the BRU episode — instead of a generic web page or the app home screen. We need them so every “Watch on Tonton” click lands viewers straight on the drama, with no extra taps to lose them. Please confirm the links and flag any geo-lock or paywall friction.

02

Customer data & audience lists

Tonton customer / audience lists to build the retargeting and lookalike audiences promised in the deck — for example:

  • Tonton subscribers
  • BRU Season 1 viewers
  • Tonton Instagram page followers
  • Tonton website visitors

Shared securely and used only for this campaign’s targeting.

03

Daily performance sync

A daily feed on how we’re progressing toward our goals — BRU viewers, subscriptions and drink sales — not just raw platform stats. It tells us what’s working so we can shift budget day by day. Without it, we’re optimising blind.

Format: a shared sheet, dashboard export, or API — we just need it daily during the flight so budget reallocation is driven by actual performance.
