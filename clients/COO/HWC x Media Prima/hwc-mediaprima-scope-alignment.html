05 — Attribution

How we track
coffee sales

Where can a sale actually be tracked?

Purchase pathTrackable?
Promo code (online + POS)Yes — if POS accepts codes
HWC app checkoutYes — with pixel / SDK access
TON in-app orderMaybe — needs in-app tracking
In-store cash / cardOnly via code redemption
Grab / deliveryNo — not directly attributable

What we’re proposing

  • Promo codes as the connective tissue — one trackable identifier across online and in-outlet.
  • Deep-links + Smart-TV QR to create a digital → order path we can instrument.
  • A baseline first: original Bru viewership + any prior sales lift, so targets are real, not guessed.
  • Ideally a holdout region to measure true incremental lift, not just last-click.

Without a trackable path confirmed before launch, coffee-sales performance can’t be measured.

07 — Risks & dependencies

Risks & dependencies

Account access

Media Prima controls social posting and won’t make external teams admins. We need a defined partner-access model (Business Manager, ad-account level) + a named MP admin, or we can’t run paid or pull data.

Creative rights

Can we cut episode footage into paid ads? Talent, music and usage rights routinely block this. Confirm clearance before we build around teasers.

Product availability

Are all 125 outlets stocking the 3 flavours from day one, or staggered? Don’t drive demand to outlets that can’t serve it.

Platform policy

Beverage/health claims and the paid path can trigger ad rejections. We build to policy from the start (a live lesson from a current client account ban).

Approvals & sign-off

Who is the single point of approval on creative, budget and go-live? One named approver keeps a tight timeline moving.

Deep-link friction

Is TON geo-locked? Any sign-up or paywall between ad-click and watch? Every step of friction leaks the audience we paid for.
