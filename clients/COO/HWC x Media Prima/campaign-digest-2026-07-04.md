## 0. What this is, in one paragraph

**Brew** is a Media Prima drama IP that streams on **Tonton**. Season 2 is a
**vertical drama** built around a coffee-shop story (three friends, based on true
events), with **HWC Coffee** as the integrated brand partner launching a
**Signature Salted** drink range alongside it. **Toggle** (paid media, strategy,
attribution, reporting) is partnering with **Code of Origin / COO** (CI, brand
playbook, creative) to run the digital campaign. Budget is **confirmed at RM100k**,
100% digital, over a ~7.5-week flight (**Jul 6 – Aug 26**), built to peak on the
**Jul 22 Tonton drop**.

## 4. Strategy — three strategic pillars

> One rule (deck): everything must feel like it moves the **same story** forward — never separate marketing activities.

### Pillar 1 — Conversation (Talkability Strategy)
Make BRU **impossible to ignore online** — a daily topic on Threads & social. Build a
**conversation ecosystem around every episode**: engineer reasons to react, debate, share.

- **Two levers:** Thread Seeding (organic discussion) + Cast & Influencers (big reach).
- **Talkability test:** "if we removed the coffee, what would people still discuss?" Conversation comes **first**; the coffee **amplifies** existing discussion, it never has to start it.
- **Possible topics:** Love Debate ("Sus gila ex-boyfriend Manis, tiba2 muncul"), Self-Claim/Justification ("this is literally me… dulu masa aku jadi barista"), Millennial shared experience, Participation/FOMO ("sedap pulak resipi coffee Manis dengan Mohsin ni"), Terpaling Theory ("scene coffee tu mesti ada maksud").

### Pillar 2 — Ownership (BRU as an IP / Identity)
Today all comms live under **Tonton**, competing with many daily drama posts. A
**dedicated BRU social presence** (IG + TikTok) gives:

- Consistent storytelling · stronger retention/fanbase · creative & engagement freedom · **long-term IP value that outlives the finale**.
- BRU can then own: personality, fanbase, **merchandise**, **playlists (OST)**, **BRU Cafe Story**, future collaborations, a possible real-life BRU coffee shop.
- Flexible trend-jacking (e.g. WC2026 "Norway wins / Haaland" mockup, opinion polls "Oatmilk atau Almond Milk?").
- **IP ownership stays with Media Prima**; agency creates/runs the accounts.

### Pillar 3 — Experience (Ground & Community Activation)
Turn the story into **real-world experiences** so fans **participate, not just consume**.

- **Target communities:** pickle/running, coffee lovers, barista community, university students, young working adults.
- **Offline:** HWC Coffee meet-up · BRU Watch Party (ideally timed to finale) · Mini Acoustic Session · Coffee Workshop · Latte Art Challenge · Character-themed Menu Launch · Creator Meet & Greet.
- **Online participation:** UGC · coffee-review videos · character quizzes · episode discussions · fan edits · community challenges.
- **Audience journey:** *before watching* (curiosity, trailer reactions, character intros) → *while watching* (episode discussions, theories, HWC visit, community events, fan content, merch).

### The coffee is a character, not a placement
The Signature Salted range is **written into the script** so that *not buying the coffee
feels like missing part of the experience* (FOMO). Food collabs land **after** audiences
are attached — so we need the **episode map** from Media Prima: which episodes **introduce
the product**, which carry the **cliffhangers/mentions**.

## 5. Audience

**Klang Valley drama-watchers who love coffee.** Selangor + KL · ages 18–45 ·
mobile-first streamers · **race-agnostic** (Malay or Chinese). The sweet spot is the
**overlap of drama fandom and coffee culture** — the highest-density streaming + café
market in Malaysia.

| Persona | Mindset | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| **The Binge-Watcher** | Drama-first, always streaming; pain = nothing worth committing to | A hook they can't scroll past — "your next binge is 2 minutes away" |
| **The Coffee-Culture Chaser** | Café-hopper, shares everything; pain = FOMO on the next viral cup | A launch everyone's talking about — "the drink everyone's filming" |
| **The Deal Seeker** | Value-driven, offer-responsive; won't pay full price for streaming | A deal too good to skip — "free month of Tonton with every cup" |

*(Open: HWC's actual customer mix by ethnicity/age/geo — needed to size the overlap precisely.)*

## 6. Media plan — 100% digital, three platforms

Every ringgit optimised to cost-per-view and sign-up, weighted to the cheapest,
highest-attention inventory. **Reallocation reviewed weekly — no platform is
guaranteed its split.** Premium takeovers (TopView, Masthead) sit **outside** this tier.

| Platform | Split | Budget | Role | Projection |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **TikTok** | 45% | **RM45K** | Primary reach + views engine; Spark Ads + In-Feed, Top Feed takeover Jul 22 | ~4.5M views @ ~RM0.010 CPV |
| **Meta (FB+IG)** | 30% | **RM30K** | Reach, engagement + retargeting (combo offer) | ~2.2M views + community engagement |
| **Google/YouTube** | 25% | **RM25K** | High-attention reach + demand; bumpers (6s) + skippable (15s) + Demand Gen (YT/Discover/Gmail); **no search** | ~1.0M premium views + 4.5M+ impressions |

### Creative concepts by platform
- **TikTok** (native, fast, unmissable — fuelled by the KOL shoot): ① *POV: You're in the Drama* (Spark Ad, 15–21s) · ② *Salted or Sweet?* (In-Feed, 15s taste-test) · ③ *Top Feed Launch Takeover* (5–9s, release day Jul 22).
- **Meta** (reach → community → retarget): ① *The 2-Minute Cliffhanger* (9:16 Reel, 15s) · ② *The Salted Trio Reveal* (Feed+Reel, 20s) · ③ *Free-Month Retarget* (Stories+Reel, 10s). **Retargeting fires only at viewers who watched ≥50%.**
- **Google** (reach + demand, 100% top-funnel): ① *Launch-Day Blitz* (in-stream burst Jul 22) · ② *Bumper + Skippable* (6s + 15s) · ③ *Demand Gen Campaign* (YT/Discover/Gmail).
