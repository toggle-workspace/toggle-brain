PILLAR 1 · CREATOR STRATEGY

Local Creators Make the Ads. TikTok Guarantees the Quality.

The main blocker you raised on our call was content production: TikTok demands native, fast-turnaround video that a fair organiser can't produce in-house for every market. The TikTok Creator Marketplace (TTCM) handles this. It's TikTok's official platform for commissioning vetted local creators, with deliverable quality backed by TikTok itself.

How it works
  • Brief local creators in each market (e.g. UAE-based creators for the UAE leg) through TTCM
  • Creators produce event-specific videos in their own style, in Arabic, English, or both
  • Videos publish on the creator's page and REXPO's page, then run as Spark Ads with paid spend behind them
Why this beats studio ads
TikTok's auction rewards content that looks native to the feed, and creator videos outperform repurposed brand ads there. Creators bring their local audience as bonus organic reach, and TTCM's vetting locks guaranteed deliverables on a deadline that fits your 45-day runway. Our team supports production in-country where needed.
Content angles that convert
  • "I studied in Malaysia" testimonials, the strongest trust signal for Gulf parents
  • Campus & city lifestyle tours: cost of living, halal food, community
  • Event countdowns: which universities are exhibiting, scholarships on offer, walk-in details
Scale by market: the same TTCM brief re-runs for every market on the calendar. Commission UAE creators for the UAE, Qatari creators for Doha, Kuwaiti creators for Kuwait City. One playbook, localised ads at scale.
The blocker on the call was content. TTCM turns content from a production problem into a procurement step inside the campaign timeline

PILLAR 2 · FUNNEL STRUCTURE

The Funnel You Already Trust on Meta, Rebuilt on TikTok

Nothing here is experimental. TikTok has a direct equivalent for both of your working Meta lead routes, so the switch reuses your existing landing pages, lead handling and follow-up process. Two lanes run in parallel, and you split budget between them on what the results show.

Lane 1 · Instant Forms (TikTok Lead Generation)
  • Native in-app forms, pre-filled from the user's profile: lowest friction, and a mirror of your Meta instant forms
  • Best for volume: event registration, brochure requests, "which universities interest you?" qualifiers
  • Leads sync to your existing CRM and follow-up flow, the same counsellor process you run for Meta leads today
Lane 2 · Landing Page Campaigns
  • Your existing event landing pages, reused, with the TikTok Pixel installed for conversion tracking
  • Best for quality: fuller forms produce higher-intent registrations and richer lead data for LG's agency clients
  • Pixel builds retargeting pools: video viewers and page visitors get event-reminder ads as the date closes in
Full-funnel logic: creator videos build reach and warm audiences (top) → lead-gen forms and landing pages capture registrations (middle) → retargeting converts the undecided in the final two weeks (bottom). Every layer feeds the next, and every lead lands in the same pipeline your team already runs.
Setup cost is one-time: pixel install, form templates and CRM sync are built once, then reused for every event on the calendar

The 45-Day Flight Plan: Produce, Ramp, Convert

Your campaigns already start 45 days out, and this plan slots TikTok into that same window. Creator production happens inside the runway, not before it, so the timeline holds even with the September date still unconfirmed.

Days 45–31 · Produce & set up
  • Commission creators per market via TTCM; briefs from proven Meta angles
  • Pixel installed, lead forms built, CRM sync tested
  • First creator videos delivered & approved; Spark Ad permissions set
Days 30–14 · Ramp & learn
  • Awareness + lead-gen live in all markets; Meta runs in parallel as control
  • Weekly creative rotation: winners get budget, losers get cut
  • Retargeting pools building: viewers, form-openers, page visitors
Days 13–0 · Convert
  • Budget shifts to retargeting + event-countdown creative
  • Final-week reminders geo-tightened to each event city
  • Event day: "doors open" content; post-event follow-up to no-shows
Budget framing: we recommend a 70/30 Meta–TikTok split for this first full run, roughly 9k of a standard 30k one-day event budget, with creator commissioning included. That's big enough to produce a real read on CPL and registration quality, and small enough that the proven Meta engine keeps carrying the event.
Meta stays the workhorse. TikTok gets a full, honest test, with a clean side-by-side comparison at the end of it
