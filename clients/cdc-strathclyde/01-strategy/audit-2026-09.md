# CDC Strathclyde: what is good and what is bad

Strathclyde's Malaysian MBA partner since 1999. Two intakes a year at about 35 students each, so they need roughly 70 enrollments annually from working executives.

## What is good

**They only need small numbers.** Seventy enrollments a year is not a volume problem, it is a precision problem. That is good news, because fixing the things below does not require a big budget.

**They are producing content.** Their LinkedIn showcase page posts weekly. The work is being made, it is just not reaching many people, which is a distribution problem and an easier one to solve than a blank page.

## What is bad

**Signing up for the October intake runs through three different links, none of them theirs.** It goes Linktree, then TinyURL, then a Microsoft form. None of those hops record anything, so they cannot tell how many people started signing up and gave up. Worse, their own website never links to that path at all, so somebody who lands on the site cannot find the way in.

**Their MBA page has no way to say yes.** The only form on that page is the search box. No enquiry form, no brochure to download, and no WhatsApp button even though the page says "Talk to us". For a degree people research for months before committing, there is no way to raise your hand early.

**The homepage takes 10 to 25 seconds to load.** We tested five times and it was slow every time, and it varies a lot, which usually points at the hosting. That gets worse under exactly the traffic that advertising creates.

**They have a Google Ads tag on every page measuring nothing**, and no Facebook, LinkedIn or TikTok tracking at all. We could not find a single Facebook ad from them.

## Careful

The MBA page itself is fine, about two seconds. Only the homepage is slow. Do not call the MBA page slow, because they can open it in front of you. And say "we could not find any Facebook ads" rather than "you have never run any", because we could not run the definitive check.
