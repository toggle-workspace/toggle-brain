## Parent page: VoiceRun Q4 CX Insurance Leader GTM Strategy (30-Day Focus)

### Overview

Use the next 30 days to assemble one workable Insurance and VoiceScore campaign
system. The minimum viable motion is a small approved account list, one clear offer,
one focused destination, a simple outbound sequence, a paid layer supported by an
agency, and a verified path from response to sales follow-up.

### Goals

1. Build awareness of VoiceScore (and VoiceRun in general).
2. Build pipeline for VoiceScore.

### Target audience

- **Priority vertical:** insurance.
- **High-fit accounts:** an enterprise insurer, carrier or MGA with a meaningful
  contact-center operation; a visible CX, contact-center or QA leader accountable for
  service quality; human QA, AI voice agents, or a hybrid operation that needs a
  consistent way to evaluate calls.
- **Account signals:**
  - *Account fit:* substantial call-center activity, a dedicated QA function, AI or
    voice transformation work, and an identifiable CX or contact-center owner.
  - *Buying intent:* active AI-agent rollout; inability to trust or prove agent
    performance; pressure to reduce QA cost or tool sprawl; disagreement between QA
    scores and supervisor judgment; event engagement; or interest in a tailored
    assessment.
- **Buying committee:**
  - Champion: CX or contact-center leader.
  - Operational evaluator: QA director and call-center operations.
  - Technical evaluator: CIO, IT or AI leadership when integration or the wider voice
    platform is involved.
  - Economic influence: finance or an executive sponsor when the case depends on
    savings, risk or broader transformation.
- **Core messaging territories:** proof and control of AI-agent performance; more
  efficient, trusted QA.

### GTM architecture (MVP), as drawn

Contact acquisition (contacts acquired through a third-party tool, based on the target
account list and ICP criteria) feeds the CRM database (target accounts and contacts
list). The CRM feeds three channels:

- **Email:** focused outbound efforts.
- **LinkedIn:** build awareness and engagement across target accounts and roles.
- **Google Search:** capture active demand around contact-center QA, call scoring and
  AI performance monitoring.

All three point to a **new Insurance and VoiceScore landing page** (one verticalized
story connecting buyer pain, product demonstration, proof, and enterprise trust), then
**sales follow-up** (respond to qualified hand-raisers), then **demo**, then
**opportunity**.

### Nick's parameters and lead universe list

Platform: LinkedIn Sales Navigator people search.

Geographic coverage: the United States, searched nationally and then state by state to
work around Sales Navigator's result cap (the captured state searches cover 40 states),
plus South America as a regional filter.

- **US contact center QA search.** Current job title: `(quality OR QA) AND ("contact
  center" OR "call center") AND (manager OR director OR head OR lead)`. Keywords:
  `"contact center" OR "call center"`. Geography: United States.
- **South America contact center QA search.** Current job title: `(quality OR QA OR
  calidad OR qualidade) AND (manager OR director OR head OR gerente OR diretor OR jefe
  OR coordenador)`. Keywords: `"contact center" OR "call center"`. Geography: South
  America.
- **Insurance expansion search.** Account criteria: insurance carriers, administrators,
  brokers, agencies, and insurance-serving BPOs; US headquarters or substantial US
  operations; at least 201 total employees; evidence of a QA problem was helpful but not
  required.
- **Additional source:** saved attendees from the ITC Vegas 2026 directory, plus
  previously collected company-fit research and enrichment.

**Qualification and data handling:** each lead should include name, current title,
company, location, and LinkedIn profile URL. Records are deduplicated first by LinkedIn
profile ID, then by normalized name plus company. Manager-level and above contact-center
operations or QA leaders count as potential sellable leads. Junior, technical-only,
commercial, recruiting, ambiguous and incomplete profiles are routed to review or
excluded. Inclusion indicates title-based fit only: current employment, account fit,
contact information, suppression status and prior outreach still need verification
before activation.

**ICP (as written):** an organization with a meaningful contact-center or
customer-service operation in the United States or South America. The target buyer is a
manager, director, head, or executive responsible for contact-center operations, quality
assurance, service performance, or customer experience. The strongest accounts have
enough agents, interactions, operational complexity, or regulatory exposure to make
manual QA, coaching, consistency, and performance measurement difficult to manage. The
current universe is broad across industries, with a specific emphasis on insurance
carriers, administrators, brokers, agencies, and BPOs. Priority goes to leaders with
clear ownership of contact-center performance and a likely need to scale QA, improve
service quality, modernize operations, or evaluate the performance of human and AI
agents.

## Subpage: Campaign Landing Page (1)

**Readiness evaluation:** strong product foundation, missing verticalized conversion
layer. VoiceScore already has a credible product story, a demo experience, enterprise
assurances, and a useful low-friction CTA. The primary gap is the absence of a dedicated
Insurance and VoiceScore journey connecting the target buyer, industry pain points, and
campaign messaging to the existing product.

### Website audit

| Asset | Status | Insurance-specific? |
|---|---|---|
| voicerun.com homepage | Live, current nav | No, general enterprise positioning |
| voicerun.com/voicescore/ | Live, current nav | No, generic "call center leaders" audience |
| voicerun.com/pricing/ | Live, current nav | No, platform-wide usage-based tiers |
| voicerun.com/research/ (Perspectives) | Live, current nav | No, banking is the only industry piece |
| voicerun.com/industries/insurance/ | Live URL, orphaned from current nav and branding | Yes, but off-message (agent deployment, not QA) |

VoiceScore's current messaging targets three pain points: AI grading that never
improves, QA corrections vanishing into a spreadsheet with no feedback loop, and alert
fatigue from noisy tools. The solution narrative is a self-tuning judge that works from
the client's own scorecard, cites evidence per criterion, and gets sharper from QA
overrides, with human and AI agents graded on one standard.

Existing proof points: "Up to 70% of QA costs saved" (a homepage stat with no case study
behind it); TCPA compliance already appears as a scored and alerted category in the
interactive demo; demo transcripts lean insurance-flavored ("cancel my policy") without
being labeled as such. No named customer logos, case studies, or quantified proof points
specific to VoiceScore exist anywhere on the site.

**Legacy insurance page:** voicerun.com/industries/insurance/ still resolves but sits
under an old nav and branding structure, unlinked from the current site. Its audience
(claims managers, customer service directors, operations leaders, IT directors) and use
cases (claims intake, policy changes, coverage inquiries) are about deploying voice
agents in general, not QA and not CX or QA leadership, and it is not connected to
VoiceScore's current narrative. It does not count as an available insurance asset for
this motion.

## Subpage: Paid Campaigns (1)

**Readiness evaluation:** existing infrastructure, missing motion-specific architecture.
Google Ads and LinkedIn are already active, but the earlier audit showed the main issue
was not channel performance alone. Intent, audience, message, offer, and destination
were not consistently aligned. The Insurance and VoiceScore motion should therefore enter
Q4 as a focused campaign layer rather than as a light edit to the current campaigns.

### Paid ads audit (Insurance and VoiceScore lens)

| Campaign or ad set | Platform | Relevant to this motion? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| VoiceScore (Search) | Google Ads | Yes | About 29% of impressions are real competitor or category searches (NICE CXone, Klaus, Scorebuddy, Gong, Calabrio), already-qualified QA-buyer traffic. The Automated Call Scoring ad group has a keyword contamination problem: "automated QA scoring" is matching software test-automation searches (LambdaTest, QA Wolf, Mabl), consuming 54% of that ad group's spend |
| Enterprise Executives (Search) | Google Ads | No | Best CTR in the account (10.5%), but targets enterprise buyers broadly rather than CX or QA; its homepage landing issue is a separate fix outside this motion |
| Bundled Tool (Search) | Google Ads | No | Paused competitor-conquesting campaign, already resolved |
| Code-First Builder (Search) | Google Ads | No | Developer and self-serve motion, explicitly out of scope |
| Enterprise, Ops-NA | LinkedIn | Yes | Targets contact center directors, VP CX, VP Ops and COOs at 201+ employee companies, close to the workshop's champion and operational-evaluator roles. CTR is on forecast, but the media buy runs 1.5 to 2 times above LinkedIn's own CPM and CPC forecast |
| Graduation, TechBuyer-NA | LinkedIn | No | Targets CTOs and engineers, a different (developer) funnel; loses 75% to 80% of viewers before the 25% watched mark |

Lawrence's read: VoiceScore's Google campaign is a reusable signal rather than something
to build from scratch, since it already pulls qualified QA-buyer traffic, and fixing the
Automated Call Scoring contamination is a lower-lift fix than a new campaign build.
LinkedIn's Ops-NA ad set is a different case: once the new insurance TAL exists, the
campaign should be rebuilt to target that list directly rather than patched for
efficiency, since its current targeting is not insurance-specific. The caveat is list
size: if the TAL turns out too small to sustain a dedicated campaign, the fallback is to
build off the existing Ops-NA campaign rather than a full rebuild. Enterprise Executives,
Bundled Tool, Code-First Builder and TechBuyer-NA serve different buyers or are already
resolved, and none belong in this motion's scope.

The VoiceScore (Search) and Enterprise Executives (Search) campaigns did not appear in
the 15 July to 27 August 2026 exports Toggle audited (see
`2026-08-27-google-ads-audit-verified-data.md`).
