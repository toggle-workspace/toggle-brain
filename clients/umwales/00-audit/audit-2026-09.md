# Universiti Malaya-Wales

## Identity, confirm this in the first minute

Viknesh's message said "University of Wales Malaya." No institution by that exact name exists. The prospect is almost certainly **Universiti Malaya-Wales** at `umwales.edu.my`, a private university in Kuala Lumpur, formerly the International University of Malaya-Wales and renamed on 7 March 2024. Confidence is roughly 85%, so treat it as provisional and confirm the website before using any of this.

Ruled out: the University of Wales Trinity Saint David, which is the awarding partner in the United Kingdom rather than a Malaysian buyer, and the University of Malaya, which is a separate public university. The University of Malaya is also the joint venture partner in the name, so the two will get confused on the call. Be precise about which one you mean.

## The three findings

**1. They built a reporting system and not a conversion signal.**

Their tag container `GTM-P5WBMR2` was fetched and read directly. It holds 31 GA4 event tags including `submit_enquiry_foundation`, `submit_enquiry_mba` and `submit_scholarship`. Those events exist and they fire. They go to Google Analytics and they stop there.

The container contains zero occurrences of `fbq`, `fbevents` and `connect.facebook.net`, so there is no Meta pixel. There is no `AW-` Google Ads conversion tag. There is no TikTok pixel. Ten live Meta ads are running in Malaysian ringgit against a website that cannot report a conversion back to Meta.

Open by crediting the work, because somebody did it properly and that person may be in the room: "Somebody built your measurement carefully. Thirty one GA4 events, including submit_enquiry_foundation. They fire today, they go to Analytics, and they stop there. There is no Meta pixel anywhere, so Meta is being asked to find applicants with a signal nobody handed it."

The safe phrasing on Conversions API, which cannot be checked externally: "Meta cannot see your website conversions from the browser."

**2. Seven of ten live ads lead with a discount.**

The live set includes 50% off, a 100% foundation scholarship offered twice, a 30% waiver plus PTPTN, and a free iPad or hostel year. There is no brand, outcome or employability creative anywhere in the live set.

Two foundation scholarship ads were created six seconds apart on 25 August 2026, carrying the same offer to the same intent, which means they are bidding against each other. All ten ads are English only, while a competitor buying the same category runs Chinese language creative and IMU runs program level rather than price led creative.

Discount led advertising with no upper funnel teaches the delivery system to buy the audience that is already deciding, which is the most expensive audience in the auction.

**3. Every paid click lands on a page that takes about nine seconds.**

`/foundationsem/` is the page matching the live fast track and scholarship ads. Five spaced tests on 31 August 2026 returned totals of 8.9s, 9.6s, 9.6s, 9.4s and 8.8s, with first byte between 7.2s and 8.3s, on 716KB of HTML. The homepage tested at a median of 8.2s total on 714KB.

Those figures are HTML alone. They do not include 87 images, reCAPTCHA, or Elementor injecting the enquiry form, which is not present in the initial HTML at all. A student on mobile data waits noticeably longer than nine seconds before a form appears.

This finding was re-tested in round two and came back slightly worse than round one reported, so it is the most solid claim in this file.

## The funnel gap

The loop is broken end to end. Discount led ads with no upper funnel teach the algorithm to buy the most expensive audience, the missing pixel means that audience is never fed back, and the landing page loses people before the form renders. There is also no WhatsApp path anywhere on the site, even though their own container already defines `click_wa`, `click_whatsapp_us` and `click_whatsapp_2025`. The tracking for a channel they do not offer is already built.

This is UNITAR's problem set in the same vertical and the same country, which makes the transfer line straightforward and the conflict disclosure necessary.

## What is not verified

- **Meta ad body copy and destinations.** Three ad snapshot URLs returned 403 to automation. A human with a browser closes this in ten minutes and it would sharpen finding two considerably.
- **Whether they run Google or TikTok ads at all.** Both transparency tools defeated automated access. What is verified is the missing conversion tag, not the absence of campaigns. Do not conflate the two.
- **Social follower counts**, which came from search snippets rather than the platforms.
