# Universiti Malaya-Wales: what is good and what is bad

A private KL university, formerly IUMW, running nine live ads.

## What is good

**Their doctorate ad is the best single ad in this whole group of five prospects.** It names real prices, RM38,388 for Malaysians and RM46,228.72 for international students, states you need a master's degree to apply, and sells a specific picture, which is graduating at Dewan Tunku Canselor. Somebody there knows how to write an ad.

**Most of their ads are not just discounts.** Five of the nine sell something real: a one year fast track, campus access, a dual heritage degree. Worth saying out loud, because it is better than it first looks.

**Somebody built their web analytics properly.** They track 31 different actions on the website, including separate events for foundation, MBA and scholarship enquiries.

## What is bad

**All that careful measurement goes to one place and stops.** The 31 events feed Google Analytics, which is a reporting tool, and nothing is sent to Facebook. There is no Meta pixel anywhere on the site, no Google Ads conversion tag and no TikTok pixel. So the platform spending their money is told nothing about who actually enquired. It is like keeping perfect sales records in a notebook and never showing them to the person you hired to find customers.

**Every ad we opened dumps people into Messenger instead of the website.** Three of nine were checked and all three did this. That means an enquiry becomes a chat message that a human has to notice, answer and follow up, and nothing about that is measured. Nobody can tell which of the nine ads produced a student.

**Two of their ads are word for word identical.** Same nine bullet points, different headline, started one day apart, both live. That is a headline test running as two separate ads competing against each other rather than one ad with two versions.

**The website takes about nine seconds to show a form.** This hurts people who find them on their own rather than through the ads, since the ads do not go there.

## Ask them

Your ads open a Messenger chat. Who answers those, how fast, and where does the conversation go afterward? Also, there is a page called My University Guide running ads in Indian rupees with headlines that look like yours. Is that an agent you appointed?
