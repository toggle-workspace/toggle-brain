## Roscoe webinar campaign (4 Nov, 4pm SGT)
- Campaign 120256353573130569, built by Benedict on 16 Oct for the 15 Nov webinar.
- It launched with a S$7 cost cap on both ad sets (S$15 a day each, Website plus Instant Form, pixel Lead). It spent S$34.86 from 16 to 18 Oct for 1 lead, then cents, and has had zero impressions since 23 Oct.
- Jordan removed the cap at 13:40 SGT on 3 Nov. Benedict moved it to a S$350 a day campaign budget at 00:14 on 4 Nov, likely meaning S$35; that would break the S$2,000 plan if it spent.
- After 26 hours without the cap it still had zero impressions while the eBook campaign delivered, with no issues_info, active ads and audiences of 2.3M to 5.8M.
- **Diagnosis:** the cost cap starved delivery and the ad sets now carry a dead delivery history.
- **Fix proposed:** fresh duplicate ad sets, no cap, Instant Form only, a sane budget.

By 6pm on 4 Nov Sedifly had replaced it with a new Roscoe campaign (120256629675680569, S$150 a day, ages 18 to 65, Advantage+ on). All Readiness Check campaigns were paused, and the eBook campaign was paused that day.
