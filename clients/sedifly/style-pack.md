# Sedifly: Style Pack

> Base: `prompts/style-packs/toggle-default.md`. This file overrides only what differs.
> Read from sedifly.com and Sedifly's own ads in November 2026, not from a brand guide.

## Voice overrides
- Tone: warm and specific, built on student stories (Ke Yue, Joash, Jeremy). Sedifly is a Singapore company writing to Singapore parents and students, so use plain English with S$ figures.
- Cold copy speaks to parents, or to applicants aged 18 to 24. Meta cannot target under-18s.
- Keep the January deadline framing (US Regular Decision, UCAS) until those dates pass.
- Price line: the site publishes no prices. Use "about 20% of traditional consultant fees", which is on the site.
- The Readiness Check is free: the Speedy Prep page calls it "complimentary".

## Claims to check before any copy ships
- Offers and Ivy/Oxbridge share: the homepage says 250+ offers and 40%+ Ivy/Oxbridge (4 Nov 2026), but the Speedy Prep page still says 150+ and 30%+. Use one pair and confirm it with Sedifly.
- Ke Yue's SAT: 1490 in the ads, 1390 on an inner case study page (possibly a first sitting). Ask which score to use; do not call it a typo.
- The Ke Yue carousel's last card reads "Once Cycle Later".
- Sedifly's 28 Oct copy already used "Joash, polytechnic to Columbia", so Jeremy's ITE angle is not new to the account.
