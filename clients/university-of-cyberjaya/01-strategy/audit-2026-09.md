# University of Cyberjaya: what is good and what is bad

A private health sciences university running 51 live ads.

## What is good

**They make the best creative of the five prospects.** Ten faculties each get their own ads rather than one generic university ad. The work was refreshed through August, and they ran a strong campus tour campaign on TikTok in April. Nobody else in this group is producing at that level.

## What is bad

**Every enquiry gets filed under the same label, whatever its source.** Their web form sends leads into Salesforce, their customer database, with the campaign field locked to a single option labeled "CUCMS-FB-General English". So 51 ads across 25 angles, plus Google, plus TikTok, all arrive stamped as the same Facebook campaign. It is like a shop with five doors that writes "walked in" for every customer, then wonders which door to keep open.

**Their pixels are split so neither one can learn.** A pixel is code that reports what visitors did. They have three. The one that sees all the traffic records no enquiries. The one that records enquiries only loads on the thank you page, so it sees almost no traffic. Neither has both halves, so Facebook cannot work out which ads produce students. Their Google tag has the same problem, firing only on a page where the tracking code from the click is already gone.

**You cannot enquire from the page the ads sell.** The pharmacy, psychology and open day pages have no enquiry form at all. So somebody who clicks "Start Your Career in Pharmacy" lands somewhere with no way to raise their hand, and has to find another page and pick pharmacy again from a menu of 49 options.

**Fourteen of fifty ads send people into chat**, where nothing is measured at all.

## Ask them

Which of your 51 ads brought in this month's enquiries? They will not be able to answer, and that is the whole conversation.
