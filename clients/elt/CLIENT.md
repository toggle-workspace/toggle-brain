## Brand state, confirmed on the founder call (3 Sep 2026)
- Launched January 2026. Roughly 8 months trading. Sold out twice, about 2.5 months of lost
  trading in total. Restocked 3 September 2026.
- **30% reorder rate, organic, with no retention strategy running.** 10 subscribers against a
  65% subscription target.
- Shopee ROAS 6x. Meta ROAS peaked at 3x, fell to 1.75x before the stockout.
- Average cart RM120. Box price RM64.80 for 12 sachets.
- Channels: Shopee and own Shopify. No Lazada, no TikTok Shop, no Google Ads.
- Founder is an ex-chef. 8 months of R&D. Taste is the stated differentiator, alongside
  all-natural ingredients and sodium calibrated for everyday Malaysian use.
- Audience: recreational padel, golf, soccer, squash, tennis and pickleball players, plus
  parents of young athletes. **Explicitly not HYROX or serious runners.** Approachable, not elitist.
- Top ad angles: cramping as a mineral problem; "the heat killed us in Malaysia"; best-tasting
  for people who hate the alternatives.
- Direct competitors named: Ten Plus (Indonesian, poor taste reputation), Above Hydration
  (targets runners), Supernatural+ (Hong Kong, aggressive spend), Electrify (weak on performance
  marketing). Lit Hydration unfamiliar to Yuli.
- Six-month target: RM500 a day Meta spend, ROAS 4x to 5x, cost per purchase under RM50.
- Geography: Malaysia first, Philippines and Singapore later.
- Founder cited IM8 and Supernatural+ as reference brands.
- Formula: 600mg sodium, 200mg potassium, 60mg magnesium, 0g sugar, 29 percent real citrus
  powder, stevia sweetened, halal certified ingredients.
- Instagram @drinkelt at 1,356 followers. Seven blog posts 6 May to 11 June 2026, then stopped.
