# ELT pitch deck, skeleton and research base

The slide by slide skeleton lives in the artifact linked above (22 main slides, five acts,
four appendix slides, plus a cut list and an unverified data register). This file holds the
research base behind it so the numbers survive independently of the deck.

## The thesis the deck argues

ELT already owns the asset that cannot be bought, a 4.9 rating and a batch that sold out.
It does not own a demand engine. The next million ringgit comes from converting a padel court
reputation into a daily habit, in that order, and that sequence is a creative problem.

## The three findings that shape the pitch

1. **ELT is far earlier than the brands the founder cited.** 1,356 Instagram followers and
   roughly 917 boxes of lifetime volume against IM8's 22,104 reviews and Supernatural+'s six
   product lines. The pitch is a 0 to 1 scaling pitch, not an enterprise pitch, and pricing
   has to reflect that.
2. **They are sold out.** Any pitch that creates demand without naming the supply constraint
   is dishonest, and saying it first is the highest trust move available in the room.
3. **Their content and their positioning point at different audiences.** The site sells a
   daily foundation for the Malaysian climate. The Instagram grid sells padel. Both convert,
   but the creative has to pick one to lead with, and that decision is the engagement.

## Market evidence, by source

**Category (global)**
- Global electrolyte market around USD 38 billion, growing 6 to 8 percent a year (The Food Stack)
- US powder sachets a USD 1.5 billion category, volume up 15 percent in 2024 while ready to
  drink stayed flat (CNN Business, May 2025)
- Zero sugar is 63 percent of top selling electrolyte brands
- LMNT at USD 72 million in 2025 sales, 95 percent from the US (ECDB). Other trade sources
  say above USD 200 million, so cite the range
- Unilever acquired Liquid IV for USD 200 to 300 million in 2020

**Malaysia, demand drivers**
- March 2026 heat alerts across Peninsular Malaysia, stage one at 35 to 37 degrees, three
  Kedah districts at stage two above 37 degrees (Malaysian Meteorological Department)
- May 2026 red level warnings above 41 degrees, humidity adjusted heat index frequently
  above 45 degrees (New Straits Times, 2026)
- Obesity 22 percent, diabetes 21 percent, highest in ASEAN, up from around 5 percent in 1990
- NHMS 2024: half of adults exceed 37.5g free sugars a day, half of that from beverages
- Per capita sugar 57kg in 2023, highest in ASEAN, 27 percent above Thailand
- The SSB tax has not cut consumption (ISIS Malaysia, April 2026)

**Malaysia, the incumbent**
- 100Plus holds close to 90 percent of the isotonic market, one in four carbonated drinks consumed
- 100Plus Zero at roughly 80mg sodium and 20mg potassium per 325ml can, around RM2.50
- ELT delivers roughly seven times the total electrolyte load at 2.2 times the price

**Malaysia, the beachhead (bottom up)**
- 143,000 active pickleball players, Malaysia leading Asia
- 397 pickleball and padel venues nationwide
- Roughly 425,000 gym members, derived from 12.5 memberships per 1,000 people against a
  population near 34 million (Singapore 22, Thailand 18)
- Anytime Fitness passed 100 clubs in Malaysia in early 2026
- SCORE Marathon 2026 at 51,077 runners, up from 33,000, 21km sold out in 24 hours
- 61 percent of adults met WHO activity guidelines in 2023, up from 54 percent in 2020
- Deduplicated addressable range: roughly 450,000 to 600,000 actives

**Channels**
- Malaysian TikTok Shop GMV past USD 5 billion; beauty, health and personal care leads at
  roughly 14 percent of Malaysian TikTok Shop GMV (Statista)
- Malaysian e-commerce growing around 48 percent year over year
- Shopee at roughly 53 percent of platform GMV, TikTok Shop second (Cube Asia, Digital in Asia, 2026)

**Import price gap (ELT's own published research)**
- LMNT at RM11 to RM13 a sachet in Malaysia, roughly RM330 to RM390 a month
- ELT at RM5.40 a sachet, RM162 a month, roughly RM2,200 annual saving
- Malaysian import listings carry a 60 to 90 percent margin for the import work
- LMNT has no official Malaysian distribution and no halal certification

## Unit economics model (the centre of the deck)

RM5.40 a sachet at daily use is RM162 a month and RM1,944 a year per retained customer.

| Scenario | Retained daily customers | Penetration of 450,000 actives | Annual revenue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | 675 | 0.15 percent | roughly RM1.3M |
| Base | 1,350 | 0.30 percent | roughly RM2.6M |
| Aggressive | 2,250 | 0.50 percent | roughly RM4.4M |

Assumes daily consumption and a fully retained 12 month cohort, so each figure is the ceiling
of its band. Re forecast on real cohort data after 90 days.

## Reference brands the founder named

**IM8** (im8health.com): co-founded with David Beckham. Equity holding ambassadors including
Giannis Antetokounmpo, Aryna Sabalenka, Lily Collins, Ollie Bearman, Jay Shetty. Advisory board
from Mayo Clinic, NASA, Cedars-Sinai and Yale. A 12 week randomized controlled trial. NSF
Certified for Sport. 4.8 stars across 22,104 reviews. USD 78 to 183 a month, subscription only.
The playbook is manufactured authority at scale, sold as a daily ritual.

**Supernatural+** (supernaturalplus.com): launched Hong Kong November 2023 by ex-banker Josh
Anstey and F&B operator Jared Lawler. SUPERsalty at 1,000mg sodium. Signed local athlete roster.
Six product lines from electrolytes to creatine electrolytes, soda, flask, bottle and nose strips.
Subscribe and save 20 percent. Own site plus HKTVmall. The playbook is a regional performance
brand built on athletes and range.

ELT already holds Supernatural+'s athlete asset and none of its range or subscription. It holds
none of IM8's manufactured authority, and at RM5.40 a day it does not need to.
