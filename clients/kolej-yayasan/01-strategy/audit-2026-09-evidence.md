# Kolej Yayasan

## Identity is unresolved, and that is deliberate

Two candidates are close to equally likely, so both were audited rather than guessing one.

| Candidate | Confidence | What it is | Who it sells to |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kolej Yayasan Saad Melaka (KYSM) | ~45% | Fully boarding secondary school in Ayer Keroh, Form 1 to Form 5 | Parents |
| Kolej Yayasan UEM (KYUEM) | ~40% | Cambridge A Level only college in Selangor | Post SPM school leavers and their sponsors |
| Something else | ~15% | | |

Nothing in this repository names the prospect. `Sales/sales-pipeline.md` and `cockpit/current.md` were both searched and returned no hits.

Ruled lower: KYS Business School, which runs no ads. Kolej Yayasan Pelajaran Johor, which became a university college in October 2025 and runs no ads of its own. Kolej Yayasan Pahang, which has no findable footprint.

**Ask which institution in the first sentence.** The buyer is a parent in one case and a school leaver in the other, and almost every recommendation changes with the answer. One useful coincidence: these are the only two Kolej Yayasan institutions running live Meta ads in Malaysia, and both started on 26 August 2026.

Since there are nine days until this meeting, the identity should be confirmed by message well before then.

## The three findings, which hold for either candidate

**1. The ads run into a website that cannot see them.**

KYSM's main site carries no Meta pixel, no Google Analytics and no tag manager on the homepage, the contact page or the admissions page. The only pixel, `653351485260002`, and the only Google tag, the conversion tag `AW-940108404`, sit on a separate subdomain at `admission.kysm.edu.my`. That pixel fires PageView only, with no lead event.

KYUEM is further behind: zero tags of any kind anywhere, with a live ad running.

**2. The ads do not name what is being sold.**

All three live ads return an empty link title or the page's own name. KYUEM's reads `"Kolej Yayasan UEM - KYUEM |   |  "`.

In the same auction that week, Epsom College ran "World-Class British Boarding School in Asia" and "Weekday Boarding Programme", and Methodist College Kuala Lumpur ran "Cambridge A Level at MCKL" and "Study AUSMAT at MCKL". The competitors name the offer and these two do not.

State the observation, which is the blank headline, rather than the inference that these are boosted posts. The inference may be right and it reads as a status insult, which costs more than it gains.

**3. Affiliates own their highest intent query.**

Searching "yuran Kolej Yayasan Saad 2026" returns sistembayar.my, biayaberapaan.my, biayaitu.my, hargaterkini.my and infokolej.com at the top. Zero results are kysm.edu.my. The school's own answer to its own fee question is a PDF.

TikTok discover pages exist for "Yuran", "Cara Masuk", "Dorms" and "Keburukan Kolej Yayasan Saad". The demand is in Malay, it is specific, and everybody except the school is answering it.

Sayable version: "Search yuran Kolej Yayasan Saad 2026 on your phone right now. Five content farms, and not one result from your own domain. Your own answer is a PDF."

## The funnel gap

There is no lightweight way to raise a hand anywhere. KYSM's main site has no form at all and its contact page renders empty. The only path is registering a full application account on another subdomain that asks for a parent's salary statements or a court verified affidavit. KYUEM's path is an email address or a downloadable `Application-Form.pdf`, and its WhatsApp button is commented out in the source with the placeholder number 60000000, so it never worked.

Paid traffic arrives at a wall, and nothing behind that wall is measurable.

## Held in reserve

KYSM's homepage carries a live hero slide titled "Home Test", a leftover `http://unicamp.local/` theme demo link, and an admissions page dated 3 September 2024 still advertising "2025 Intakes". First byte times ranged from 0.85s to 7.22s.

These are credibility details rather than opening findings. Use them if the room disputes that anyone is minding the site, not as the first thing out of your mouth.

## What is not verified

- **Ad body copy and language.** All three snapshot URLs returned 403 to automation. Open them manually.
- **Google Ads.** The Transparency Center is JavaScript only and the endpoint returned HTTP 400.
- **TikTok follower counts and any TikTok ad history.** The pages are JavaScript shells and Malaysian TikTok ads are not publicly archived. Say so on the call.
- **Real mobile speed**, which needs a phone on mobile data.
- **The actual fee figures**, which came from aggregator sites. Do not quote a number until the school's own PDF has been opened.

## How to run the room

If the prospect is KYSM, this is a boarding secondary school and it is not higher education. Keep UNITAR entirely out of the room and lead with Kith and Kin plus Al Hidayah, which together cover a parent buyer in a Malay language market. There is no conflict of interest to disclose.

If the prospect is KYUEM, it competes with UNITAR, so disclose the relationship in the first five minutes and lead with UNITAR on method. Keep the RM11m figure out.
