# Kolej Yayasan: what is good and what is bad

**Confirm which school first.** Two candidates are equally likely: Kolej Yayasan Saad Melaka, a boarding secondary school that sells to parents, and Kolej Yayasan UEM, an A Level college that sells to school leavers. Both started running ads on 26 August 2026. Almost everything below applies to either, but who you are talking to changes the advice, so ask before the meeting.

## What is good

**They are advertising, which means somebody has decided this matters.** There is a budget and a starting point, which is more than nothing.

**KY Saad has some tracking in place**, a Facebook pixel and a Google tag, on the separate site where applications happen. A pixel is code that reports what visitors did.

## What is bad

**The tracking is on the wrong site.** The main website, the one the ads point people toward, has no tracking of any kind on the homepage, the contact page or the admissions page. And the tracking that does exist only records that somebody visited, never that they enquired. KY UEM has no tracking anywhere at all while running a live ad.

**Their ads do not say what they are selling.** The headlines come back blank or just show the school's own name. In the same week, Epsom College ran "World-Class British Boarding School in Asia" and Methodist College KL ran "Cambridge A Level at MCKL". Their competitors name the offer and they do not.

**Other websites own their most important search.** Search "yuran Kolej Yayasan Saad 2026", which is parents looking up the fees, and the top five results are all content farms. Not one is the school's own website. The school's own answer to its own fee question is a PDF nobody can find. Parents are asking, and strangers are answering for them.

**There is no easy way to enquire.** KY Saad's contact page loads completely empty, and the only real path is registering a full application account that asks for a parent's salary statements. KY UEM offers an email address or a PDF to download, and its WhatsApp button is broken in the code with a placeholder number, so it has never worked.

## Ask them

Search your own fees on your phone right now and tell me which result is yours.
