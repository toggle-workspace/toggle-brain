## What this version is

Brand Core sits between Essentials and the full programme. It restores the four things that make a rebrand hold up under real use — **proper discovery, a choice of design directions, a brand guide teams can actually operate from, and tender-grade collateral** — while still deferring the full playbook, the complete tender kit, and the launch/measurement phase.

**Every deliverable is a building block of the full programme.** Upgrade within 6 months and we scope only the delta — KMU never pays twice.

### Phase 1 · Brand Strategy (condensed) — Weeks 1–4 · RM7,000
- Kickoff workshop + **3 stakeholder interviews** (leadership, tender/BD, PT KMU representative)
- Audit of current materials + **benchmark snapshot** (Gamuda-tier references, key regional peers)
- **Positioning, brand persona, messaging core** (brand story, message pillars, boilerplate), **2 tagline directions**
- Gate: strategy sign-off before design

### Phase 2 · Visual Identity & Brand Guide — Weeks 4–8 · RM12,000
- Logo refinement — **2 design directions, 2 revision rounds** (refine, not replace), colour system, typography, core graphic language
- Applications: letterhead & stationery, email signature system, **PowerPoint/tender deck template**, social profile kit
- **Brand Guide (~30 pages)** — usage rules for internal teams, printers, and future vendors
- Master asset pack with all source files
- Gate: identity sign-off before production

### Phase 3 · Photography & Image Library — Weeks 7–10 · RM9,000
- Pre-production: shot list mapped to profile + deck needs, site recce, safety/access coordination
- **2 shoot days** — 1 full day on an active site + 1 day split between a second site and corporate (leadership portraits, office)
- **40–50 retouched, rights-cleared images**, tagged as a reusable library
- Gate: library accepted before profile layout

### Phase 4 · Corporate Profile & Capability Statement — Weeks 10–13 · RM7,000
- **Corporate profile booklet, 20–24 pages** — print-ready + interactive digital PDF, English master
- **Capability statement (4 pages)** — the fast-scan version for tender annexes and first meetings
- Print management support (print costs pass-through at cost)
