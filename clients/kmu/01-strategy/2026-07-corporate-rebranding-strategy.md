## Page 1 — Where KMU stands, and why now

### What we heard

KMU has spent 25+ years becoming the largest player in its field in Malaysia — and has done it almost entirely on the strength of its work and its relationships. That was the right priority. But three things have now converged:

1. **The materials no longer match the company.** The website and company profile booklets are outdated. When KMU walks into a government or corporate tender, the documents in the room understate the business behind them.
2. **The brand has recognition, but not identity.** People in the industry know KMU. What's missing is the *"when I see this, I think of KMU"* effect — a distinct visual and verbal identity that separates KMU from every other contractor in the stack of tender submissions.
3. **The story deserves a bigger audience.** KMU's work — much of it underground and invisible — delivers clean water and critical infrastructure to society. Almost nobody outside the industry understands this. That's not just a branding gap; it's an unclaimed leadership position.

### What this is (and isn't)

This is **not** a from-scratch rebrand that throws away 25 years of equity. It is a **brand alignment and elevation programme**: one identity, applied consistently across every touchpoint — from the tender document to the email signature to the site hoarding — in Malaysia and cascading down to PT KMU in Indonesia.

The benchmark KMU named is Gamuda. That's the right reference: not because KMU should look like Gamuda, but because Gamuda proves that a construction group with a disciplined brand gets treated differently in the room — by clients, by government, by talent.

### Why phased

A single RM80K commitment is a board conversation. Five sequenced phases — each with its own deliverables, its own sign-off, and its own invoice — is a series of operational decisions. Each phase gates the next: KMU approves the strategy before we design, approves the identity before we produce, and can pause between phases without losing anything already delivered. **No single invoice in this programme exceeds RM11,000.**

## Page 3 — Phase detail (Phases 1–3)

### Phase 1 · Brand Discovery & Strategy — Weeks 1–4 · RM14,000

The thinking that everything else stands on. We go in, look at the internal and external data, and repaint the persona before a single pixel is designed.

**Activities**
- Kickoff workshop + stakeholder interviews (up to 5: leadership, tender/BD team, PT KMU representative)
- Audit of all current brand materials — website, profile booklets, decks, signage, templates
- Benchmark scan: Gamuda-tier corporate brands + regional infrastructure peers (MY + ID)
- Brand architecture: how KMU Malaysia and PT KMU Indonesia relate visually and verbally

**Deliverables**
- **Brand Strategy Blueprint** — positioning, brand persona, audience map (government evaluators, corporate clients, industry, public, talent)
- **Messaging house** — brand story, 3 message pillars, elevator pitch, boilerplate copy, 3 tagline directions
- Sign-off gate: strategy approved before Phase 2 begins

### Phase 2 · Visual Identity & Brand Playbook — Weeks 5–9 · RM22,000

The identity itself, plus the operating manual that keeps every department — and any future agency — on the same page. This is the single deliverable Dewei asked about most directly.

**Activities**
- Visual identity refresh: logo refinement (preserving 25 years of recognition — refine, not replace), colour system, typography, graphic language (2 design directions, 2 revision rounds)
- Application design: letterhead + stationery, email signature system, PowerPoint/tender deck template, site hoarding & signage direction, vehicle/PPE livery direction, social media profile kit

**Deliverables**
- **KMU Brand Playbook** (~40–60 pages) — logo usage, colour, type, tone of voice, templates, do's & don'ts; written so internal teams and external vendors can reproduce the brand without us
- Master asset pack (all logos, templates, source files)
- Sign-off gate: identity approved before production phases

### Phase 3 · Photography & Image Library — Weeks 8–11 · RM16,000

Underground work is genuinely hard to shoot — which is exactly why professionally-shot underground work is a differentiator nobody else in the field has. Our production house turns "not so nice" into national-infrastructure-grade imagery.

**Activities**
- Pre-production: shot list mapped to profile/website needs, site recce, safety & access coordination with KMU site teams
- **2 shoot days** on active sites (underground works, machinery, scale shots, teams in action) + **1 day** corporate — leadership portraits, office, culture

**Deliverables**
- **60–80 retouched, rights-cleared images**, tagged and organised as a reusable brand library (tenders, website, social, PR)
- Sign-off gate: image library accepted before Phase 4 layout begins

## Page 4 — Phase detail (Phases 4–5) + timeline

### Phase 4 · Corporate Profile & Tender Kit — Weeks 11–15 · RM18,000

The commercial payoff: the documents KMU actually takes into tenders, rebuilt on the new identity and the new imagery.

**Deliverables**
- **Corporate profile booklet** (24–32 pages) — print-ready + interactive digital PDF, English master (BM/Bahasa Indonesia adaptations available as add-on)
- **Capability statement** (4 pages) — the fast-scan version for tender annexes and first meetings
- **Project case-study sheets** (up to 6) — one per flagship project, modular for tender assembly
- Tender document cover system (covers, dividers, spine)
- Print management support (print costs billed pass-through at cost)

### Phase 5 · Rollout, Internal Launch & Measurement — Weeks 15–18 · RM10,000

A rebrand that stays in a PDF changes nothing. This phase pushes the brand into daily use and then proves what it did — the answer to "what did the rebranding actually do for us?"

**Deliverables**
- **Internal launch kit** — all-staff announcement materials, brand briefing workshop (1 session), email signature deployment pack
- **External launch assets** — LinkedIn/website announcement set, press-release draft
- **Brand perception measurement** — internal + external baseline survey and share-of-voice snapshot at launch, plus a **6-month re-measure** so leadership sees before/after in numbers
- 90-day light-touch brand QA on the first assets KMU teams produce themselves

- **Each phase is approved independently.** Sign off Phase 1 for RM14K; everything after is KMU's call, made with the previous phase's deliverables already in hand.
