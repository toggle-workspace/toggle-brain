What this programme is

KMU has spent 25+ years becoming the largest player in its field in Malaysia — but the materials in the room no longer match the company behind them. The website and profile booklets are outdated, and the brand has recognition without a distinct identity.

Brand Essentials fixes the three most visible gaps first, at a quarter of a full rebranding programme's investment:

1 · A refreshed, unified identity

Logo refinement (refine, not replace — 25 years of recognition is an asset), colour, typography, and a lite guidelines document so every department reproduces the brand correctly.

2 · Professional site photography

Underground work is hard to shoot — which is exactly why professionally-shot underground work is a differentiator nobody else in the field has. One focused shoot day, done right.

3 · A tender-ready corporate profile

A 12–16 page profile booklet built on the new identity and new imagery — the document KMU actually takes into government and corporate tenders.

Every deliverable is a building block of the full rebranding programme. Nothing here is throwaway — commission the full programme within 6 months and we scope only the delta. KMU never pays twice for the same work.

Phase Detail

Three phases, each gated by your sign-off

Phase 1 · Brand FoundationsWeeks 1–5

Strategy-lite plus the identity itself — enough thinking to design correctly, without the full discovery programme.

  • Kickoff workshop + 2 stakeholder interviews (leadership, tender/BD team)
  • Rapid audit of current materials + Gamuda-reference positioning review
  • Positioning statement, brand persona, boilerplate copy, 1 tagline direction
  • Logo refinement — 1 design direction, 2 revision rounds · colour system · typography
  • Core applications: letterhead, email signature system, PowerPoint cover/master slide

You receive: Brand Guidelines Lite (~15–20 pages) + master asset pack with all source files. GATE: identity sign-off before production

Phase 2 · Photography EssentialsWeeks 4–7

One focused shoot, planned tightly against the profile's exact image needs.

  • Pre-production: shot list mapped to the profile, 1 site recce, safety & access coordination with KMU site teams
  • 1 shoot day on an active site + half-day corporate — leadership portraits, office

You receive: 25–30 retouched, rights-cleared images organised as a starter brand library. GATE: image selection accepted before layout

Phase 3 · Corporate ProfileWeeks 6–10

The commercial payoff — the document that goes into the next tender batch.

  • Corporate profile booklet, 12–16 pages — print-ready + digital PDF, English master
  • Built on the Phase 1 identity and Phase 2 imagery
  • Print management support (print costs at cost, pass-through)

Choosing the Right Fit

Essentials vs the full rebranding programme

Both options are on the table. Essentials moves fast on the most visible gaps; the full programme adds the strategic depth, organisation-wide rollout, and measurement that make a rebrand stick for the next 25 years.

The honest recommendation

If the immediate need is walking into the next tender with materials that match the company, Essentials covers it. If the goal is the one Dewei described — "when I see something, I think of this company", aligned across every department and down to PT KMU in Indonesia, with numbers to show leadership what changed — that is what the full programme is built for. Starting with Essentials and upgrading later is a completely valid path: within 6 months we scope only the delta, so nothing is paid twice.

Optional add-ons to Essentials (quoted on request)

  • Extra site shoot day — best value if added while the crew is mobilised, retouching included
  • Capability statement (4pp fast-scan version for tender annexes)
  • Corporate website revamp · brand video · educational content retainer (~2 posts/week)
  • BM / Bahasa Indonesia adaptations of the profile and guidelines
