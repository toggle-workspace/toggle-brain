What this programme is

Three stakeholder interviews (including a PT KMU voice), a benchmark snapshot against Gamuda-tier references, and a positioning + messaging core — so the identity is built on evidence, not taste.

Phase Detail

Four phases, each gated by your sign-off

Phase 1 · Brand Strategy (condensed)Weeks 1–4
  • Kickoff workshop + 3 stakeholder interviews (leadership, tender/BD, PT KMU representative)
  • Audit of current materials + benchmark snapshot — Gamuda-tier references, key regional peers
  • Positioning, brand persona, messaging core (brand story, message pillars, boilerplate) + 2 tagline directions

GATE: strategy sign-off before design

Phase 2 · Visual Identity & Brand GuideWeeks 4–8
  • Logo refinement — 2 design directions, 2 revision rounds · colour system · typography · core graphic language
  • Applications: letterhead & stationery, email signature system, PowerPoint/tender deck template, social profile kit
  • Brand Guide (~30 pages) — usage rules for internal teams, printers, and future vendors + master asset pack

GATE: identity sign-off before production

Phase 3 · Photography & Image LibraryWeeks 7–10
  • Pre-production: shot list mapped to profile + deck needs, site recce, safety & access coordination
  • 2 shoot days — 1 full day on an active site + 1 day split between a second site and corporate (leadership portraits, office)
  • 40–50 retouched, rights-cleared images, tagged as a reusable brand library

GATE: library accepted before profile layout

Phase 4 · Corporate Profile & Capability StatementWeeks 10–13
  • Corporate profile booklet, 20–24 pages — print-ready + interactive digital PDF, English master
  • Capability statement (4 pages) — the fast-scan version for tender annexes and first meetings
  • Print management support (print costs at cost, pass-through)
