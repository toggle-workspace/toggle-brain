## What this version is

The Brand Essentials programme delivers the three things KMU explicitly asked for — **a refreshed identity, professional site photography, and a new corporate profile** — at a quarter of the full programme's investment. It trades depth for speed and affordability: lighter discovery, one design direction instead of two, condensed guidelines instead of a full playbook, one shoot day instead of three, and the profile alone rather than the full tender kit.

**Every deliverable is a building block of the full programme.** Nothing here is throwaway — if KMU later commissions the full rebrand, this work carries forward.

### Phase 1 · Brand Foundations — Weeks 1–5 · RM9,000
- Kickoff workshop + **2 stakeholder interviews** (leadership + tender/BD)
- Rapid audit of current materials + Gamuda-reference positioning review
- **Positioning statement, brand persona, boilerplate copy, 1 tagline direction**
- **Logo refinement — 1 design direction, 2 revision rounds** (refine, not replace), colour system, typography
- Core applications: letterhead, email signature, PowerPoint cover/master slide
- **Brand Guidelines Lite (~15–20 pages)** — enough for internal teams and printers to reproduce the brand correctly
- Gate: identity sign-off before production phases

### Phase 2 · Photography Essentials — Weeks 4–7 · RM6,000
- Pre-production: shot list mapped to the profile, 1 site recce, safety/access coordination
- **1 shoot day on an active site + half-day corporate** (leadership portraits, office)
- **25–30 retouched, rights-cleared images**, organised as a starter library
- Gate: image selection accepted before profile layout

### Phase 3 · Corporate Profile — Weeks 6–10 · RM5,000
- **Corporate profile booklet, 12–16 pages** — print-ready + digital PDF, English master
- Built on the Phase 1 identity and Phase 2 imagery
- Print management support (print costs pass-through at cost)
