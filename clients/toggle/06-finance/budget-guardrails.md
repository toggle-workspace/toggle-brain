# Toggle Solutions — Budget Guardrails

**Purpose.** A single page of rules the founders agree to operate within, so spend stays disciplined without needing a meeting for every decision. The numbers here are *guardrails* (ceilings and triggers), not budgets to spend to. The live numbers live in the tracker — this doc tells you the lines you don't cross.

> **The one rule above all others:** never let closing cash drop below **RM150,000** (the minimum cash floor already hard-coded in the model). Every other guardrail exists to protect this floor.

**Two baselines — operate against the *current* one, plan toward the *target*.**

| Metric | **Current (Q2–Q3 2026)** | Plan / target (end-state) |
|---|---|---|
| MRR / total revenue | **RM80–90K / mo** (modelled at RM85K) | RM144,000 / mo |
| Full-time founders | **1 from Oct '26** (phased in) | 3 |
| Office rental | **RM0 until Oct '26**, then RM3,150 | RM3,150 |
| BD director | **deferred** until MRR ≥ RM110K | RM7,000 base + commission |
| Total OPEX (burn) | **~RM44K / mo** (Oct: 1 FT founder + rental) | RM66,105 / mo |
| EBITDA margin | **~48%** @ RM85K | 54.1% |
| Opening cash (Jun '26) | RM284,034 | — |
| Min cash floor | RM150,000 (hard rule) | RM150,000 |

> The **% -of-revenue ceilings are the durable rule.** RM caps in Part A are shown at the **current RM85K MRR** — re-derive them whenever MRR moves materially (at the RM144K target the OPEX cap becomes ~RM72K). Cash is *not* a constraint today: generating RM34–44K surplus/month on a RM284K base means the RM150K floor is never in play. **The thing to manage right now is margin, not runway.**

## Part A — Spend guardrails by cost pillar

Each pillar has a **% -of-revenue ceiling** and a **RM cap** at current MRR. The pillar ceilings are *individual maxima* — they are **not** all meant to be spent at once. The binding overall constraint is the master guardrail below.

> **Master guardrail — EBITDA margin: target ≥ 50%, hard floor ≥ 45%.** Equivalently, total OPEX ≤ 50% of revenue (≤ 55% at the floor). At RM85K MRR that's an OPEX **target ≤ RM42.5K** and a **floor ≤ RM46.75K**. October's ~RM44K burn sits in the 45–50% *watch band* at the low end of MRR, and clears the 50% target once MRR holds **≥ RM88K**. If one pillar pushes toward its ceiling, another must give.

| # | Pillar | What's in it | Now /mo (Oct) | % rev @85K | **Ceiling (% rev)** | **Cap @ RM85K** | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **People & Founders** | Director fees, allowances, staff salary, EPF/SOCSO (BD excluded — deferred) | ~RM36,000 | 42% | **≤ 45%** (salary-only sub-cap ≤ 42%) | RM38,250 | Board |
| 2 | **Freelancers / Delivery (COGS)** | Project freelancers, pass-through delivery labour | ~RM3,000 | 3.5% | **≤ 6%** | RM5,100 | Delivery lead |
| 3 | **Office & Facilities** | Rent (from Oct), utilities, office ops | RM3,150 | 3.7% | **≤ 3%** target / **RM3,500** absolute | RM3,500 | Ops lead |
| 4 | **Software & Tools (IT)** | SaaS, hosting, devices (amortised) | RM600 | 0.7% | **≤ 1.5%** | RM1,275 | Ops lead |
| 5 | **Growth & Marketing** | Own ad spend, brand, BD tooling (BD *commission* is self-funding, see note) | RM0 | 0% | **≤ 3%** | RM2,550 | Growth lead |
| 6 | **Reserves & Tax** | Tax provision, cash floor, distribution pool | ring-fenced | — | see Part C | — | Finance lead |

**Notes per pillar**

- **1 · People & Founders.** Founders go full-time **one at a time, MRR-gated** (see the readiness ladder in Part C) — *not* all three at once. Founder #1 goes full-time in Oct '26 at a **RM12,000** director fee + RM650 allowance; the rest continue current part-time draws until their step is unlocked. Full-time director fees are **fixed by board resolution** — no change without a documented board decision. Keep the transport portion of allowances ≤ RM500/founder to stay tax-exempt. The **salary-only ratio (draws + staff) must stay ≤ 42%** of revenue — the early-warning line before the 45% loaded ceiling.
- **2 · Freelancers / Delivery.** The *variable* pillar — should scale **with** project revenue, not ahead of it. Rule: freelancer spend on an engagement must keep that engagement's **contribution margin ≥ 60%**. Don't grow the standing bench past the 6% ceiling without converting demand into a hire decision (which is itself gated).
- **3 · Office & Facilities.** Rental was **deferred to Oct '26** (no rental cost before then — no pressure). At RM3,150 it is **~3.7% of RM85K MRR — slightly over the 3% ceiling until MRR clears ~RM105K.** Accepted as a fixed strategic cost; the **RM3,500 absolute cap** covers it. A larger lease is a board decision. Revisit the % when MRR steps up.
- **4 · Software & Tools.** Any **new recurring** subscription > **RM200/mo** needs the Ops lead's sign-off and goes on the SaaS register. Audit the stack quarterly — kill what isn't used. One-off devices > RM3,000 follow the approval tiers below.
- **5 · Growth & Marketing.** Currently RM0. This pillar gives founders **up to 3% of revenue (RM2,550/mo @ RM85K)** of discretionary growth budget *if* the margin band and cash floor hold. **BD commissions are excluded from this cap** — paid only on collected new revenue (5–9% of first invoice + 2% residual), they are self-funding and never threaten the floor.
- **6 · Reserves & Tax.** Governed by triggers, not a spend cap — see Part C. At ~RM41K EBITDA the tax provision is ~RM6.7K/mo (SME tiered); ring-fence it monthly.

## Part B — Margin view by practice (revenue lines)

Beyond *how much we spend*, founders should see *where margin comes from*. Toggle's three delivery practices (per `brain/process.md` and each `CLIENT.md` `practice:` field):

| Practice | What it sells | Target revenue mix* | Contribution-margin floor |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Acquisition** | Performance marketing, SEO, content, creative | ~55% | ≥ 55% |
| **Conversion** | CRO, web dev, analytics/tracking infra | ~25% | ≥ 55% |
| **Retention** | Lifecycle/email, consumer retention, BI, reporting | ~20% | ≥ 50% |

\* *Targets are placeholders for the founders to set against the actual book.* The model today carries ~100% gross margin because no delivery cost is allocated to revenue lines. **Action:** once Pillar 2 (freelancers/delivery) is tagged per engagement, allocate it to a practice so we can read true contribution margin per practice. Until then, the floor rule is enforced **per engagement** (Pillar 2 note above).

**Concentration guardrail (read this one carefully).** Audaura (UNITAR) is roughly **RM35–45K of monthly revenue** — at the current RM80–90K MRR that is **~45–50% of the book**, far over a safe limit and a single point of failure for the whole P&L.
- **Rule:** no single client should exceed **35% of MRR**.
- **While over the line (now):** keep the effective cash floor at RM200,000 (not 150k), hold distributions conservative, and make new-client BD the **top growth priority** — landing 1–2 mid-size clients both fixes concentration *and* unlocks the next founder full-time (Part C).

## Part C — Triggers, gates & rituals

### Founder full-time readiness ladder

Founders join full-time when **MRR sustains the threshold for 3 months** — calendar-gated only for #1 (Oct '26). Each step assumes a RM12K fee + RM650 allowance, net ~RM6.5K step-up over the prior part-time draw.

| Step | Cumulative burn | MRR for 45% floor | MRR for 50% target | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **#1 full-time + rental** (Oct '26) | ~RM44K | ≥ RM80K | ≥ RM88K | **here ✅** (RM85K) |
| **+ Founder #2 full-time** | ~RM50.5K | ≥ RM92K | ≥ RM101K | locked |
| **+ Founder #3 full-time** | ~RM57K | ≥ RM104K | ≥ RM114K | locked |
| **+ BD director** (RM7K base) | ~RM65K | ≥ RM118K | ≥ RM130K | deferred → unlock at MRR ≥ RM110K |

> The end-state (3 FT founders + BD + rental ≈ RM65–66K burn) only holds the 50% target at **MRR ≥ RM130K** — i.e. close to the original RM144K plan. Climb the ladder in order; don't stack two steps in one quarter.

### Cash circuit-breakers (escalating)

| Trigger | State | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Cash < **RM200,000** (1.3× floor) | **Amber** | Freeze discretionary spend (Pillars 4–5). Pause new distributions. Review pipeline. |
| Cash < **RM150,000** (the floor) | **Red** | Hard freeze. No distributions. Board reviews founder fees + variable costs immediately. |
| MRR drops **> 10% MoM** | **Amber** | Freeze the ladder + discretionary spend until recovered or replaced. |
| EBITDA margin < **45%** for 2 consecutive months | **Amber** | Mandatory cost review across all pillars. |
| Single client > **35% of MRR** | **Amber** | Cash floor lifts to RM200k; BD prioritises diversification. *(Active now.)* |

### Distribution rule

- Distribute **only from surplus above the RM150,000 floor**, capped at the **20% quarterly distribution pool** (per shareholder model). Salaried founders' payouts are offset by their fees (auto-calculated in the Equity Distribution sheet). Aizad: director fee only, no equity split. **No distribution in any Amber/Red state** — and concentration is Amber today.

### Hiring gate (any new headcount, incl. founders going full-time & the BD director)

Proceed **only** when **all three** hold:
1. Forward 3-month MRR covers the new fully-loaded cost while keeping **salary ≤ 42%** of revenue *and* EBITDA ≥ 45% (use the ladder thresholds);
2. Cash floor is maintained with **≥ 6 months runway** on the new burn level;
3. The role is **revenue-linked** (BD/delivery capacity at a real bottleneck) or removes a documented founder bottleneck.

*BD director is pre-approved in principle but **deferred until MRR ≥ RM110K**; commissions are self-funding per the structure in the tracker.*

### Spend-approval tiers

| Amount (one-off or new recurring) | Who approves |
|---|---|
| ≤ RM500 | Any full-time founder — no approval needed |
| RM500 – RM2,000 | The pillar owner |
| RM2,000 – RM10,000 | Two full-time founders |
| > RM10,000, any new lease, or any new headcount | Board (all full-time founders), documented |

### Rituals

- **Monthly:** update MRR (row 7) and actuals in the P&L tracker; check each pillar's actual vs ceiling; check cash + margin vs triggers; check ladder thresholds.
- **Quarterly:** review this doc with the distribution review — re-baseline RM caps if MRR has moved materially, confirm pillar owners, recompute concentration.
- **On every Amber/Red:** log the trigger and the action taken in `cockpit/decisions/`.
