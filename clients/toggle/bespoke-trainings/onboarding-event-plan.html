01 The concept

What we are building

One full day, a room of the right people, and three parties that each win something different from it.

What

🎤 A full-day event and workshop

Registration through networking dinner. Talks and breakout sessions on the latest studies, AI for brands, and TikTok Shop onboarding, with lunch and networking built in. People can leave at 4pm to beat the jam, or stay and lepak until 8–9pm.

Why us

📈 Branding and a warm pipeline

Toggle gets front-of-room positioning as the growth partner these brands should hire: one senior team running acquisition, conversion, and retention, judged on profit rather than vanity metrics. Plus a room of qualified SME owners we can convert into retainers, the real prize on our side.

Who

🏪 SME and D2C brand owners

Growing D2C brands and SMEs where the owner makes the call. They move fast, the pool to fill the room is large, and TikTok Shop is a natural next step for them.

02 The deal and the economics

What each party gets

The incentives differ across the three parties, and that is fine. Aligning on what TikTok wants back is decision #7, and the most important one to settle.

🎵 TikTok gives

USD $400 in cash, direct, for every client we sign and onboard. They have committed a 2-month window. In return they want signed sellers on TikTok Shop, so we must confirm exactly what counts as a qualifying signed client.

We bring the content, speakers, AI expertise, and real e-commerce proof, Al Hidayah at 11.5x ROI on TikTok Shop and EduKids at 6x ROAS on TikTok. We get brand positioning, a thought-leadership stage, and first right to convert every attendee into a Toggle retainer.

03 Target audience and sourcing

Who we put in the room, and where we find them

Pick the SME lane and let the rest follow it. Enterprise brands are hard to convert here because they already have an agency, so SME-focused invites and SME positioning should match.

Address the invite to
  • Founder or business owner, especially SMEs, where they make the call
  • Head of Marketing, Marketing Manager, or Brand Manager
  • E-commerce or Online Sales Manager
  • Social Media or Digital Marketing Lead
Where to source them
  • Existing Shopee or Lazada sellers, already selling online, so TikTok Shop is the natural next step
  • Brands active on Instagram but not yet on TikTok
  • Our current clients and referral network, for warm intros
  • CM's client base, which CM is already pulling prospects from
Demographic to design the content around (2026)

Core buyers on the platform are 18–34, and the 25–34 band is now the largest group. That is who the attendees are trying to reach, so our talks and case studies should speak to selling to that buyer.

Why this lane fits Toggle: our e-commerce book, Al Hidayah, Kualesa, and EduKids, is exactly these Shopee, Lazada, and TikTok Shop sellers, so the case studies land with this room. CM's own note ties the SME lane to a pricing-floor question, so keep the SME invite list and SME pricing aligned.

09 The 2-month sprint

Working backwards from event day

A skeleton to pressure-test at the weekend meeting. We pin the dates once the event date and TikTok window are set.

This weekend

Lock the concept

Meet, walk the 7 decisions, get TikTok terms in writing, agree the budget and split, and set the event date.

Week 1–2

Foundations

Book the venue, confirm the budget, lock speakers (Faris, TikTok, Dynamo), and finalize the agenda and the four session topics.

Week 3–4

Build and open invites

Design the eDM and event branding off the Toggle design system, open registration, and start sourcing from the CM base, Toggle clients, and Shopee and Lazada seller lists.

Week 5–6

Fill the room and produce

Drive signups to target, build the slide decks and session content, and confirm F&B, door gifts, games, and run-of-show.

Week 7

Final week

Reminders and reconfirmations, print and logistics, dry-run the sessions, brief the speakers, and prep the onboarding and signing flow so the $400 conversions are captured cleanly on the day.

Event day

Run it and sign clients

Deliver the day, sign SMEs onto TikTok Shop and Toggle, capture testimonials, then follow up the whole room within 48 hours while it is warm.
