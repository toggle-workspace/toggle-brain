01 How this workshop is designed

Three rules the whole day obeys

CM's client base told us plainly that attendees care about the topics and the speakers, not the pitch. So the day earns the right to sell by being useful first.

Rule 1

🎁 Teach first, sell second

Every session delivers a real, do-it-Monday takeaway before it mentions Toggle or TikTok. Value builds the trust that makes the ask land.

Rule 2

🌉 Every session has a bridge

Each talk ends by naming the heavy part of doing it alone, then points to the help in the room, whether that is TikTok's onboarding or a Toggle retainer. It offers a natural next step instead of a hard pitch.

Rule 3

🎯 Two clear wins, one for each partner

The day drives two actions side by side: get set up on TikTok Shop, which pays us USD $400, and book a Toggle growth call, which opens the retainer that is our real prize. We optimize for both, not just the signing.

📐
Working assumptions, confirm at the weekend meeting

This run-book assumes roughly headcount TBD attendees and a planned onboarding-staff ratio of about 1 staff : X attendees. Nearly every sizing decision below (worksheet counts, desk staffing, wifi load, room layout, the "staff at your elbow" promise in Session 3) depends on these two numbers, so lock them first.

03 The sessions, built
Session1
9:45 to 10:30 AM · 45 min Keynote TikTok + Toggle

Session 1: Where Your Buyers Went

The opening keynote. Show an SME owner that the market has moved, the window is open, and they are early enough to win.

They walk away able to

Explain, in one sentence, why TikTok and social commerce is a priority for their brand this year, not next.

Narrative arc

  1. The shift. Attention moved to short-form video, and a growing share of product discovery now starts in-feed. insert verified stat
  2. Who is buying. Frame the core buyer age band and the biggest-spending cohort, then what they expect: authenticity, creators, entertainment-led selling. insert verified figures
  3. Commerce follows attention. TikTok Shop, live selling and shoppable content are where that attention converts. insert MY or SEA GMV figure
  4. The window. There is still room for new SME sellers to gain ground, and Shopee and Lazada sellers already have a head start to extend. frame with a dated source
  5. What it means for you. Three concrete implications an owner can act on.

Content to build

  • One headline-stat opener slide
  • Buyer persona slide for the biggest-spending cohort
  • Channel-shift chart, attention then commerce
  • "The window is open" slide
  • "3 implications for you" recap
✎ Interaction

Close with a 60-second beat: each attendee writes their own one-sentence "why now", then says it to the person next to them. The outcome gets produced in the room, not just claimed from the stage.

Bridge →

"The opportunity is real. Doing it well is the heavy part, and that is exactly what today, and Toggle, are here for."

◆ Facilitator note

This sets the energy for the whole day. Land both feelings at once: the fear of missing out, and the relief that it is doable. Hold every number to a verified, dated source, since this deck sets the credibility for everything after it. No product pitch yet.

Materials: deck, verified stat sources, clicker, one strong local example.

Session2
10:45 to 11:30 AM · 45 min Keynote + live demo Toggle · Zaid

Session 2: Do the Work of a 10-Person Team

Toggle's differentiation moment. Show a small team how AI lets them produce more and better content, ads and ops without hiring.

They walk away able to

Run at least three concrete AI plays next week, with the exact prompts and tools to start.

Narrative arc

  1. The SME squeeze. Small team, endless content demand. AI closes the gap.
  2. Play 1, content at scale. Hooks, scripts, variations, and repurposing one idea into a week of posts.
  3. Play 2, creative and visuals. Product shots, ad variations, and localization across Malay and English.
  4. Play 3, insight and ops. Mining comments and reviews, customer replies, faster reporting.
  5. Guardrails. Brand voice, fact-checking, keeping a human in the loop.

Content to build

  • Three "play" slides, each with a real before and after
  • One live demo that builds a TikTok script or product image on stage
  • Guardrails slide
  • "Your Monday list" takeaway slide
✎ Interaction

Have the room try one prompt on their phone during the demo. A small win on their own screen lands better than another slide.

Bridge →

"Keep the easy part you just learned. Hand the heavy, weekly grind to Toggle, where acquisition, conversion, and retention run on one senior team. It is the same stack that took EduKids to 6x ROAS on TikTok."

◆ Facilitator note

Keep it practical and show the work rather than talk about it. This is where Toggle earns credibility as the growth partner, so the demo has to succeed live. Rehearse it, and keep a recorded version ready as a fallback.

Materials: live tool logged in and tested, backup recorded demo, prompt handout.

Breakout3
1:00 to 2:00 PM · ~60 min Hands-on breakout TikTok + Toggle

Session 3: Get On the Shelf

The engine room of the day. Attendees submit their TikTok Shop setup with staff at their elbow, rather than sitting through a talk about it. This is where signings happen.

They walk away with

A submitted TikTok Shop seller account and a first product drafted. Verification is reviewed by TikTok off-site and usually completes within timeframe TBD, so the realistic on-the-day win is "submitted", not "live and selling".

Two tracks, run in parallel

  1. New seller, set up. Create the seller account and start verification. Bring business registration, bank details, and product info.
  2. New seller, list. Draft the first product with images, price, and shipping.
  3. Pay and get paid. Payment and payout setup. Airwallex station if confirmed for cross-border payouts.
  4. Already selling? A separate optimization station for existing TikTok Shop, Shopee, or Lazada sellers: sharpen listings, set up the affiliate program, and plan a first live. These sellers are often the warmest retainer prospects, so this track matters.
  5. First content, optional. Anyone ready records one simple product video. Live-selling mechanics are taught in depth in Session 4, so keep this light here.
✎ The exercise is the session

Each attendee works a printed checklist while TikTok and Toggle staff circulate to unblock them. Two outcomes count, and they are different things.

The learning win: an attendee who completes their own setup succeeds, full stop. The commercial signing: the assisted onboarding logged at the desk is what qualifies for the USD $400. Leave anyone who is happy self-serving to finish on their own, since pushing them toward the desk would break Rule 1.

Content to build

  • Onboarding checklist worksheet, one per track
  • "What to bring" pre-event reminder so people arrive ready
  • Station signage and a staff run-sheet
$ Conversion moment →

"Finish your setup with us at the onboarding desk, where TikTok's onboarding support and Toggle's done-for-you kick in." Log every assisted signing to the right source for the USD $400. The exact qualifying definition comes from TikTok first, see plan decision #7.

◆ Facilitator note

Staff-heavy by design, so confirm the staff-to-attendee ratio up front. Set expectations early that verification finishes off-site, so nobody feels short-changed leaving without a live shop. Have a wifi fallback ready, mobile hotspots, since a full room uploading documents and video at once will strain a single connection.

Materials: checklists, charged laptops and phones, strong wifi plus hotspot backup, onboarding staff at the planned ratio, signing capture sheet or QR.

Breakout4
2:15 to 3:15 PM · ~60 min Hands-on breakout Toggle + CM · guest: Faris

Session 4: Sell More, the Seller Playbook

A shop with no traffic sells nothing. This breakout hands over the content and promo engine that turns the shop into sales, and covers the eShop question Lynn raised.

They walk away with

A 30-day content and promo plan skeleton for their own brand.

Narrative arc

  1. Content that converts. The hook, retain, and call-to-action structure. UGC versus polished, and how often to post.
  2. The creator and affiliate engine. How TikTok's affiliate program and a tiered GMV Max strategy multiply reach, and how to find creators.
  3. Promotions and live selling. Vouchers, a live-selling rhythm, and the mega-sale dates worth planning around.
  4. eShop integration. Connecting Shopee, Lazada, or an own-store, and omnichannel basics. covers Lynn's ask
  5. Measure and iterate. The few numbers that matter.
✎ Decompress, then build

Open with a 5-minute share: each table swaps one win or snag from the Session 3 setup grind. It resets energy after the post-lunch push before the worksheet starts.

Then attendees fill a 30-day plan skeleton and share one idea with their table, so they leave with a plan, not just notes.

Content to build

  • 30-day plan worksheet
  • Content-structure one-pager
  • Mega-sale calendar for the coming months verify against current-year dates
Bridge →

"Keep running the simple parts you just planned. Hand the weekly content, ads, and live-selling engine to Toggle. The tiered GMV Max and affiliate-creator playbook we just walked through is what drove Al Hidayah to 11.5x ROI on TikTok Shop."

◆ Facilitator note

CM co-runs this given their client-base fit, and Faris joins as a guest voice once his topic is confirmed. Keep it tactical and specific to SME retail. The worksheet is the takeaway, so protect time for people to fill it.

Materials: worksheets, pens, table facilitators, one worked example plan.

Session5
3:15 to 4:00 PM · 45 min Testimonial · fireside Dynamo + Toggle client

Session 5: Proof It Works

Social proof placed where it counts, right before the networking and onboarding push. Real brands share the real numbers behind their results. This session has to show that brands win specifically with Toggle, over and above the channel itself.

They walk away believing

"A brand like mine did this with Toggle and it worked, so I can too."

Format

  • Short fireside or two tight talks, kept human and honest
  • Dynamo (FMCG), brought in by CM, shares their journey and what changed
  • A Toggle case carries the numbers: Al Hidayah reached 11.5x ROI and +300% revenue on TikTok Shop in 4 months, and EduKids hit 6x ROAS on TikTok. Confirm which we can name on stage, otherwise present the result anonymized
  • Host closes by pointing the room to the onboarding desk and the Toggle booking link
$ Conversion moment →

This is the warmest point of the day. Close with two low-friction next steps: "Set up before you leave, the desk is open through dinner, and book your free Toggle growth call at the same time."

◆ Facilitator note

Coach speakers to tell the messy middle, not a highlight reel. Lock which case we feature, Al Hidayah or EduKids, and confirm naming permission before the day, since a vague result on stage means the section quietly sells TikTok for us instead of Toggle.

Materials: confirmed speakers, agreed talking points, cleared metrics, two stools and mics.

The desk

🪑 Sign and book together

Staffed all day, busiest right after Session 3 and through the networking dinner. Every visit does two things: complete or log a TikTok Shop signing, and book a Toggle growth call using a live calendar link while intent is highest.

The offer stack

🎁 One clear, AI-backed offer

TikTok onboarding support, Toggle's TikTok Starter retainer (8 reels a month, plus TikTok Shop and performance management), and any Airwallex perk, in one sheet so the choice is easy. Pricing sits with Viknesh and Zaid, finalize with plan decision #4

The capture

📋 Capture everyone, not just signers

A QR or sheet logs every attendee into the CRM, tagged by source and readiness. Signers feed the USD $400 count. The not-ready segment enters a nurture track so warm leads never leak.

For the not-ready

🌱 Nurture track

Many SMEs will not have their registration, bank, or product info ready on the day, so they cannot sign yet. A defined email sequence keeps them warm and gives them a reason to come back to Toggle, rather than losing them at the door.

Protect the payout

⏱️ Activation sequence

The USD $400 depends on a qualifying signing inside TikTok's 2-month window. A timed post-event sequence nudges first listing, first live, and first sale, so signups activate before the deadline instead of stalling.

The exact definition of a qualifying "signed client" and how the USD $400 is paid must come from TikTok first, see plan decision #7. Gate the desk and activation mechanics on that landing, since the whole engine assumes it is settled.
