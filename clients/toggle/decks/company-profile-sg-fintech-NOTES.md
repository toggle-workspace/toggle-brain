## Open risk (Osome play) — from BD discussion

We do NOT currently have in-house depth on cross-border accounting / tax / incorporation content. Options before pitching that vertical hard:
1. Position the compliance-content **system** (SME-interview-driven editorial, where the client's own experts are the E-E-A-T source) rather than claiming domain expertise — this is defensible and honest.
2. Line up 1–2 vetted domain writers/reviewers on retainer *before* the pitch, so external help is a managed input, not a dependency.
3. Scope those engagements SEO-architecture-first (technical, programmatic, internal linking) where our edge is real, with editorial ramping later.
