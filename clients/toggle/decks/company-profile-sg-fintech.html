The Fintech Growth Problem

Fintech doesn't have a demand problem. It has three structural ones.

CAC only goes one way

VC-funded competitors crowd the same auctions. Fintech CAC is up 15% year on year globally; a financial-app customer in Southeast Asia already costs US$200+. Paid-only growth is a treadmill that speeds up.

Compliance shrinks the playbook

Fair-and-balanced rules on investment products, DPT marketing bans, and now FSG-03 accountability for every influencer and affiliate you touch. Most agencies produce work that dies in legal review.

The stack fragments as you scale

Analytics, CRM, lifecycle, attribution — bought tool by tool, sprint by sprint. Nobody owns the whole picture, so every channel optimises for itself and the funnel leaks between them.

And underneath all three: trust. In a category where users hand you their money, every touchpoint either builds authority or borrows against it.
