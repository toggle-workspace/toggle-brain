## The conflict of interest, and why it comes first

UNITAR is a paying client. MMU, Universiti Malaya-Wales, the University of Cyberjaya and Kolej Yayasan UEM all compete with UNITAR for the same Malaysian students. Our own case file at `brain/case-studies/unitar.md` claims 77% impression share of that category, which means the proof we would use to win these accounts describes share taken from the people we would be taking it from.

Disclose this unprompted in the first five minutes of the four affected meetings. Say that we work with UNITAR, that we will not move budget or creative between competing accounts, and that if they want an exclusivity clause in the category we will discuss it at contract. A prospect who finds out later treats it as concealment, and a prospect who hears it first treats it as evidence that we are careful with client information.

There is a second collision. MMU and Universiti Malaya-Wales appear in each other's Ad Library results and bid on the same students. If both want to proceed, the first signature takes the segment. Cyberjaya against MMU is partial and workable with a written boundary, because the faculty mix differs. CDC against MMU is narrow and can be handled by carving out the executive MBA. Kolej Yayasan Saad has no conflict at all.

## The question that will come up in all five

Somebody will ask to speak to a client who will vouch for the work. Per `brain/case-studies/PROOF-GAPS.md`, gap 1, we have no named client quote and no contactable reference for any of the nine cases. That is a real hole and five meetings in seven days will find it.

The honest answer, in our voice: we have not asked our clients to act as references, and we would rather show you the method than hand you a phone number. Then offer the checkable thing instead, which is the finding we just gave them for free and which they can verify without us.

Closing this gap is three emails to UNITAR, Al Hidayah and Kith and Kin. It is the highest value hour available before 2 September.

## What we could not verify for any prospect

Two walls stopped every line of research and both should be stated plainly rather than papered over.

**Google Ads.** The Google Ads Transparency Center is a JavaScript application that returns an empty document to any automated fetch. For MMU, Cyberjaya, Universiti Malaya-Wales and CDC we can prove a Google Ads account exists, because the conversion tags sit in their published tag containers, but we cannot prove any of them is spending, or on what. Somebody needs to open adstransparency.google.com in a browser and search each advertiser. That is fifteen minutes of manual work and it closes the largest remaining hole.

**TikTok.** The TikTok Ad Library covers the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. Malaysian commercial ads are not in any public archive. We can see that MMU carries a TikTok pixel, which proves intent to measure TikTok traffic, and we cannot see anyone's TikTok ads. Say this plainly on the call rather than guessing.

**First response time.** Nobody has submitted a test enquiry to any of the five. `playbooks/sales-play.md` treats first response time as a core part of the diagnosis, and it remains the cheapest unclaimed finding available. This needs a human to send it, from a personal number and email, because it is an outward facing action and it should be Toggle's decision rather than an automated one.

## Method note

Round one ran five research agents in parallel, one per institution. Round two attacked their conclusions from three angles: a falsifier that defaulted to refuted, an evidence closer working through a real browser, and a room readiness critic reading against `playbooks/sales-play.md` and `brain/voice/writing-standards.md`.

The falsifier and the evidence closer both stopped early on a session limit. The most important checks they were assigned were completed by hand afterward, and one of them overturned the central MMU finding. The remaining gap from their unfinished work is the Google Ads Transparency Center check and the body copy of individual ads, both listed above. Every claim in the five audit files is labeled with what it rests on.
