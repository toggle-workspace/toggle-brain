The problem

Generalist marketing quietly leaks money on the Marketplace.

What usually happens

An agency runs generic B2B ads and blog posts, ignores listing SEO and evaluator journeys, and can’t tell you which spend actually moved paid conversions. You pay for motion, not growth.

What we do instead

We diagnose the whole funnel — listing, trial, evaluation, renewal — before recommending a channel. Sometimes the fix isn’t ads at all; it’s the listing or the trial flow. We tell you first.

B · Build checklist

  • Scaffold page at /atlassian-marketplace; add “Atlassian Marketplace” to the Solutions nav.
  • Implement the 9 sections above in order; light/dark alternation as modelled.
  • Wire both primary CTAs to the booking link; wire the lead-magnet form to the CRM/email tool.
  • Fully responsive (single-column stack ≤640px); the process row and stat grid must reflow.
  • Apply tabular-nums to every numeral; accents as fills only (no blue body text).
  • Accessibility: AA contrast (tokens already pass), semantic headings, alt text on client logos, visible focus rings (#3056C9).
  • SEO: title/meta for “Atlassian Marketplace marketing”; schema for case studies & reviews; internal links to /work pages.
  • Performance: monochrome SVG logos, no stock photography, lazy-load below-fold images.
