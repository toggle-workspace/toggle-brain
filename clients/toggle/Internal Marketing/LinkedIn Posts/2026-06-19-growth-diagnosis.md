## Post 1 — The diagnosis (Pillar 1)

A founder paid us to run his ads last month.

We gave the money back and told him to fix his sales team first.

His leads were fine. His ads were fine. His team was just taking 48 hours to call a new lead back — and by then the buyer had already signed with someone faster.

No ad budget fixes that. More spend on a leaky funnel doesn't scale revenue. It scales the leak.

So we don't run campaigns on day one. We run a diagnosis first.

Before we touch an ad account, we benchmark the things most agencies skip because they can't bill for them:

→ First-response time on inbound leads
→ Mobile load speed (6 seconds quietly kills more sales than any bad ad)
→ Checkout drop-off before payment
→ Whether anything is being tracked at all

Sometimes the fix is the CRM. Sometimes it's the lead form. And sometimes the ads really are the answer — except now they actually convert, because the system behind them finally works.

That's the difference between an ad agency and a growth partner. An ad agency sells you ads. A growth partner finds the bottleneck that's actually costing you money, even when it's nowhere near the ad account.

We'd rather hand back a retainer than spend your budget fixing the wrong thing.

What are you blaming on "bad marketing" right now that might be living somewhere else in your funnel?

## Post 2 — The setup mistake before the first ad (Pillar 3 / foundation-first)

"Our ads don't work."

We hear it almost every week. And 9 times out of 10, the ads were fine. The setup wasn't.

Here's what we usually find when a brand has been running paid campaigns on its own:

→ No Meta Pixel or Google Tag firing correctly
→ Conversion events never configured, so the algorithm has nothing to optimise toward
→ No UTMs, so nobody can say which campaign drove which sale
→ Overlapping audiences competing against each other in one messy account
→ No baseline — they don't even know their CPL or ROAS before they start spending

Then the numbers come back ugly, and the creative gets the blame.

Before a single Ringgit goes to ads, four things have to be in place:

→ Tracking installed and verified
→ A clean account structure
→ A baseline to measure against
→ One clear objective: awareness, leads, or sales

Ads amplify whatever is already there. Point real budget at a broken funnel and you don't get growth. You get the same broken funnel, faster and at full scale.

This is the unglamorous part most agencies skip, because nobody gets excited about pixel setup. We do it first because it's the only thing that makes the creative pay off later.

One enrolment campaign we set up this way came in at a 47% cheaper cost per lead than the client was getting before — same product, same market.

So before you launch your next campaign, ask one thing: can you actually see which ad drove your last sale? If you can't, that's where to start. Not the creative.
