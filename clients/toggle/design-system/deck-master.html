Where growth actually breaks

Most growth problems are system problems wearing a marketing costume. There are three of them and they need different fixes.

Mode one
The auction is trained on the wrong thing

Nobody feeds closed revenue back to the platform, so the algorithm optimizes toward form fillers. Lead volume looks healthy while enrollments or sales fall. The fix is server-side outcome events, not better targeting.

Mode two
The leads die after the submit

First response time runs into days, the CRM has no owner, and the landing page asks for more than the visitor is ready to give. Media spend is not the constraint here, and adding to it makes the leak bigger.

Mode three
One creative angle carries the whole account

Frequency climbs, cost per lead drifts up every month, and each new budget increase buys less. The account needs a testing system that produces several winners, rather than one hero asset defended for a year.

The cost of guessing wrong: UNITAR was paying RM154 on Google, RM291 on Facebook and RM525 on TikTok per lead by mid-2024 while spending more every month. The channel was never the problem.

Who we take on, and who we turn down

We have learned which conditions decide whether this works. These are not preferences, and we have lost money finding each one out.

We are a fit when

  • You can name the single number you are judged on this year, and you know roughly what a customer is worth.
  • Someone on your side can approve a tracking change inside two weeks, because everything downstream depends on it.
  • You are willing to be told the problem sits outside marketing, and to act on that if it turns out to be true.
  • Your media budget can absorb a testing period of six to eight weeks without the whole plan being judged in week two.

We decline when

  • The brief is to run ads while the website, the sales follow-up and the offer stay untouched.
  • The decision is being made only on agency fee, because the fee is the smallest line in the acquisition math and optimizing it costs you the other ninety percent.
  • Three agencies have been cycled through in eighteen months with the same brief each time.
  • Nobody internally owns the CRM, so closed revenue can never reach the ad platforms.

If we are not a fit, we will say so on this call and point you at someone who is. That costs us a proposal and saves you a year.
