Executive summary

Plan

SignWell spends most of its budget on non-brand searches that do not convert, and sends one email after sign-up. We fix tracking first, scale branded SEM, then add email and social once the data holds.

Where you are

  • Poorly set up non-brand SEM campaigns take 52% of spend and return 1.9% of conversions.
  • Unwanted pixels clutter your Meta setup and can feed bad signals to any Meta ads you run.
  • SignWell sends one email after sign-up and none after a trial lapses.

What we change

  • One tracking spec, then a SignWell-owned Google Ads account.
  • We scale brand-focused SEM first and run test campaigns to find the non-brand topics worth funding. We grow organic social while the SEM account clears its learning phase.
  • Paid social on Meta, LinkedIn and Reddit.
  • Email, in-app prompts, retargeting and founder-led organic.
  • Organic social media growth.

What it costs

$20–25k

A month in media, the budget you run today. October starts at $20,000 on search, and social opens in November once the pixels are clean.
