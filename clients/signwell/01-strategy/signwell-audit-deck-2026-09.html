Plan

SignWell spends most of its budget on searches that do not convert, and runs no retargeting, almost no email, and little organic. We fix the measurement, move the money to buyers who raise ACV, and open three channels that cost less than search.

  • Search rebuilt around API, team, healthcare and switcher buyers.
  • Paid social on Meta, LinkedIn and Reddit.
  • Email, in-app prompts, retargeting and founder-led organic.

What it costs

A month in media, the budget you run today. October starts at $20,000 on search, and social opens in November once the pixels are clean.

What you get by January

  • Three channels feeding the top of the funnel.
  • A weekly dashboard in place of the monthly PDF.

Measurement and reporting

You get one tracking spec and one live dashboard

We write the specification with your engineers, remove what does not belong, and replace the monthly PDF with a dashboard you open any day.

The spec

Six events every channel shares

  • Sign-up and qualified sign-up, with questionnaire answers.
  • Document sent and limit hit.
  • Plan changed and purchase, with real value.
  • One GA4 property and one container.

The cleanup

Everything that distorts bidding

  • Meta fires once per event.
  • We remove the Universal Analytics tag and the foreign Bing tags.
  • We exclude accounts.google.com as a referrer.
  • We import purchases into Google Ads.

The dashboard

What Lawrence and Henry see weekly

  • Spend, qualified sign-ups and paying customers by campaign.
  • Cost per qualified sign-up against the $70 line.
  • Nurture and retargeting lift against their holdouts.
  • ACV by channel and by vertical.
The monthly PDF retires in October.
