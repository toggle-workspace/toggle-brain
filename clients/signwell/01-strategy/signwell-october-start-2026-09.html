Why this order

Google learns from brand search while we find the posts that work

We use the Google track to prepare non-brand search, and the social track to prepare Meta ads.

Google

A new ad account starts with no history.

Google sets your bids by learning which clicks turn into sign-ups. A new account has no data yet, so Google needs several weeks of sign-ups before it sets your bids well.

1,044Brand search brought in 1,044 qualified sign-ups in August, so the new account gets about 1,000 a month to learn from.

Non-brand search waits until December. In August it spent $9,088 and brought in 24 qualified sign-ups.

Social

Paid social has not worked for SignWell yet.

LinkedIn ads spent $2,342 in August and brought in no qualified sign-ups. You stopped a $400 Reddit test soon after it started.

$0We test your messages in organic posts, with no ad spend. After eight weeks we know which topics your buyers respond to.

In December we turn the posts that worked into Meta ads, and we show them first to the people who followed or engaged with your pages.

In December we add non-brand search and Meta ads, using what we learned in October and November.

A Google Ads account in your name

You open it, or you let us open it for you. SignWell owns it, and Toggle works inside it as your manager.

These are the Google tools on your website that record each sign-up. We use them to count qualified sign-ups in the new account from its first day.

Admin access to your social pages

We need your Facebook page, your Instagram account and your LinkedIn page.

One person who approves posts
