# SignWell: Style Pack

> Base: `prompts/style-packs/toggle-default.md`. This file overrides only what differs.

> from a brand guide. Replace it with their real guidelines once we have them.

## Voice overrides
- Tone: plain, practical and unfussy. They sell simplicity against DocuSign's bloat, and the copy matches. Use short claims with a number attached. SignWell is a US software company, so write in American English with US dollar figures.
- Words we use for this client: qualified sign-up, ACV, product-led, trial, switchers
- Words they use: eSignature, documents, agreements, compliant, audit trail, zero setup, no account needed, legally binding, sandbox, embedded signing.
- Words to avoid for this client: enterprise, platform, suite, transformation, agreement intelligence. Those belong to DocuSign and PandaDoc, and using them hands SignWell's positioning to the companies it competes with.
- One number to settle before any copy ships: the homepage claims documents get signed 40% faster, while `/docusign-alternative/` claims 60%. Ad copy has to pick one.

## Platform tone
- Google Search: their own live copy is the best reference. "No Account Needed to Sign, Free and Takes Under a Minute" and, for the API set, "Integrate document signing in days, not months. No bloated SDK, just clean."
- LinkedIn: compliance and customer stories. SignNow shows the format works, with 52 ads built entirely from DocuSign switcher stories in clinical research.
- Meta: uncontested ground. DocuSign sells enterprise AI there and PandaDoc sells revenue operations, so the simple switching message is open. Write to an office manager holding a renewal quote, not to a procurement committee.
- TikTok: not applicable. Neither SignWell nor any competitor runs TikTok ads.

## Examples of "yes / no"
- ✓ This works: "No account needed to sign. Free, and it takes under a minute." It is concrete and checkable, and no competitor with a paywalled free tier can copy it.
- ✓ This works: "SOC 2, HIPAA and eIDAS on the free plan." It can be checked, and it is true only of them in this price bracket.
- ✗ This doesn't: "Streamline your agreement workflows." Every competitor could paste that line unchanged, which is the test it fails.
