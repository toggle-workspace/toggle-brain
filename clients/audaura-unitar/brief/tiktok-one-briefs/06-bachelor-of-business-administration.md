# UNITAR – Bachelor of Business Administration (Hons) (3-Year Degree, Up to 100% PTPTN)

## Project Name
UNITAR – Bachelor of Business Administration (Hons) (3-Year Degree, PTPTN-Eligible)

## Project Description
UNITAR International University is recruiting school leavers into the Bachelor of Business Administration (Hons) at its main campus in Kelana Jaya, Petaling Jaya. Non-physical product (undergraduate degree programme). Target audience: 17–20-year-old Malaysians who have just finished SPM, STPM, matriculation, foundation, or a diploma, and want a business degree that leads to a job in a company or their own business. Collaboration goal: drive enquiries to UNITAR via unitar.my/request-info.

## Product Description
A 3-year (9-semester) MQA-accredited Bachelor of Business Administration (Hons) at UNITAR, delivered on campus (with an online option). Students learn the core of running a business — management, marketing, finance, economics, and business analytics — and pick one of six areas to specialise in: Marketing, Finance, Human Resource Management, Logistics, International Business, or Management. Up to 100% PTPTN financing is available, so students can fund it with a study loan instead of upfront cash. Total tuition is RM 45,000.

## Product Selling Points
- **Fund it with up to 100% PTPTN** — you can cover the whole degree with a PTPTN study loan, so you don't need the cash upfront to start.
- **Pick the business area you actually want** — specialise in Marketing, Finance, HR, Logistics, International Business, or Management, so your degree matches the job you're aiming for.
- **Learn how a real business runs** — money, people, marketing, and operations — the skills you need whether you want to work in a company or start your own.

## Preferred Creator Description
- **Identity:** Malaysian school leaver aged 17–20 who just finished SPM, STPM, foundation, or a diploma and is deciding what to study. Interested in business, marketing, or running their own thing. No degree yet.
- **Style:** "What I'm studying and why", "How I chose my degree", "A day at my uni" — honest talking-head storytime in BM, Manglish, or English. Real, friendly, a bit excited, not over-produced.
- **Audience:** Malaysian school leavers (17–20) figuring out what to study after SPM/STPM, and their parents who want a degree that leads to a real job.

End frame: UNITAR logo + CTA "Click on the button below to learn more."

- **Compliance:** Without the permission from all necessary rights holders, creators must not include copyrighted music in their videos. Creators may use tracks selected from the Commercial Music Library.

## Avoid
Voiceover-only, slideshow, corporate narrator, "invest in your future" / "be your own boss" clichés, specific salary claims, guaranteed-job language, comparisons to named universities, "the world needs entrepreneurs" generic openers, implying the degree automatically lands you a management job or a high salary, flashing luxury cars / watches / cash, and any wording that frames PTPTN as "free money."
