# UNITAR – Bachelor of Information Technology (Hons) (3-Year Tech Degree, Up to 100% PTPTN)

## Project Name
UNITAR – Bachelor of Information Technology (Hons) (3-Year Degree, PTPTN-Eligible)

## Project Description
UNITAR International University is recruiting school leavers into the Bachelor of Information Technology (Hons) at its main campus in Kelana Jaya, Petaling Jaya. Non-physical product (undergraduate degree programme). Target audience: 17–20-year-old Malaysians who have just finished SPM, STPM, matriculation, foundation, or a diploma, are into computers, gaming, or tech, and want a degree that leads to a job in the tech industry. Collaboration goal: drive enquiries to UNITAR via unitar.my/request-info.

## Product Description
A 3-year (9-semester) MQA-accredited Bachelor of Information Technology (Hons) at UNITAR, delivered on campus. Students learn programming, databases, networking, cloud, and AI, and pick one of four areas to specialise in: Cybersecurity, Cloud Computing, Data Analytics, or Computer Networking. Alongside the degree, students can earn up to 20 Microsoft certifications. Up to 100% PTPTN financing is available. Total tuition is RM 45,000.

## Product Selling Points
- **Specialise in the tech skills companies are hiring for** — choose Cybersecurity, Cloud Computing, Data Analytics, or Networking, and learn the hands-on stuff, not just theory.
- **Get up to 20 Microsoft certifications while you study** — real proof of skill you can show any employer, on top of your degree.
- **Fund it with up to 100% PTPTN** — cover the whole degree with a PTPTN study loan, so you don't need the cash upfront to start.

## Preferred Creator Description
- **Identity:** Malaysian school leaver aged 17–20 who just finished SPM, STPM, foundation, or a diploma and loves computers, gaming, coding, or tech in general. Deciding what to study. No degree yet.
- **Style:** "What I'm studying and why", "A day as an IT student", "Skills I'm learning that actually get you a tech job" — honest talking-head storytime in BM, Manglish, or English. Real, curious, a bit geeky, not over-produced.
- **Audience:** Malaysian school leavers (17–20) into tech and gaming who want a career in IT, and their parents who want a degree that leads to a real job.

## Video Suggestions
- **Duration:** 30–35s, 9:16.
- **Hook (first 2s):** "POV: kau minat komputer & gaming — ni degree yang boleh buat kau kerja tech." OR "Nak jadi cybersecurity analyst atau cloud engineer? Start dengan ni." OR "How I'm turning my gaming habit into a tech career."
- **Visuals:** Creator in a real student setting — at a computer lab, coding on a laptop, on campus, or showing their setup at home. Cut to them talking to camera about what they're learning. On-screen text must show: "Bachelor of Information Technology (Hons) · UNITAR · MQA/FA1697 · 3 years · Up to 100% PTPTN". End frame: UNITAR logo + CTA "Click on the button below to learn more."
- **Audio:** Upbeat, modern, slightly energetic track from TikTok's Commercial Music Library.
- **Compliance:** Without the permission from all necessary rights holders, creators must not include copyrighted music in their videos. Creators may use tracks selected from the Commercial Music Library.

## Avoid
Voiceover-only, slideshow, corporate narrator, "tech is the future" / "everyone should learn to code" clichés, specific salary claims, guaranteed-job language, comparisons to named universities, "AI is taking over" fear openers, implying the degree automatically lands you a tech job or a high salary, flashing luxury cars / watches / cash, jargon like "ecosystem" or "stack", and any wording that frames PTPTN as "free money."
