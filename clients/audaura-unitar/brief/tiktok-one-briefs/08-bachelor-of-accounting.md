# UNITAR – Bachelor of Accounting (Hons) (3.5-Year Degree with ACCA Exemptions, Up to 100% PTPTN)

## Project Name
UNITAR – Bachelor of Accounting (Hons) (3.5-Year Degree, PTPTN-Eligible)

## Project Description
UNITAR International University is recruiting school leavers into the Bachelor of Accounting (Hons) at its main campus in Kelana Jaya, Petaling Jaya. Non-physical product (undergraduate degree programme). Target audience: 17–20-year-old Malaysians who have just finished SPM, STPM, matriculation, foundation, or a diploma, are good with numbers, and want a professional career as an accountant or auditor. Collaboration goal: drive enquiries to UNITAR via unitar.my/request-info.

## Product Description
A 3.5-year (10-semester) MQA-accredited Bachelor of Accounting (Hons) at UNITAR, delivered on campus. Students learn financial accounting, auditing, taxation, corporate reporting, and management accounting. The degree is recognised by ACCA, CPA Australia, ICAEW, and CIMA, and gives students exemptions from those professional exams — plus a pathway to sit two ACCA papers during their internship. Up to 100% PTPTN financing is available. Total tuition is RM 48,000.

## Product Selling Points
- **Get a head start on becoming a chartered accountant** — the degree gives you exemptions from ACCA, CPA Australia, and ICAEW exams, so you finish your professional qualification faster.
- **Sit two ACCA papers during your internship** — you graduate already ahead on the exams most accountants take after uni.
- **Fund it with up to 100% PTPTN** — cover the whole degree with a PTPTN study loan, so you don't need the cash upfront to start.
