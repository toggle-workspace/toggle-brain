# UC Network Leads, Intake 2026

**Executive summary** · Performance through 31 July 2026 · Prepared 3 August 2026

---

## Position at the end of the cycle

The UC network recorded 732 leads against a 3,255 lead target for the March to June cycle, or 22.5% of plan. Kuching is the only campus that hit its number. The rest finished between a tenth and a third of target, and the four campuses carrying the largest budgets sit at the bottom of that range.

Two campuses show what the network produces when a campaign fits its market. Kuching cleared its target on 5% of network budget, and Kota Kinabalu delivered the second highest lead volume on 12%. In both markets, prospects pulled toward degree level Education programmes ahead of the diplomas we built the campaign around.

## Two causes behind the shortfall

**Coverage.** We built the campaign around 18 creatives covering 6 locations across 3 priority programmes, and the network runs 10 campuses. Four campuses spent their budget against creative made for somewhere else. The programme mix shows it: Business Diploma, our stated second priority, produced zero or one lead in five locations, and Multimedia and IT accounted for 5.3% of network leads. The campaigns are carrying one programme, DECE, plus whatever each campus falls back on.

**Programme level.** We lead with UC's own diploma programmes, and diplomas convert at a low rate once admissions picks up the lead. DECE Diploma turned 5.4% of its leads into applications last year. The RC degree tracks did far better over the same period: Bachelor of Education (Hons) at 18.8%, and Bachelor of Education (Early Childhood Education) (Hons) at 27.8%. Those two degrees make up 16.7% of UC lead volume today, and two campuses account for 82% of that volume. We are spending the bulk of the budget at the level where the fewest leads turn into students.

The site repeats the problem after the click. Our programme card shows one campus and hides the other seven behind a chevron, with only the Main Campus registration code on display. A prospect in Kuching or Kuantan reads a page that gives them no signal the programme runs in their city.

---

## Recommendations

**Rebuild the programme card so campus availability shows without a click.** Every campaign in the network routes through this card on the way to the enquiry form, so one fix lifts all ten campuses. The card should state that the programme runs at 8 locations, let a visitor pick theirs, and show the registration and MQA reference for that campus. We have five design options drafted and ready for a decision, and we recommend the labeled campus selector or the campus chip row, since both remove the hidden state.

**Move the centre of gravity from UC diplomas to RC bachelor's programmes.** The two Bachelor of Education tracks convert three to five times better than the diploma we built the network plan around, and they run in only two campuses. Adding them across the remaining eight, with creative of their own, will lift applications faster than any change we can make to lead volume. Keep DECE running underneath as a volume driver.

**Size creative production for all ten campuses.** Ten campuses across three priority programmes needs close to double the creative the current plan funds. Until that gap closes, the lead counts for Business Diploma and Multimedia measure our coverage rather than market demand.

**Fund the campuses that are proving demand.** Kuching and Kota Kinabalu produce results on a small share of budget while the largest budgets sit in the weakest markets. Shift budget share toward the two that work, and hold the others flat until they have creative of their own. Document how the Kuching and Kota Kinabalu teams set up their targeting and creative, since that is the template the other eight campuses need.

---

## Sequencing

Site and creative work comes first. If we reallocate budget while half the campuses lack their own creative and all ten share one programme card, we move money between campaigns without changing what a prospect sees. Fix the card, extend creative to all ten campuses, add the RC degree tracks, and the next cycle will tell us which markets are soft.
