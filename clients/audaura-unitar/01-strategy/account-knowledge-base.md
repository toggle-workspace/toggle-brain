## 0. TL;DR — what this engagement is

**UNITAR International University** (Malaysia's pioneering private university group — 12 campuses after acquiring **Cosmopoint College Group in 2024**, QS 5-Star for Online Learning, 98% graduate employability) runs a **performance-marketing lead-gen engagement** to drive enrolments. The work is delivered by **Toggle Solutions** through **Audaura Digital Sdn Bhd** (a 2025-incorporated intermediary brand). UNITAR pays Audaura "Media Fees"; **Toggle earns 55% of those fees**. The mandate came via UNITAR's **2025 RFP "Road to 10K"** (service period **1 Jul 2025 – 30 Jun 2026**, total budget **RM9.5M–11.5M**), won against incumbent **Mindshare (GroupM)**.

Three channels — **Google (Search + PMax), Meta, TikTok** — segmented by **intake cycle, geo tier, language (EN/BM), funnel stage, and campus type**. The single defining problem of the account: **platform-reported CPL (~RM55–130) hides a Salesforce-attributed cost-per-real-lead 5–18× higher (RM700–1,747)** — everything (CAPI, EC4L tracking, two-stage validation, PMax kill, Instant-Form pivot) is built to close that gap.

## 7. CPL economics — the central tension ⚠️

The **CPL Calculation** workbook reconciles platform-reported leads against **Salesforce-attributed** real leads. The gap is the whole story:

| Basis | 2024 online leads | Spend (RM) | **Blended CPL (RM)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Platform-reported | 48,414 | 4,612,492 | **95** |
| **Salesforce-attributed** | 4,622 | 4,283,664 | **926.80** |
| SF + late attribution | 5,941 | 4,283,664 | 721.03 |

- **Google SEM (SF-attributed): CPL RM1,747** (RM1,335 with late attribution). By cycle (SF): C1 RM2,255 · C2 RM2,329 · C3 RM1,260.

- **Implication:** plans quote optimistic platform CPLs (RM70–175); true cost-per-qualified-lead is **5–18× higher**. The CAPI / EC4L / two-stage-validation / downstream-Salesforce-bidding programme exists to close this gap. **Dependencies:** without Meta CAPI, CPL +150% / ~10% leads lost; without Google EC4L, +130% / ~20% lost.

- **TikTok:** native **Vlog** for TOF + Instant Forms; **T2** most reliable tier; **TikTok ONE (KOL/creator)** launched mid-May'26 at **RM53.84 — best TikTok CPL ever**, now the growth engine. *Didn't:* **T1b (Sabah/Sarawak)** weakest (RM85–578), high-production-for-lead-gen, BOF retargeting (pool too small), slow creative refresh → fatigue (**#1 root cause of CPL spikes**).
- **TikTok creative comparison (key finding):** for Instant Forms, **native/TikTok-style ≫ high-production** — CTR 3.16% vs 0.24%, submission rate **0.62% vs 0.03% (20×)**. *"High-production looks good but underperforms at motivating action."* (For awareness, Vlog also wins on LPV rate 52% vs 26%.)
- **Benchmarks (TikTok report):** MY generic ~RM50/lead; global **education ~RM1,090/lead** → UNITAR's higher-intent/longer-form leads justifiably cost above generic but sit far below global ed.

## 15. The playbook in one screen (durable learnings)

1. **Instant Forms beat Website Conversion** on Meta — and **native/creator-style beats high-production** for action (TikTok ONE is the breakout).
2. **BM beats EN** on Google PMax (~50% cheaper); **competitor + brand + SPM** are the efficient Google themes; **generic/informational keywords are the money pit** → weekly keyword hygiene.
3. **Feed downstream Salesforce conversions back to the platforms** (CAPI/EC4L/value bidding) — the single highest-leverage fix for the platform-vs-CRM CPL gap, and Google's most-repeated recommendation.
4. **Refresh creative every ≤2 weeks** — fatigue is the #1 cause of CPL spikes; **harden the website/tracking** — outages repeatedly wrecked months.
5. **Allocate by winning-channel-per-state**, not evenly; **front-load budget into result-release windows** (SPM = the best month, April).
6. **Early Childhood Education / teaching is the demand engine**; **SPM/school-leavers** the priority top-of-funnel; **Main Campus** the magnet, **outstation** the growth gap.
7. **Specify the right entity/price in ads** (RM12K MBA = UUCKL only) and respect programme compliance constraints.
