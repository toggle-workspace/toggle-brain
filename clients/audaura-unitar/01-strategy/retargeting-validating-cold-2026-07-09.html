Half Your Leads Stall at One Exact Step

Lead volume is healthy — up 15% week on week. The leak is one step in: leads submit, enter the validating stage, then go quiet. Only about half now clear that step to an appointment — down from roughly seven in ten a year ago. That one stage is where the demand is escaping.

Why this is the cohort to fix first
These students already raised a hand — the ad spend, the click and the form-fill are all paid for. They aren't cold traffic; they're one contactable conversation from an appointment. An avalanche of new leads won't help — the sales desk already can't work them all at scale. This layer re-verifies them and routes them to the new nurturing team, so it adds enrolments without adding load to that desk. Note: part of the gap may be sales capacity or genuinely uncontactable leads — the root cause is still being confirmed with Shifa. This layer recovers the reachable slice first, while that's investigated.
This isn't a lead-volume problem. It's a validating-stage re-engagement problem — and it has its own fix.

Slide 4 · How It Works — Belief: "There's a real mechanism matched to the stall — not just 'show more ads.'"

How We Re-Open the Stalled Cohort

We upload the exact validating-stage list into Meta, Google and TikTok as a matched audience, run a short intent-aware sequence, and route re-engagers through a phone-verified door so the recovered lead is actually contactable — then feed the validation signal back so the engines learn who really re-validates.

01
Match the exact list
Upload hashed phone/email of validating-stage cold leads as a CRM Custom / Customer-Match audience. Not lookalikes — the actual people.
→
02
Sequence to the objection
2–3 touches over several days: intake-deadline nudge → objection-handler (PTPTN "bantuan", 98% employability) → easy re-entry. Matched to why they stalled.
→
03
Verified re-entry
A short one-time-code (OTP) form or WhatsApp click-to-chat — 91% of form-fillers already move to WhatsApp — so the recovered lead is reachable, not another dead end.
→
04
Feed validation back
CAPI now passes signal to the "applied" stage (7 stages). We optimise to the validating event — so ads chase students who re-validate, not just re-click.
We tag it separately. Recovered leads carry their own labels in Salesforce, so we can prove they're genuinely extra — never claiming a lead sales or nurturing would have closed anyway.
Handled by nurturing, not the stretched sales desk. Recovered leads are phone-verified and routed to the new nurturing team being stood up — so this adds enrolments without piling more onto a desk that already can't keep up.

Why the budget stays small
The validating-stage cold cohort is a defined Salesforce segment — roughly 3,500 stalled leads a month, not the millions in the cold universe. RM 8k reaches that whole list across a short sequence, on every platform where it clears the match floor.
The one deliberate cost
Optimising all the way to the validating event lifts cost-per-action (~3× — the team's own estimate is CPL rising ~300%) — but on a warm, already-acquired list the absolute spend stays tiny, and the KPI we're buying is a validated student, not a click.
Start at RM 8k/mo. Scale only on a proven cost-per-recovered-validated-lead — final number set with you.
