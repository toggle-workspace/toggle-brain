# UNITAR Ad Copies, Cycle 3 2026 (Batch 5, Meta, English)

> English copies for the 13 Batch 5 creatives cleared to launch.
> Written 2026-09-20. Revised after two rounds of `/stop-slop` and `/copy-test`.
> Each ad ships in two versions: a clean version, and a giveaway version whose closing
> sentence carries the MacBook Neo draw, matched to the NEO STICKER creative.
> Account: UNITAR (MYR) - Audaura, `1034316391892752`.

**Two standing rules for this batch, set by Zaid on 2026-09-20:**

1. **No tuition fee, price or cost appears in any ad.** This removes the UUCKL
   and UIU pricing trap from the whole batch, since that rule only bites when a
   fee is quoted.
2. **Copy stays close to what is already live.** These are written to sit
   alongside the current UNITAR set, not to differentiate from it. Where a line
   echoes a live headline, that is deliberate, and reviewers should not treat it
   as a defect.

Standard footnote, ships as visible ad text under any ad carrying an asterisk:

`*Terms and conditions apply. Subject to eligibility and approval.`

**Four headlines are held deliberately against reviewer objection.** 2A, 2B, 6B
and 8A address the reader's education stage, which both review passes flagged as
a Meta personal-attributes risk. They are kept because UNITAR is running
`"Doing HR Without the Qualification?"`, `"Finished SPM, No Idea What Next?"` and
`"Degree Holder? Teach Next"` in market right now, and because the documented
disapproval risk on this account came from the June set's `"Unemployed?"` and
`"Lost your job?"` lines, which assert joblessness rather than education stage.
Third-person alternates are listed under each one. Swap them in if any ad in this
batch draws a rejection.

# 1. Diploma in Occupational Safety & Health

Creative folder 13.

**Compliance note:** this diploma is **not DOSH-department accredited**. No line
may imply it qualifies the holder to hold the statutory Safety Officer role.

Chat with our counsellors about the entry route.

**Headline:** Inspections First, Then Incident Reports.

**Body Copy:**

The Diploma in Occupational Safety and Health at UNITAR teaches hazard inspection and incident reporting. Students practice both before they graduate.

Our counsellors can take you through the syllabus.

# Giveaway versions

Every ad above has a second version for the NEO STICKER creative. The headline
does not change. The closing sentence is replaced with:

> Students who complete registration for the next intake may be entered into a weekly draw for one of 350 laptops, running until October 2026. Entry conditions apply.* This promotion is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by Meta.

Rules this line follows, from `c3-macbook-giveaway-google-search-bm.md`:

1. **"May be entered", never a promised win.** Registration alone does not enter
   anyone. The contest terms require the registration fee, administration fees
   and first-semester tuition paid, Malaysian citizenship, minimum programme
   entry requirements met, and continued active enrolment.
2. **No fee figure**, which the no-fee rule for this batch already covers.
3. **No brand name by default.** UNITAR is not an authorized Apple reseller. A
   MacBook-named variant ships as a swappable alternate.
4. **No intake date.** September is at or past its cut-off and no confirmed date
   exists in this repo.
