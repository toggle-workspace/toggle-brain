## Blocking compliance flag (read before activating anything)

All 16 images carry **"FULLY FUNDED by PERKESO"** as on-image text, sitting next to **"RM4000"**. The June copy spec forbids this in writing:

> Do NOT use "Fully funded" (LP says "up to RM4,000, subject to eligibility, approval and ESO referral").

Read together, the two elements claim the programme costs RM4,000 and that PERKESO covers all of it. The landing page does not support that claim. Meta reviews text baked into images, and this account already carries accepted Personal Attributes risk from the "Unemployed?" call-out, so an unsubstantiated funding claim raises account-level risk rather than single-ad risk.

Three of the four July ads already live in this ad set carry the same claim, so the gate was flagged in June and never cleared. Nikki or Kelvin needs to sign off, or creative needs to re-export with "Funded by PERKESO EIS" before these go live.

Every primary text below uses the compliant wording and carries the mandated disclaimer, so the ad copy is clean even where the image is not.
