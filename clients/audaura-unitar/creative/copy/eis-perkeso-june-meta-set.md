# UNITAR × PERKESO EIS — June Meta Set (ad copy + lead form)

_UNITAR · PERKESO EIS reskilling · Meta (FB/IG) lead ads · goal: qualified leads._
_Aligned to the June creative batch (Data Science, Business Analytics, Business Communication, Digital Business)._

## Angle: "Unemployed" — blatant eligibility call-out (client direction)
The offer is only for unemployed / retrenched Malaysians (PERKESO EIS eligibility), so the copy calls that out directly in both primary text and headlines. "Unemployed" is anchored to the EIS eligibility fact ("EIS funding is for Malaysians who have lost their jobs") — the truthful framing and the one most likely to survive review.

## Rules (before running)
- **Meta Personal Attributes risk (accepted).** Copy that asserts employment status ("Unemployed?", "Lost your job?") is a top auto-disapproval trigger and, if repeatedly rejected, can restrict the ad account. Client has chosen the blatant approach knowingly. Mitigation: anchor every "unemployed" mention to EIS eligibility, monitor disapprovals closely, and keep a softer backup variant ready to swap in if the account gets flagged.
- **No official endorsement.** Say "under the PERKESO EIS initiative" or "funded by PERKESO EIS" with the disclaimer. Never "government-supported."
- **Funding line:** "Funded by PERKESO EIS" is used per client direction. It always ships with the disclaimer so it stays substantiated. Do NOT use "Fully funded" (LP says "up to RM4,000, subject to eligibility, approval and ESO referral").
- **Disclaimer on every ad and the lead form (exact):** `*EIS funding subject to PERKESO eligibility. T&Cs apply.`
- **Safe claims only:** 98% graduate employability · Employer's Preferred University (Talentbank) · 100+ industry partners · MQA-accredited · 100% online · MCP and completion certificates. No job guarantees.
- **Format:** hook in first ~125 chars · headline ≤40 · one CTA per ad. Button: Apply Now.
