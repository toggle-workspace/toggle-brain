# UNITAR SEM ad copies and keywords, August 2026

**Deliverable:** `2026-08-unitar-sem-ad-copies-keywords.xlsx` (same folder)
**Scope:** 15 programmes, English and Bahasa Melayu, Google Search
**Ad account:** UNITAR - MY - Degree (905-920-2225)
**Format reference:** the Cycle 3 Google planning sheet (keyword bank, per campaign ad copy blocks with live character counts, extensions row)

## Programmes covered

Psychology (Diploma and Bachelor), Diploma in Logistics Management, Diploma in TESL, Bachelor of Mass Communication (Hons), Bachelor of Accounting and Finance (Hons), Bachelor of Education (Hons), Post-Graduate Diploma in Education, Diploma in Early Childhood Education, Bachelor of Business Management (Hons), Diploma in Business Management, Bachelor of Business Administration (Hons), Master of Business Administration, Diploma in Business Administration, Bachelor of Information Technology (Hons), Foundation in Management.

## What is inside

| Tab | Contents |
|---|---|
| README | Scope, asset counts, build notes, rollout steps |
| Campaign Setup | 30 rows (15 programmes x 2 languages): campaign name, ad group, destination URL, tracking template |
| Ad Copies EN | 300 assets, one four-column block per programme, character count calculated per cell |
| Ad Copies BM | 300 assets, written natively in Bahasa Melayu rather than translated |
| Keywords | 757 keywords tagged by match type, keyword focus and language |
| Negative Keywords | 20 shared negatives covering job seekers, free and informational intent, and public universities |
| Claims and Compliance | Every claim used, its source, and what UNITAR needs to confirm before launch |

Per programme per language: 12 headlines (30 characters), 3 long headlines (90), 1 short description (60), 4 descriptions (90), plus URL, display paths, sitelinks, callouts, structured snippet, call and CTA.

Keyword buckets per programme: Brand (exact and phrase), Programme core, Programme long-tail, Funding, Flexible learning. There are no duplicate keywords across programmes, so the ad groups do not compete with each other.

## Open items before launch

1. **Claims sign off.** Every claim is listed on the Claims and Compliance tab with what needs confirming. The binding one: the RM12,000 online MBA is UNITAR University College KL only, UIU is RM19,000, so the entity has to stay in the ad.
2. **Destination URLs.** The links point to level anchors on `unitar.my/university-college-programmes`, not to programme pages. Swap in programme landing pages where they exist.
3. **Budgets.** The daily budget column on Campaign Setup is set to TBC pending the cycle media plan.
4. **Match type rollout.** Launch phrase and exact first, then open broad once conversion data builds. Broad match and long-tail are where waste collected on this account before, so keyword hygiene runs weekly.
