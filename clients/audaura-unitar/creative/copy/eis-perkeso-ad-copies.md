# UNITAR × PERKESO EIS — Meta Ad Copy

_UNITAR · PERKESO EIS upskilling · Meta (FB/IG) lead ads · goal: qualified leads._

Five pain-point angles. Punchy, opportunity-framed. All four programmes covered.

## Rules (before running)
- **No personal-attribute copy.** Never imply the reader is jobless, broke, or "outdated." Put the change on the market, not the person.
- **No official endorsement.** Say "under the PERKESO EIS initiative" — never "government-supported."
- **Disclaimer on every ad (exact):** `*EIS funding subject to PERKESO eligibility. T&Cs apply.` Any asterisked line ships with this footnote in the same primary text.
- **Don't over-claim EIS.** Repo only confirms "Apprentice Law." Hedge as "under the initiative" + disclaimer; confirm per-programme eligibility before spend.
- **Safe claims only:** 98% graduate employability · Employer's Preferred University (Talentbank) · 100+ industry partners · MQA-accredited · 100% online. No job guarantees. Keep the ~685k AI stat hedged ("Govt estimates").
- **Format:** hook in first ~125 chars · headline ≤40 · one CTA per ad.
