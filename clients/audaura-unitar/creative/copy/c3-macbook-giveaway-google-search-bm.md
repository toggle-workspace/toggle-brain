## Read before launch

**1. The BM landing page does not show the offer.** As of 16 August 2026, `laman-utama` carries three carousel slides (Ijazah Sarjana Muda Pendidikan Nombor 1 di Malaysia, 98% Kebolehpasaran Graduan, Bangkit Lagi Tinggi Bersama UNITAR) and no giveaway banner. The English homepage does carry one. Google requires that an advertised offer be findable on the landing page, so ask UNITAR to add the BM banner to `laman-utama`, or point these ads at `/c3-open-day/` instead. Until one of those happens, keep the giveaway headlines unpinned so the brand headlines carry most impressions.

**2. Do not state a registration fee amount.** The English homepage banner implies RM100 qualifies you. The contest terms require the registration fee plus administration fees plus first semester tuition. The two do not agree, so no fee number appears in any line below. Get the correct figure from Nikki before anyone adds one.

**3. Never promise a win.** Every giveaway line uses "peluang" or "berpeluang" (chance to win). Nothing below says a registration wins a laptop.

**4. Three RSAs, each with fallback swaps, because of the Apple trademark.** Google applies Apple's trademark restrictions to ad text and UNITAR is not an authorized reseller, so any asset naming MacBook carries disapproval risk. Every RSA below ships with a small list of replacement assets that say the same thing without the brand name. A disapproval is then a two or three asset swap, not an ad rebuild.

**5. Run all three in the same ad group.** Google caps an ad group at three RSAs, so this fills the slot. RSA 1 leads with brand plus offer, RSA 2 pushes the deadline and the Monday draw, RSA 3 argues credibility with the prize as a bonus. Keep ad rotation on Optimize and give it two full weeks before reading anything into the split. RSA 3 is the one to keep running after the campaign closes in October, since only three headlines and one description mention the prize.

# Notes for the person building this

Pin one of headlines 1, 2 or 3 to position 1 so brand searches always see a brand line first. Leave positions 2 and 3 unpinned so Google can rotate the giveaway and the program credibility lines against each ad group's keywords.

Keep the giveaway headlines to a minority of the pinned slots until the BM landing page shows the offer. Once it does, promote headline 4 or 5 into a position 2 pin and measure the CTR change against the current brand baseline.

Every ad that mentions the giveaway needs Description 2 eligible to serve, because it carries the "terma dan syarat dikenakan" disclaimer. Do not pin all four descriptions in a way that squeezes it out.

The prize is non-transferable, non-exchangeable and carries no cash equivalent, so never write a line implying a winner can take the value in cash or pass the device to someone else.
