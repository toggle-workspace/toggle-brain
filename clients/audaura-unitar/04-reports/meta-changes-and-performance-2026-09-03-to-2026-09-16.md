## 4. Flags

1. **The English Macbook Neo ad set spent MYR 24,228 and got 0 qualified leads.** All 29 of the campaign's leads came from the BM ad set (MYR 887 each). The 22 new batch5 ads from 11–13 Sep all went into the English ad set.
2. **The Generic Awareness reach burst cost MYR 34,168 in about 3 days** (budget raised 4 Sep, paused 6 Sep). It reached 6.94M people at MYR 4.92 per 1,000 reached. Worth checking this was a planned Merdeka push.
3. **C3 Retargeting still has a MYR 11,882 daily budget.** All MYR 32,600 of its spend landed between 3 and 6 Sep (it matches its 31 Aug – 6 Sep total exactly), and it has spent MYR 0 since, although it is still ACTIVE. Cost per conversation rose to MYR 66.94 from MYR 19.20, and frequency is 4.68. The budget is a risk: if delivery restarts, the campaign could spend close to that amount in a single day. *(Corrected 17 Sep. The first version said the campaign averaged about MYR 2,329 a day.)*
4. **Rising frequency and costs on C1 Retargeting:** frequency 4.46, cost per lead MYR 1,156, the most expensive after Macbook Neo.
5. **C2 UC city ad sets:** Melaka spent MYR 3,857 for 1 lead (frequency 4.81), and Kuching spent MYR 2,676 for 2 leads (frequency 4.75).
6. **C2 MED spent MYR 11,354 with no qualified-lead data in either period.** Either the lead tracking isn't recording anything, or it's set up differently. Its "List LALs" ad set is also flagged by Meta for a very small audience (about 1,000 people).
7. **C3 SPTM Result Focus is active with a MYR 400 daily budget but spent MYR 0 this period.**
8. **Delivery errors:**
   - 91 ads in paused campaigns hit "ad limit reached" for the Page. So did 1 ad in the active C1 Scale Up Prospecting campaign.
   - 1 ad in TOF Reach is blocked because its WhatsApp number is invalid.
   - One ad set and one ad in the active C1 SPM Focus Retargeting campaign are showing "not delivering".
