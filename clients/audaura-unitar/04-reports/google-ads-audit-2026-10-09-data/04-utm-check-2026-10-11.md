Source: Expanded landing pages report, 1 to 11 Oct 2026 (the page would not hold "Last 30 days"), top 68 served URLs by clicks (MYR 94,361 of spend in range). Read-only. Covers 27 of the 69 campaigns that spent in the last 30 days; these 27 carry the large majority of spend.

## Template actually served (all 27 campaigns seen)
`{lpurl}?utm_source=online&utm_medium=sem&utm_campaign={campaignid}&utm_content={creative}&utm_term={keyword}+{matchtype}`

This differs from the account-level tracking template (`...utm_medium=SEM&utm_campaign={campaignid}&utm_term={keyword}`, no utm_content), so live campaigns carry an override at campaign or ad group level. Final URL suffix is empty at account level.

## Other issues
- `utm_term` carries the match type glued on with "+" (`upu online+p`, `unitar+e`), so GA4/Salesforce see "upu online p".
- Stray "&" before "#" in some Psychology and Area of study URLs (`gad_campaignid=…&#diploma`): the final URL itself ends in "/?&#…" or similar.
- Aug-26 campaigns logged 1,892 clicks at ~MYR 0.10 with empty utm_term on 1 to 10 Oct: Display traffic before Display was switched off on 10 Oct.
- utm_campaign is the numeric campaign ID; readable only with a lookup.

## Not covered
