## 2. Central College Penang (RC Lead Generation)

- Central College Penang is an RC partner, not a UC, located in Georgetown
- No program overlap with UC: RC carries UIU bachelor programs while UC stops at college level
- Problem statement: RC is getting no enrollments and wants dedicated lead generation
- Concern raised: ads would run under the UNITAR page but land on the Central College Penang site, which creates a brand disconnect
  - Suggested fix: flip it so ads run under UNITAR but clearly mention Central College Penang
- Proposed approach is a separate campaign with a dedicated budget, state-level targeting, and a pre-filled lead form (Penang campus, relevant programs only)
  - Higher effort, so the team recommends revisiting it after the overall retargeting plan is finalized
- Enrollments through RC partners do count as NE in Salesforce

- Agreed CTA angle: "Send us a message for a chance to win a MacBook Neo"

  - Redundant pipeline stages need to be skipped and the leads flagged as highest priority

## 4. Chatbot as Alternative or Complementary Channel

- Proposal: allocate part of the retargeting budget to AI chatbots instead of ads
  - Covers Facebook, WhatsApp, TikTok, and Instagram
  - Intelligent rather than templated, and handles Malay and English automatically
  - Would also reduce sales team load on initial replies
- Rationale: appointment rate is a lagging indicator for NE, and a chatbot nurtures closer to conversion
- Suggested target shift: reverse-calculate the appointments needed to hit 2,800 NE, then optimize to appointments
- Next step: finalize the retargeting pool number first, then revisit the chatbot allocation
