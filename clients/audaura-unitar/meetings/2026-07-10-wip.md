# UNITAR WIP · 10 July 2026

_Source: Granola meeting notes._

## 1. Landing Page Critique and Fixes

- Current pages are product pages, not insight-driven landing pages
  - No messaging around job loss, career transition, or emotional hook
  - Too much copy; nobody reads at this length
- Required format is a simple lead-gen page
  - Form at the top, four bullet points on the side
  - Apply button visible above the fold and repeated throughout
- Lead form sits too low and needs to move up immediately
- Sticky "Apply Now" banner recommended for mobile scrollers
- Team reviews on desktop, but traffic may skew mobile
- Landing page must mirror the creative's angle to perform

---

## 2. Payday Hypothesis

- Hypothesis: lead intent spikes from the 25th of the month through the 5th of the following month
  - Analogous to gym membership sales at month-end
  - Messaging angle: "Got your pay? Invest in yourself"
- Test: pull enrollment and lead data by date, plot a histogram, check for a 65/35 or stronger distribution
- Caveat: education is a considered purchase, not an impulse buy, so the data comes before any action
- If the pattern holds, shift messaging and creative to target the payday window

---

## 3. Pakistan Campaign: Lead Quality and Messaging

- 82% of leads are uncontactable via WhatsApp
  - Numbers appear valid on WhatsApp, so the issue is non-response, not fake numbers
  - OTP will not fix this because people are not replying
- Scholarship expectation: most contactable leads expect a 100% scholarship
  - Fix: add "up to 20% scholarship available" to the ad headline as both a filter and a hook
- "100% online" is not prominent enough and is causing confusion about in-person attendance
  - Upgrade it to the primary headline or add a sticker on existing ads
- Consider adding a short text field ("why do you want to apply?") as a friction filter
- A Pakistan-based outbound number was suggested to improve answer rates
- UTM tracking is now live, so ad angle performance is trackable

## 4. TikTok Creator Program

- Upgraded from Essential to Classic tier

  - MBA submitted 18 June; only one video came back and it was disabled

## 5. Website Traffic Analysis

- Jan to Mar 2026 dip (~300k sessions vs 600k to 700k in prior years) is likely a tracking or tagging issue
  - Traffic normalized from April onwards; Apr to Jun 2026 is comparable to 2024
- Apr to Jun 2025 was double 2024 because of Pmax, the car giveaway, and the gold giveaway campaigns
- Paused CTW and Pmax campaigns account for roughly 15% of this year's traffic gap
  - The remaining 85% is attributable to reduced above-the-line spend
- Organic decline is partly explained by on-platform conversions on Meta, Google, and TikTok
  - Fewer people click through to the website because leads are captured natively
