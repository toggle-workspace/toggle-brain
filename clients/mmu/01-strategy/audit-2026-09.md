# MMU: what is good and what is bad

They run 56 live Facebook and Instagram ads in English and Malay.

## What is good

**Their tracking is the best of the five prospects.** They have two Meta pixels, a TikTok pixel and a Google Ads tag. A pixel is a small piece of code on a website that reports back what visitors did. Theirs report twelve different actions, including form submits and brochure downloads.

**Their website is fast.** The homepage loads in about five seconds and the Apply Now page in under one. Nobody is dropping off because of speed.

## What is bad

**Fifty six ads, but only four different ideas.** Every ad is a version of one of four messages, so roughly thirteen near copies of each. They all compete in the same auction, which means MMU is bidding against itself and each ad learns from a thirteenth of the results it could have had.

**No ad says what you would actually study.** Not one names a faculty, a course, a campus or an intake month, even though their website is organized exactly that way. It is like a restaurant advertising "great food" and never showing the menu.

**They are the only university in the auction not naming a number.** Competitors say "50% off", "FREE degree", "October intake now open". Of MMU's fifty headlines, zero name a price, a discount, a deadline or a date. Their scholarship ads run on a fifth of their budget without ever saying how much the scholarship is worth.

## Careful

Do not say they cannot track their leads. They can, and they will show you the twelve events. The narrower point is that their lead actions are recorded as custom events rather than Meta's standard "Lead" event, so ask which one their campaigns actually optimize toward.
