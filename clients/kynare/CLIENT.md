## Capacity, which is the actual constraint

- Client's stated target is **20 active clients**. At 2 sessions per week that consumes 40 of the 130 available client-hours, roughly 31% utilization.
- The theoretical ceiling is 65 clients (130 hours divided by 2). The client's own estimate of 50 to 65 is consistent. In practice it is lower, because unbilled walk-in assessments draw from the same pool and 130 hours already assumes each of three people bills about 43 hours a week.
- Holding 20 active clients on a six-week cycle needs about **3.3 sign-ups a week** in steady state. Ten sign-ups a week would build to roughly 60 active.
- Ad economics are not the constraint. Moving from 20 to about 60 active clients is roughly RM92,000 a month of additional package revenue for roughly RM4,500 a month of additional media. **This is the strongest commercial argument on the account.**

## Open items with the client

1. **Is "10 new clients a week" a lead target or a sign-up target?** Ten sign-ups a week builds to about 60 active, three times the stated 20 target. This changes the budget materially.
2. **Is 20 active the goal or the floor?** See the capacity section. Filling to 50 or 60 still works at roughly RM7,000 to RM10,000 a month of media.
3. **The three-month contract contradicts the six-week cycle.** A 12-session package is six weeks. If clients genuinely stay 12 weeks, holding 20 active needs only 1.7 sign-ups a week, and ten a week would imply 120 active, roughly double the ceiling. Either repurchase is a hidden LTV upside nobody has counted, or the contract is nominal.
4. **Nobody has the show-up rate.** With an education-first model, the percentage of booked assessments who actually walk in governs everything. Ten leads a week at a 50% show rate and an 80% close rate is four sign-ups a week, not ten.
5. **WhatsApp number** for the `wa.me` link in the Meta form ending.
6. **One real client quote** with a first name and a rough timeframe, or a Google review count. There is currently no usable social proof (see Notes).
7. **Privacy policy page.** Both lead forms point at `https://www.kynare.com/contact-us`, which is a contact page.
8. **Mandarin.** Ads run in English and Bahasa Malaysia only. Worth testing for Mont Kiara, but not confirmed with the client.
