**English**

> Hi! Thanks for messaging Kynare. "[exact client quote]" is how [First name, age] described it after [number] weeks with us. Every client starts the same way, with a one to one assessment.

**Bahasa Malaysia**

> Hai! Terima kasih kerana menghubungi Kynare. "[petikan klien]" itu kata [Nama, umur] selepas [bilangan] minggu bersama kami. Setiap klien bermula dengan cara yang sama, iaitu sesi pemeriksaan satu ke satu.

## Blockers on these forms

1. **The privacy policy field on both platforms points to `https://www.kynare.com/contact-us`,** which is a contact page and not a privacy policy. Meta and TikTok both require a genuine privacy policy URL, and this is a common rejection reason. The page needs to exist before the forms go for review.
2. **The WhatsApp number for the Meta `wa.me` link is still outstanding.**
3. **Confirm `kynare.com/class` is the right landing spot** for someone who just asked about the PT Hybrid program, since every TikTok lead is sent there after submitting.
4. **Keep ad creative clear of second-person health assertions.** Meta's personal attributes policy blocks ads implying we know the reader's condition. The form copy above is deliberately conditional and general. This matters more in the ad creative than in the form.
