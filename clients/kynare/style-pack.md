# Kynare — Style Pack

> Base: `prompts/style-packs/toggle-default.md` and `brain/voice/writing-standards.md`. This file overrides only what differs, and adds the compliance rules that are specific to advertising a private healthcare clinic in Malaysia.

## Voice

- Calm, plain and explanatory. The client's whole model is education-first, so the copy should sound like the clinic explaining something, not like a gym selling a package.
- The audience skews older and is usually in some kind of recurring pain. Write to a reader who has already tried something that did not hold.
- Lead with the assessment, not the program. The thing we are asking for is a walk-in, and the walk-in is where the client closes.
- Concrete over clinical. "You are not back in the same chair in six months" beats "improved long-term outcomes".
- Languages: English and Bahasa Malaysia. Malay copy should read native Malaysian, with natural code-switching (keep "personal training" in English rather than forcing "latihan peribadi"). Mandarin is unconfirmed and worth raising for Mont Kiara.

## The positioning term (client-directed, 2026-08-12)

**"Human Performance Medicine"** is the category term the client wants to own. Use it as the headline and define it immediately underneath, because no reader arrives knowing what it means.

The definition to keep reaching for, which is grounded in their actual service list rather than a slogan:

> Human Performance Medicine looks at the whole system behind the pain. It brings together physical therapy, strength and movement calibration, and diagnostics such as blood and biomarker analysis.

Keep the term in English on Malay surfaces. Translating it ("Perubatan Prestasi Manusia") reads clumsy and breaks it as a brand term.

**Do not write "physio and personal training under one roof" or any variant of it.** The client rejected this framing on 2026-08-12 on the grounds that plenty of clinics claim it and deliver poorly, so it reads as a commodity claim rather than a differentiator. This retires the earlier Toggle positioning line.

Two cautions on the term, raised with the client on 2026-08-12:

1. **"Medicine" is a heavier word than "physio and PT" from a compliance angle.** The PT Hybrid program is delivered by physiotherapists and personal trainers, and the doctor does not join until November 2026. Advertising a program as Medicine before a doctor is in place invites scrutiny under Malaysian private healthcare advertising rules, and platform reviewers flag medical language on health accounts. The exposure drops once the doctor arm is live.
2. **The term is safest post-click.** A lead form headline is read by someone who already tapped the ad, so an unfamiliar category name costs little. Using it as the ad hook is a different bet, since nobody is scrolling or searching for it. Keep ad creative in plain problem language and let the form carry the brand term.

## Compliance (binding, do not waive)

1. **No cure, guarantee, permanent, fix, or pain-free claims.** Private healthcare advertising in Malaysia is more restricted than ordinary commercial advertising, and the exposure rises sharply once the doctor arm launches in November with treatments and bone density screening. Verify the client's own licensing position before any doctor-arm creative.
2. **No second-person health assertions.** Meta's personal attributes policy blocks ads that imply we know the reader's condition. "Struggling with knee pain?" gets rejected. Keep phrasing conditional and general: "If knee pain keeps coming back after a long day" rather than "You have knee pain".
3. **No invented proof.** There is no verified testimonial, patient count, review count or outcome statistic for Kynare as of 2026-08-12. Do not write one to make a line land. Ask the client, or use the structural truths instead.
4. **No before-and-after body imagery** implying a medical outcome.

## Locked strings

Service names must appear exactly as the client wrote them in the lead forms:

- `KYNARE Outset (First Timer)`
- `Modern Physical Therapy ~ (Single Session)`
- `Strength & Movement Calibration ~ (Single Session)`
- `Integrative IV Formulations`
- `Blood Analysis & Biomarker Insights`

Stated operating hours, used in both form endings: **Monday to Sunday, 8am to 7pm.**

## Platform tone

- **Meta:** interest-targeted, so the reader has some fitness or physio affinity already. Copy can go straight to the offer.
- **TikTok:** broad targeting and Spark Ads, so the reader arrives cold and may not know what Kynare is. Establish what the clinic is and where it sits before the offer.
- **WhatsApp:** conversational. The greeting carries one value sentence and then asks for the four details in a single message.

## Destinations, which are deliberately different

- Meta form ending routes to **WhatsApp**.
- TikTok form ending routes to **`kynare.com/class`**.

Do not sync these.
