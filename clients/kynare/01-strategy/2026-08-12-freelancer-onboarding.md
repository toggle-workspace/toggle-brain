## Kynare — Client Background

- A hybrid physiotherapy and personal training clinic with two locations, PJ Section 13 and Mont Kiara. Website is kynare.com. Operating hours are Monday to Sunday, 8am to 7pm.
  - Small team of 3 practitioners (physiotherapists and personal trainers) managing all clients.
  - Ad accounts are named "Kynare Ecosystems" on Meta and "KYNARE WELLNESS & PERFORMANCE" on TikTok.
- Unique Selling Point (USP): physiotherapy and personal training under one roof. Physiotherapy settles the pain, then the trainers rebuild strength around it so the same problem is less likely to return. A standard physio clinic discharges you at that point and a standard gym does not understand the injury.
- Business model is education-first. Every client comes in for a walk-in assessment where the team explains what is causing the problem before selling anything. The client closes reliably once someone walks in, so the job of our ads is booking assessments, not selling packages.
- Core services (these are the exact options in the lead gen forms):
  - KYNARE Outset (First Timer), the entry assessment, and the main thing we are driving
  - Modern Physical Therapy (Single Session)
  - Strength & Movement Calibration (Single Session)
  - Integrative IV Formulations
  - Blood Analysis & Biomarker Insights
- Client economics, for context on why lead quality matters more than lead volume:
  - RM250 one-time sign-up fee
  - 12-session package at RM205 per session, which is RM2,460
  - Around 20% of clients take a 24-session package
  - Cadence is 2 sessions per week for 6 weeks, on a 3-month contract
  - Blended lifetime value is roughly RM2,950 on packages, closer to RM3,200 including the sign-up fee
- Strategic context: the constraint is clinic capacity, not ad economics. CAC of RM180 to RM250 against a blended LTV near RM3,000 is a 12x to 16x return, so almost any reasonable CPL works. The client currently targets 20 active clients, which uses only about a third of their 130 available client-hours per week. Their real ceiling is closer to 50 to 65 clients.
- Two arms are launching. The PT Hybrid program is live now and is the only thing we are advertising today. A doctor arm launches in November, with an in-house doctor doing treatments, referring into the hybrid PTs, and adding services like bone density screening. That will need its own campaign and its own budget.
- Demographic currently skews older, which shapes both targeting and creative.

## Kynare — Client Background (continued)

Reporting cost per lead alone is not enough for this account, because the client's economics only make sense once we know the show-up rate. ## Kynare — Marketing Objective

- We will run Meta Ads and TikTok Ads for the client. Toggle is creating all the ad creatives.
  - TikTok runs as Spark Ads, so we need the client to authorize their organic posts and send us the authorization codes.
  - Google Ads currently has zero budget allocated. Flagging it because their older, pain-led demographic is exactly who searches for knee pain, back pain, and post-surgery rehab, so this is worth revisiting with Jordan.
- Marketing objective: fill clinic capacity for the PT Hybrid program, first hitting and holding 20 active clients, then pushing toward the real ceiling.
- Ads objective:
  - Acquire leads who convert into a walk-in KYNARE Outset assessment, via Meta Instant Lead Gen Forms and TikTok Instant Lead Gen Forms.
  - Acquire WhatsApp conversations via Meta Message Conversations campaigns, where the team collects details and books the appointment in chat.
- Target location: PJ Section 13 and Mont Kiara. Both sit inside a single location field per campaign, so we cannot read cost per location from the platform. Keep the preferred-location question on the forms so we can attribute manually.

## Kynare — Marketing Objective (continued)

- Ad channel budget: Total RM4,500 per month
  - Meta: RM3,000 per month
    - Instant Lead Gen Form campaign: RM2,000 per month
    - WhatsApp Message Conversations campaign: RM1,000 per month
  - TikTok: RM1,500 per month
    - Instant Lead Gen Form campaign, run as Spark Ads
  - Google Search Ads: RM0 currently
  - Committed plan runs August to December 2026, which is RM22,500 total.
- Target audience:
  - Age 18 to 55+, both genders, prospecting only.
  - Meta uses an interest stack around personal training, physiotherapy, fitness services, Physio Pilates, exercise physiology, and fitness and wellness.
  - TikTok runs broad with no interest targeting.
  - Exclusions on all campaigns: existing followers, page engagers, video watchers, current purchasers.
  - Note that the client base skews older while our floor is set at 18. Report by age band from week one so we can cut the young end on evidence rather than assumption.

- Ad campaign duration: always-on from August 2026. Expect a November revision when the doctor arm launches, which the client modeled at an extra RM3,000 to RM5,000 per month as a separate campaign.
