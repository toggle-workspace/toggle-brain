## Standing constraint that overrides everything below

Any public-facing creative for the end client clears **Malaysian Dental Council** guidelines
before it runs, and the practice's dentist signs off. Malaysian regulators set this limit on
what a dental practice may publish, so it overrides any style preference. See
`01-strategy/kreloses-assessment-2026-07-29.md` for the detail.

Off the table until the dentist approves each one in writing:

- Patient testimonials that read as clinical endorsement
- Superlative claims such as best, cheapest, number one, or leading
- Any guarantee of a clinical outcome, including implied guarantees in before and after pairs
- Comparative claims against a named competitor clinic
- Patient solicitation, which is the most common failure in this category

Veneers is elective aesthetic dentistry, so this account runs under tighter creative limits
than a normal e-commerce or education client. Angles that work inside the rules cover the
process, the dentist's credentials, the materials, aftercare, and honest pricing.

## Examples of "yes / no"
