What we still need to check

Everything we know so far came to us second hand, so we are not going to present it back to you as fact. Three questions decide the whole shape of this, and only you can answer them.

What exactly fails on uploads?

Is it file size, the number of files per patient, total storage, or file types it refuses? For a veneers practice your photos and scans are the case record, so this is the question that decides whether you should change systems at all.

What does your Kreloses agreement allow?

Before we automate anything against your account we want to read your subscription terms and confirm what automated access they permit. We are not going to put your live clinic system at risk to save you a manual step.

Where do enquiries land today?

Who answers them, how fast, and what happens to the ones that do not book. This tells us whether your problem is reach, response, or follow-up, and those need three different fixes.

The method

How we get your data out

Two routes, and neither one needs Kreloses to build us anything or to give us permission. Both run on access you already own.

Route 1
The export they already offer

You request your complete database, which their pricing page says costs nothing and carries no restrictions. It arrives as raw files with internal codes and no labels, and we turn that into something usable. Slower to refresh, and it is the sanctioned route, in writing.

Route 2
Your own login, on a schedule

Every time your staff open a screen, Kreloses loads that information as structured data behind the page. Using your own account we can collect the same information on a schedule, which gives us appointments and recall dates without waiting on an export.

We confirm which route works on your live account in week one, before anything is built. We also read your subscription agreement first, because we are not going to automate against your clinic system in a way its own terms do not allow. If Route 2 is off limits, Route 1 still carries most of what the funnel needs.

12 · The Broken Loop — Belief: "This is the insight I am paying for."
[ toggle.solutions ]

Nobody buys veneers in a chat window

This is why ad money leaks on a practice like yours. Google and Meta only learn from what they can see, and right now they cannot see the part that matters.

01Someone enquiresMeta sees this and records a win.
02They book a consultMeta sees nothing.
03They attendMeta sees nothing.
04They accept a caseMeta sees nothing. This is the only step worth money.
What that costs you

Because the only signal Meta gets is the enquiry, it goes and finds you more enquiries. Cheap ones. It learns nothing about which ad produced a patient who sat in your chair and said yes to a full set of veneers, so it cannot go find more of those.

What we do about it

We read attendance and case value out of Kreloses and feed them back to Google and Meta as the real result. The platforms stop chasing cheap enquiries and start chasing accepted cases. On a practice where one case is worth many times a routine visit, that changes what the same budget buys.

14 · Two Rules We Design Around — Belief: "They know my industry, not just marketing."
[ toggle.solutions ]

Two rules we design around from day one

Neither one blocks the work. Both cost far less to build in now than to unpick after a complaint.

Malaysian Dental Council advertising rules

Dental advertising is restricted, and elective aesthetic work like veneers is the category watched hardest. Patient testimonials that read as endorsement, superlative claims, guaranteed outcomes and patient solicitation are all limited.

Published research on Malaysian dental clinic social accounts found around 15 percent of posts non-compliant, and more than half of the accounts studied carried at least one. Every piece of creative goes to your dentist for sign-off before it runs.

PDPA and your patients' data

Malaysian law treats health information as sensitive personal data, which needs explicit consent to process, and patient data cannot be used to market services without it. The 2024 amendments added mandatory breach reporting and higher penalties.

In practice: the chatbot asks for consent before it collects anything, and we only message your existing patients if they agreed to marketing contact. Consenting to treatment does not cover promotions.

We also design the ad measurement so that attendance is reported back against an advertising click reference rather than against a named patient, which keeps identifiable health information out of the ad platforms entirely.
