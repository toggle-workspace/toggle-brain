## Open questions (blocking a costed plan)
1. Enrollment target for the intake, and the deadline.
2. Media budget for the intake, and whether it is monthly or front-loaded.
3. Last intake's spend, leads and enrollments.
4. Admissions calling capacity per week. This sets the channel mix.
5. Whether the end client is recruiting domestic students, international students, or both.
