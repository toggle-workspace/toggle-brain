# Intake thread with Harsha, 28 to 29 July 2026

Transcribed from WhatsApp screenshots. This is the origin of the account.

## What happened

Harsha opened the thread at 20:29 saying he has someone working with a dental practice that
runs on Kreloses. They want to possibly replace it because it has no API, which is making it
hard to automate their funnel. He had already spoken to Viknesh, who pointed him to Zaid as
the right person for the technical side.

Zaid replied at 22:04 saying Toggle can either suggest another option or automate the scope
in house, and asked four clarifying questions: what the funnel is currently, what Kreloses
scope covers, what they want to automate and up to what point in the funnel, and what the
budget is.

Harsha answered at 22:22 with a long message covering the practice and the software. Of the
four questions, he answered only the scope one. The funnel, the automation boundary, and the
budget are all still open.

Zaid replied the next morning at 06:58 that it is a large scope and he would research it.

## What Harsha told us

**The practice.** A dental practice specializing in veneers. Because veneer cases run a
year or longer, patients return on a schedule and the practice depends on patient records
staying current.

**His read on Kreloses.** He understood it as software aimed at vets, chiropractors,
physiotherapists, and general practitioners. Research since has shown Kreloses sells a
dedicated dental edition, so this part of his understanding was incomplete.

**Kreloses scope, as the practice described it.** Harsha noted he suspects there is more
than this list.

- Calendar and appointment scheduling
- Events
- Creating invoices, raising sales, exporting invoices
- Updating patient notes
- Generating letters including MCs, timeslips, and instruction letters
- Monthly sales reports
- Dental charting
- Generating and sending messages for birthdays, recalls, and promotions
- Uploading patient data such as consent forms and other documents
- Uploading monthly expenses and payments
- Monthly and annual sales lists
- Patients, expenses, payments, salary payments

His summary was that it covers almost everything a clinic needs.

**The six gaps he listed.**

1. Extracting data from the system is difficult, which makes switching software tricky
2. No API, so no automated funnel with other tools
3. Limited patient data upload per patient, he believes images are included in this
4. No app
5. Cannot run broadcast campaigns
6. No loyalty programs

**The three that are live pain.** Numbers 1, 2, and 3.

**What the practice wants.** To automate a funnel by running ads, having spent about
RM6,000 on ads previously, and to have a chatbot of their own. Harsha raised the concern
himself that no chatbot would be able to connect to Kreloses.

## Contradictions and gaps found in research

- Gap 1 is contradicted by the Kreloses pricing page, which advertises unrestricted free
  export of the complete database.
- Gap 5 is doubtful, because the practice's own scope list includes generating and sending
  messages for recalls and promotions, and Kreloses sells SMS credits as an add-on.
- Kreloses is a dental product, not a general clinic tool the practice adapted.
- Nobody has asked Kreloses whether a partner API exists.
