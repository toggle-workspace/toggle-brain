## Notes
- **Dental advertising is regulated here.** The Malaysian Dental Council restricts dental
  advertising, and veneers marketing sits in the highest-risk category. Patient
  testimonials, superlative claims, guaranteed outcomes, and patient solicitation are all
  constrained. Published research found roughly 15 percent of Malaysian dental clinic
  social posts non-compliant. Any creative Toggle produces for this account clears MDC
  guidelines before it runs. Details in `01-strategy/kreloses-assessment-2026-07-29.md`.
- **Patient data is sensitive personal data under the PDPA.** The practice needs explicit
  consent to process it, and it cannot market healthcare services off patient data without
  that consent. This constrains the broadcast and retargeting ideas the practice raised,
  and it means the chatbot needs a consent step before it collects anything.
- **Toggle advises on the system and does not own the migration.** Moving a veneers practice
  off its patient record system puts patient records at risk, and Toggle should not carry
  that exposure on an account it reaches through a partner. The assessment splits the work
  into two tracks.
- `brain/verticals/healthcare.md` is still a placeholder. If this account converts, the
  MDC and PDPA rules belong in that file through a separate PR against `brain/`.
