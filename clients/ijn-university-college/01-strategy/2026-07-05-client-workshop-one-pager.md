# Reading the Machine: A 90-Minute Campaign Workshop for the IJNUC Team

**Prepared by Toggle Solutions for IJN University College · July 2026**

---

Your TikTok campaign sends you numbers every week — spend, RSVPs, cost per lead, show rates. Numbers only create value when the whole team reads them the same way. This workshop gets your marketing, admissions, and open-day teams fluent in your own campaign, so every decision between now and the 31 July registration deadline is made faster and with confidence.

This is not a marketing theory session. Every example, every metric, and every ad we discuss is yours.

---

## What we'll cover

**1 · The enrolment funnel — in your numbers (20 min)**
Ad spend → RSVPs → open-day arrivals → applications → enrolments. We walk through your previous campaign end to end: 205 RSVPs became 120 arrivals, 38 applications, and 70 enrolled students at roughly RM13k total spend. You'll see exactly what one enrolled student costs to acquire — and where in the funnel the biggest gains are hiding.

**2 · The metrics that matter — and who moves each one (25 min)**
Cost per lead, show rate, conversion rate, spend pacing. For each metric: what it means, what "good" looks like for IJNUC, and which team moves it. Some levers sit with Toggle (targeting, creative testing, budget allocation). Some sit with your team — the day-before reminder message alone is the cheapest enrolment lever in the entire campaign. We'll map ownership clearly so nothing falls between teams.

**3 · Why the ads look the way they do (25 min)**
We'll screen the actual creator videos running for IJNUC next to a typical corporate education ad, and show the data behind every choice: why 16–22-year-olds trust a student in scrubs over a narrator, why the first 2 seconds decide everything, why we rotate four creative angles instead of one, and why compliance rules (MQA codes, no salary claims) protect your brand rather than slow it down.

**4 · How to read your weekly report (20 min)**
We read a real weekly report together. What a good week looks like, what a normal soft week looks like, and what a soft week does *not* mean. We'll close by agreeing on shared traffic-light thresholds — so everyone knows, at a glance, when things are on track and when it's time to talk.

## What your team walks away with

- **A one-page glossary** of every term in our reports, in plain language
- **A funnel ownership map** — Toggle's responsibilities and IJNUC's, side by side
- **Agreed performance thresholds** — green / yellow / red, decided together in the room
- A team that reads Monday's report in two minutes, not twenty

Marketing and admissions team, the open-day / events team, and at least one decision-maker. The open-day team's role in show rate is a core part of the session — their presence matters as much as marketing's.
