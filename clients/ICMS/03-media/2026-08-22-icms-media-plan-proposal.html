Two weeks of fixing before we spend big

Every plan starts the same way. Nothing scales until an enquiry can be traced from click to inbox.

WEEK 1
Make the counter work
  • Add the line of code that tells Google someone finished your form
  • Send people to a short thank you page after they send it, so Facebook and Google both see it
  • Delete the three broken settings and the one that counts page views
  • Stop counting map taps on your Google business listing as enquiries
WEEK 2
Give the click somewhere to land
  • Fix the Apply Now link, which has pointed at a February 2026 seminar page for six months
  • Build one Malay page per main course, with the form near the top
  • Add a WhatsApp button so people can message instead of filling a form
  • Agree one list of intake dates, because your site and your ads give five different answers
WEEK 3 ONWARD
Then the spending starts
  • Full budget goes live once we have watched one test enquiry reach your inbox
  • Week 6 review against the cost per lead limits in this deck
  • Anything 50 percent over its limit gets switched off
Weeks 1 and 2 run at a quarter of whichever budget you pick.

Three platforms, three different jobs

Google Search is the smallest line in every plan. It only reaches people already searching. TikTok and Facebook reach people before they start looking.

Meta  Facebook and Instagram
Its job: steady enquiries at a predictable price, plus repeat ads to people who already visited.
  • In-app forms with two questions that filter out time wasters
  • A button that opens WhatsApp instead of a form
  • The only platform where you already know your cost per enquiry
TikTok
Its job: reach school leavers and football fans before they search, and fix how ICMS looks while doing it.
  • Where 17 to 22 year olds spend their time
  • Football and campus clips suit the app, so production stays cheap
  • Doubles as the branding repair, paid for as advertising
Google Search
Its job: catch people typing your course name, protect your own name, and appear against Unikop.
  • Closest to signing up, so more of them enrol
  • Also the most expensive, about RM3.96 a click today
  • Volume is capped by how many people search, so a bigger budget buys back the vague searches that already failed
Your account was shown 10,996 times in 30 days across 80 search words. That is the ceiling we priced to.

What we need from you

Six things. The first three unblock the advertising. The last three are not billable and matter more.

1
Full access to the accounts
Editing rights on Google Ads, plus your Facebook and TikTok business accounts. We hold view-only access from 18 August and cannot change a setting.
2
Half a day of your web developer
To add the code that tells Google someone finished your form, and to build a thank you page. Without it every number here is guesswork.
3
One list of intake dates
Your course pages say January, May and September. Your Apply Now page sells a March 2026 intake. Your ads say July and August. You told us it is monthly. We need one answer before any ad runs.
4
Permission to publish your fees
RM300 to register against Unikop's RM1,900. It appears nowhere on your site or in any ad.
5
Someone who owns the enquiries
Enquiries land in a shared spreadsheet with no tracking and no call-back rule. Plan C means about 40 a day. Name the owner and set a call-back time.
6
A WhatsApp business number
Your team said people click WhatsApp and give up on forms. There is no WhatsApp link anywhere on icms.edu.my today.
