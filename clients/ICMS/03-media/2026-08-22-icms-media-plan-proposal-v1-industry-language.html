The cause is one missing line of code. The Google Ads tag loads on every page and never fires a conversion event. The enquiry form submits without leaving the page and no thank you page exists, so a form submission is never reportable. The only thing the tag can report is a page view, which is why one auto created action counted 732 “conversions” while all three lead form actions counted zero.

Two weeks of measurement before any scale

The category default when a college is short of students is spend more on ads  fix the meter, then spend. Every plan in this document begins the same way, and none of them bills you for media you cannot measure.

WEEK 1
Make the meter work
  • Bind a Google Ads conversion event to the form success event, site wide
  • Add a thank you page so GA4 and Meta can read the same signal
  • Delete the three broken conversion actions and the page view action
  • Demote Business Profile taps out of bidding
WEEK 2
Make the click land somewhere
  • Repair the Apply Now link, which has pointed at a February 2026 seminar page for six months
  • Build one Malay landing page per priority programme, form above the fold
  • Add a WhatsApp click to chat route
  • Agree one intake calendar, since the site and the ads currently give five answers
WEEK 3 ONWARD
Then media scales
  • Full budget goes live only once a test lead has been recorded end to end
  • Week 6 read out against the CPL ceilings printed in this document
  • Any channel over its ceiling by 50 percent gets paused, not defended
Weeks 1 and 2 run at 25 percent of the chosen budget.
If we cannot show you a lead in the platform by the end of week 2, we do not scale the spend and you do not pay for the media you did not use.

The message we lead with

Your current ads promise affordable fees, PTPTN and online study. Your website publishes no fee, never mentions PTPTN on the programme page, and lists no online mode. We fix the claim before we buy the click.

THE POSITIONING
Register for RM300, not RM1,900. The only Football Studies diploma in Southeast Asia, and a monthly intake that does not make you wait for next year.
Every clause is checkable. The registration fee is a fact ICMS already owns and currently publishes nowhere. The Football Studies claim is ICMS's own, stated on the homepage. The intake cadence needs one decision from management before it goes live.
TikTok
  • Titans FC training and match footage, shot vertical on a phone, not repurposed from Facebook
  • Student voice, one programme per video, Malay first with code switching
  • Spark Ads on the strongest organic posts before paying for cold creative
  • Six new hooks a month, tested against a 1 percent click through kill rule
Meta
  • Instant forms with a programme question and a financing question, so sales gets a qualified lead

  • Two responsive search ads per ad group instead of the current one

  • The RM300 registration fee in a headline, since no competitor can copy it

How you will know it is working

Reporting is a cadence, not a monthly PDF. These are the numbers we hold ourselves to and the days you see them.

Weekly
Search terms and creative
Negative keywords added, losing creative paused, budget shifted between programmes.
Week 6
First full read out
Actual cost per lead per channel against every ceiling printed on slide 11.
Monthly
Lead to enrolment
The only number that decides whether the media budget is right. Requires ICMS to report closes back to us.
Quarterly
Programme mix review
Which programmes earn their budget, which get cut, which get added.
The kill rules, printed before launch
  • Any channel 50 percent over its CPL ceiling for two consecutive weeks gets paused
  • Any ad below 1 percent click through rate after 5,000 impressions gets replaced
  • Any programme below half the account average close rate loses its budget at the quarterly review
What we will not report
Impressions as a headline, optimisation score, and any conversion count that includes a page view or a Business Profile directions tap. Your account currently reports 732 of those a month as conversions. We will not put that number in a report and call it performance.

What we need from ICMS

Six items. The first three unblock the media. The last three are worth more than the media, and none of them is billable scope.

1
Admin access
Standard or Admin on Google Ads, plus Meta Business Manager and a TikTok Business Center. We currently hold reports only access, granted 18 August, which means we cannot change a single setting.
2
Half a day of developer time
To bind the conversion event to the form success event and add a thank you page. Without this every number in this document is unmeasurable.
3
One intake calendar
Your programme pages say January, May and September. Your Apply Now page sells a March 2026 intake. Your ads say July and August. Management says monthly. We need one answer before any ad goes live.
4
Permission to publish fees
The RM300 registration fee against Unikop's RM1,900 is the strongest checkable claim ICMS owns, and it appears nowhere on the site or in any ad.
5
A lead owner and a response time
Leads currently land in a shared spreadsheet with no CRM and no response SOP. At Plan C volume that is roughly 40 leads a day. Name an owner and commit to a first contact time.
6
A WhatsApp Business number
Your own team said prospects click WhatsApp and abandon forms. There is no WhatsApp link anywhere on icms.edu.my today. This is the cheapest conversion rate gain available to you.
