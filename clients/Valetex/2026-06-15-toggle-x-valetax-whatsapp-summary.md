## 1. Executive summary

**Valetax** — a multi-region forex / CFD trading broker (CopyTrade product; "follow
top-performing traders") — engaged **Toggle Solutions** as its TikTok media agency. The
entire thread is an **urgent onboarding + account-recovery operation**: Valetax cannot run
TikTok ads in its three priority Southeast-Asian markets — **Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand**
— because the underlying TikTok accounts/pages were banned and every attempt to create
replacements is **flagged and blocked immediately**. A separate Vietnam ad group
(`VXculsive VN`) was rejected for **"financial misrepresentation,"** and its appeal was
later rejected too.

Over ~10 days the two teams: established TikTok Business Center partnership and ad-account
access, defined a three-task scope from Valetax's boss, set up a shared tracking sheet and
onboarding checklist, began chasing TikTok's account manager (in China / on leave) and then
a backup "direct support" rep, and delivered a first **ad-account audit** (`Valetax Audit.pptx.pdf`).

The dominant theme is **client urgency and accountability pressure**: Manfred (Valetax's
coordinator) repeatedly asks for daily updates "before 12 noon," insists on fixing the
**accounts/pages first** before touching ad campaigns, wants to meet the TikTok account
manager at least once, and asks Toggle to **double-check work before submitting** — because
he must report cleanly to his boss/CMO. As of the recording, the meeting is being scheduled
and the core MY/TH account-recovery problem is **still open**.

## 4. The core problem (why Toggle was engaged)

1. **Accounts/pages banned in ID, MY, TH.** The Indonesia, Malaysia and Thailand TikTok
   accounts/pages are "gone." Valetax appealed before but couldn't recover them and **doesn't
   know the reason**. When pages go, the associated campaigns stop delivering.
2. **Replacement accounts get auto-blocked.** Per Valetax's boss: attempts to create new TT
   accounts for **Valetax Asia (Malaysia)** and **Valetax Thailand** result in **immediate
   flagging and blocking**. Both countries don't even appear in the TT Business Center.
3. **Business verification incomplete.** Valetax's business is **not verified** in the
   TikTok Business Center — flagged by Manfred as a possible root cause of suspensions. The
   client must self-submit; they need a clear step-by-step guide (Toggle can't submit on
   their behalf).
4. **Active-campaign rejections for compliance.** Several campaigns show "Not delivering /
   review not approved." `VXculsive VN` was rejected for **"Financial misrepresentation"**
   (deceptive/misleading financial claims — "Profit," showing off money). The **appeal was
   also rejected**.
