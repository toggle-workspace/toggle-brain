# Doctor Anywhere — Style Pack

Client voice and visual overrides. Pull the house baseline from `brain/voice/` by reference and only capture the DA-specific deltas here.

## Brand promise
- **Tagline:** "Anywhere, with you." The February 2026 brand manifesto is explicit that this is a system, not a campaign line. It should thread through brand, PR and thought leadership, and CRM as one connected promise.
- **Vision:** to be the largest tech-led healthcare company in Asia that people can trust with their lives.
- **Positioning:** Southeast Asia's most trusted, fast-growth, AI-powered healthcare ecosystem spanning the full care continuum from primary care to specialist, tertiary, step-down, and preventive and chronic care.

## Voice notes
- Write regionally by default and flag market-specific nuance (platform preference, language, regulation, healthcare maturity) only where it matters.
- Keep awareness proxies and business outcomes as separate layers in any copy or reporting language. Do not blend them into a single conversions number.
- Trust is the north star. Copy should earn regulatory, clinician, and patient confidence rather than lean on hype.
