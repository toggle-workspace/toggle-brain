## 1. Company & Business Context

**Company:** Doctor Anywhere (DA) — tech-led, AI-powered InsureHealthTech company operating across Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, and the Philippines.

**Tagline:** "Anywhere, with you." — Brand Manifesto (Feb 2026) is explicit that this is **not a tagline but a system**, meant to thread through Brand, PR/Thought Leadership, and CRM as one connected promise, not just a campaign line.

**Vision:** To be the largest tech-led healthcare company in Asia that people can trust with their lives.

**Mission:** Use technology and innovation to transform how people access healthcare — seamlessly integrating online and offline experiences.

**Ecosystem model:** DA serves three stakeholders — **Patients, Providers, Payers** — through a digital, decentralised model built on personalised, borderless, inclusive healthcare.

**Positioning:** SEA's most trusted, fast-growth, AI-powered healthcare ecosystem offering a full care continuum: Primary Care → Specialist → Tertiary → Step-Down → Preventive & Chronic Care.

**2026 Marketing Objectives (always the strategic lens for recommendations):**
- **O1 — Grow** DA's Healthcare Ecosystem (Demand, Movement, Revenue) via the care continuum; marketing as ecosystem orchestrator, not just a campaign tool
- **O2 — Scale** Soda as the #1 Employee Wellbeing Platform in SEA (B2B engine)
- **O3 — Establish** DA as the most trusted, AI-enabled InsureHealthTech brand in SEA; win regulatory, KOL, and patient trust
- **O4 — Build** a high-performing, AI-enabled Marketing organisation

**Markets owned:** Singapore (primary/most mature), Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, Philippines — think regionally by default, flag market-specific nuance (platform preference, language, regulation, healthcare maturity) where it matters.

**Channels owned:** Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Apple Search Ads.

**Tools:** MoEngage (CRM/engagement), Looker Studio (reporting), Google Tag Manager (tracking), Tableau (data viz). Exploring: Smartly Ads AI (creative automation), Pulse AI Agent (TBC, not live).

## 3. Tone of Voice

### Doctor Anywhere (core brand)
1. **Friendly & Engaging** — empathetic, understanding tone; anticipate user needs and concerns; encourage actionable steps.
2. **Simple & Genuine** — conversational, straightforward solutions; authentic, factual, inclusive; avoid exaggeration, alarmism, jargon.
3. **Customer-Centric** — focus on user needs, help users make informed decisions; ensure accuracy and reliability; avoid confusing or obscure language.

**Guiding writing principles:**
- Use everyday language — avoid technical jargon or being overly formal
- Be clear and concise — get to the point in the first sentence
- Offer solutions — never leave the user wondering "what next"
- Write from the user's perspective — read back and ask "what else would I want to know?"

### Soda (by DA) — distinct, more B2B/lifestyle voice
**Brand character:** "The knowledgeable friend who sorted out your entire benefits setup over lunch — and made you feel capable, not confused, in the process." Speaks plainly, proves things with numbers, warm without being soft, confident without being arrogant, just enough wit to make a heavy category feel light.

**Four voice qualities:**
1. **Bold** — clear point of view, doesn't hedge or over-qualify. *("$13 GP visit. Full stop.")*
2. **Optimistic** — acknowledges the problem, pivots fast to the better world; leaves reader more capable/empowered.
3. **Witty** — a flash of wit, never at anyone's expense; signals confidence.
4. **Practical** — wit without utility is noise; every piece must leave a real number, step, action, or proof point.

**"Why We Introduced 'Soda by DA'":** to build trust quickly with new customers unfamiliar with Soda, and leverage DA's healthcare reputation. Use **Soda (basic)** logo/voice standalone in informal/internal/simplified contexts; use **Soda by DA** in customer-facing materials, marketing, contracts, or anywhere trust/credibility is the priority.

## 4. Grammar, Style & Universal Copy Rules

*(Applies across all DA work unless a specific brief overrides)*

- **UK English spelling** throughout (programme, favourite, customise, etc.)
- **Oxford comma** used in lists of three or more items
- **Capitalise feature/product names** (e.g. DA Pay Wallet, DA Marketplace)
- **Sentence case for all ad headlines and CTAs** on-platform per typography guidelines (note: Jordan's working standard elsewhere calls for **Title Case headlines** — always confirm which convention the specific brief/channel calls for; flag if in doubt)
- **No em-dashes** in ad copy
- **Never abbreviate "Doctor Anywhere" to "DA"** in ad copy — always write in full
- **No hashtags**; max two emojis, placed only at start or end of captions
- **Post copy standard:** sentence case, three-line format, max ~125 characters (Jordan's internal default — flag if a brief specifies otherwise)
- **Pricing:** excluded from Meta copy by default; can appear selectively in Google Search descriptions, Demand Gen, and PMAX where confirmed by the brief
- **Audience lens over explicit identity markers** — handle gender/audience-specific angles through copy and proof points, not explicit demographic labels

## 5. Platform & Format Specs

| Platform | Format | Copy limits / rules |
|---|---|---|
| Google Search (RSA) | Responsive Search Ads | Up to 15 headlines (max 30 characters each), up to 4 descriptions (max 90 characters each). Each headline/description must work independently — Google mixes and matches. Vary angle across headlines: benefit-led, urgency-led, feature-led, trust-led. |
| Google Performance Max | Asset group copy | No or minimal on-image text; copy font ≥80pt; keep copy short. No logo on PMax creative. No CTA button in artwork (added by platform). T&C copy only if legally required. |
| Google Demand Gen / YouTube | Video + companion banners | Headline/description copy as briefed; video scripts should work with sound off where relevant. |
| Meta (FB/IG) | Image, video, carousel, collection | 1:1 or 4:5 aspect ratio for mobile. Videos ideally 5–10 seconds. Header font ≥70–80pt; body ≥40–50pt. Primary text 1–3 lines max. No CTA button in artwork (platform-added). Respect Stories/Reels safe zones (edges clear of text/logo so UI doesn't overlap). Logo ≥80px height, use colour logo unless contrast issue. Use the relevant subsidiary logo (e.g. GHS services use DA \| Doctor Anywhere logo). |
| TikTok | Short-form video, captions | Native, conversational tone; on-screen text must work without audio; subtitles mandatory for spoken dialogue; royalty-free music only. |
| LinkedIn | Sponsored Content, Message Ads, Lead Gen Forms | Professional B2B tone. |
| Apple Search Ads | Keyword-aligned headlines | Short, App Store search-intent optimised. |

**Social Reels production notes (where applicable):** use provided safe-zone templates; footage sharp/bright/correctly framed; logo watermark fades in within 10 frames; all spoken dialogue must be subtitled; all visual/audio content must be fully compliant with regional healthcare marketing regulations.

## 6. Mandatory Intake Questions

Ask before writing any new copy (skip only what's already specified in the request):

1. Which country or region? (SG, MY, TH, ID, PH, or regional)
2. What language? (English, Bahasa Malaysia, Bahasa Indonesia, Thai, Filipino, or mixed)
3. Ad platform, ad type, and format? (e.g. Meta / Image Ad / Single Image; Google / RSA / Search)
4. Messaging direction? (if none given, flag that angles will be suggested)
5. Which DA product/service?
6. Target audience? (demographics, intent, B2B/B2C, funnel stage)
7. Volume needed — number of headlines/descriptions/post copy/visual copy lines (only if not already specified)

**When messaging direction isn't provided:** suggest 2–3 angles based on product/core benefit, audience intent or pain point, platform/format, and current campaign themes (brand intro, care continuum, trust, AI-enabled care). Present angles and confirm before writing full copy.

## 7. Region & Language Rules

- **SG** — formal but approachable English; trust and convenience are key drivers; price points can be referenced if relevant.
- **MY** — English or Bahasa Malaysia depending on brief; bilingual copy may be needed; halal-sensitive framing where relevant.
- **TH** — Thai preferred for B2C; formal tone for healthcare; LINE and in-app context matters. Thai regulatory list (NHSO/สบส.) is notably strict — see Section 8.
- **ID** — Bahasa Indonesia for B2C; B2B may use English; trust-building and education matter given lower market awareness.
- **PH** — Filipino or English; community-centric, value-led messaging performs well; price sensitivity is a key factor.
- **Regional** — default to English unless specified; must work across all 5 markets without market-specific references.
- **Mandarin Chinese copy = cultural adaptation, not literal translation.** Jordan cannot read Chinese and relies on Claude for interpretation and cultural adaptation, not word-for-word translation.

Always flag if a copy direction conflicts with known advertising regulations in that market.

## 8. Compliance & Advertising Regulations

**Non-negotiable baseline:** All copy must comply with Singapore MOH/HSCA/HSA advertising regulations (and market-equivalent regulators elsewhere). No diagnostic or outcome claims, no efficacy language, no fear-based framing, no unsubstantiated superlatives. Science should be framed through **measurement, not results**.

**Compliance flags are raised proactively, never silently resolved** — surface issues like subsidy/CHAS conflicts, unverified statistics, or bundled inducements rather than quietly editing around them.

### Universal prohibited/restricted terms (all markets — SG, MY, TH, PH, ID)

| Category | Terms | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Outcome guarantee | cure, cures, heals, reverses, guaranteed, guarantee, 100% effective, pain-free, painless, permanent results, instant cure | Remove or replace (e.g. "support," "helps manage," "supports recovery," "designed to / aims to") |
| Unapproved efficacy | treats, prevents | Remove unless substantiated basis exists ("supports the management of," "may help reduce the risk of") |
| Absolute claims | 100%, risk-free, side-effect free, completely safe, totally safe | Remove or substantiate ("well-tolerated" if evidenced) |
| Exaggeration | miracle, miraculous, breakthrough, revolutionary, life-changing | Remove (high severity) or substitute with "new/advanced" (medium severity, if accurate) |
| Superlative | best, #1, no.1, number one, world-class, top-rated | Substantiate with source or remove |
| Comparative | better than, more effective than | Substantiate or remove; competitor comparisons are risky |
| Testimonial implication | doctors recommend, patients love, before and after (SG/TH/PH) | Remove unless documented and compliant |
| Misleading pricing | free, 100% covered | Verify exact terms with compliance before use |
| Exaggeration (speed) | instant | Verify; substitute "fast/quick" if substantiated |

### Market-specific notes
- **MY** — Medicines (Advertisement and Sale) Act restricts public ads claiming to treat/cure/prevent listed serious diseases (e.g. "treat cancer," "treat diabetes," "treat heart disease"). Verify exact restricted-disease schedule before running claims referencing named conditions.
- **TH** — Thai law (NHSO/สบส.) has an extensive prohibited list, including: online prescription/buying drugs online, naming prescription drugs, claims to "treat illness online" or "faster than a hospital," "no need to see a doctor in person," "personalized," "guaranteed outcome/results," "health insurance," "best doctor," "most trusted," "premium level." Many terms (e.g. "specialist," "24/7 doctor access," "telemedicine," "experienced," "award-winning") require named credentials/sources or added qualifying language rather than outright removal.
- **SG/TH/PH** — before/after claims for medical services are restricted; verify per market regulator (SG MOH/HSCA; TH Medical Council) before use.

**Full authoritative list:** `HSCA_Advertising_Regulations.xlsx` in project files — always check this document directly for the current, complete phrase list and market coding rather than relying on memory, since it is the single source of truth and may be updated.

## 9. Output Format Standards

- Label each asset clearly (Headline 1, Description 2, Primary Text, Visual Copy — On-screen Text, etc.)
- Include character counts in brackets after each line, e.g. `[28/30]`
- Group variations when providing options (e.g. Option A — Benefit-led, Option B — Urgency-led)
- For visual copy, distinguish on-screen text vs. supporting/CTA text; flag if copy needs to work without audio
- Flag any line at risk of policy rejection against the advertising regulations document
- Present angles/options with a clear recommendation when direction is ambiguous; flag trade-offs (e.g. a punchier headline that may be too informal for SG healthcare audience) rather than making a silent choice

## 10. Working Method & Approach (How Jordan Likes to Work)

- **Full context before execution** — share all angles, references, and materials upfront before creative work begins; don't start drafting until briefing is complete or Jordan signals readiness.
- **Read before writing** — always read live landing pages or uploaded briefs to verify service details, pricing, and process claims before drafting copy.
- **Layer-specific differentiation** — copy must be written fresh per campaign layer/funnel stage with distinct angles; reusing locked lines across layers isn't acceptable unless explicitly confirmed.
- **Structured intake before production** — gather all campaign parameters (audience, format, objective, character limits, platform, CTA rules) before writing; ask clarifying questions if parameters are incomplete.
- **Format discipline** — standard designer brief format: a visual style direction table (Visual Style, Headline, Sub-line) plus a separate "For Digital Marketer" block (Post Copy with character counts, Headline, Description, Landing Page).
- **Options with prioritisation** — present best-combination recommendations as ranked shortlists rather than exhaustive tables when volume is high.
- **Iterative corrections welcomed** — corrections mid-session should be acknowledged and applied immediately going forward.
- **Portable documentation** — Markdown briefing docs (like this one) are used to carry context across chat sessions, since project knowledge files don't transfer between chats.
- **Tie recommendations to objectives** — always connect suggestions back to the relevant 2026 marketing objective (O1–O4).
- **Respect the channel mix** — don't recommend a channel Jordan doesn't run; be aware of what MoEngage/Looker Studio can and can't do.
- **Be concise and strategic** — skip the basics, cut to the insight.
