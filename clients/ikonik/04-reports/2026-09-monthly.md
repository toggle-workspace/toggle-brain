## How each number was counted (keep this the same next month)

- **Spend:** taken from Jordan's platform screenshots, never from the leads sheet.
- **Slide 2, platform enquiries:** WhatsApp chats from the WhatsApp campaign row (1,603), plus form leads (227 Meta + 8 TikTok + 12 Google booking forms = 247), plus Meta calls placed (925). Google direction clicks and email clicks are left out. The total is divided by the calendar days in the month (30) to get the daily rate, and pacing is that rate over 100. Each per-unit cost uses its own channel's spend: RM3.41 per message, RM9.52 per lead (Meta and TikTok lead form spend over 235 leads) and RM4.30 per call. The blended CPL (RM6.55) is total spend, TikTok included, over 2,775.
- **Slide 4, spend by source:** each Meta campaign is matched to its clinic source. TikTok LIVE gets all TikTok spend except lead gen (RM5,079.14), and email gets all Google spend. Sources are ranked by cost per appointment, and the paused sources go at the bottom.
- **Slide 5:** month-on-month changes are shown in percent, except enquiry-to-appointment, which is shown in percentage points (+13.5 pts). The appointment mix bars are scaled to the largest value on the chart (August LASIK, 411).
- **Slide 6:** uses platform numbers, not the clinic log. Meta is chats + leads + calls (2,755), Google is all conversions including directions (387), and TikTok is N/A. This matches how August was counted, but it is not the same as slide 2's count, which leaves out Google directions. If Jordan asks to switch Google to new enquiries only, September becomes 29 and August becomes 57 (11 forms + 46 email clicks).
- **Slide 7 projection:** applies September's cost per enquiry and cost per patient by source to October's budget lines, then scales up by 25/24 for the missing 30 Sep working day. The result is about 1,650 enquiries and 750 appointments at RM24.00 each.
- **Working days:** Mondays to Saturdays, without public holidays. The clinic books nothing on Sundays or on Malaysia Day (16 Sep). August had 25 (Merdeka on 31 Aug); September had 24 logged plus the unlogged 30 Sep.
