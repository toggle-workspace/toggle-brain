# Ikonik weekly report log

The running performance history for Ikonik's weekly reports, newest week first. **Read this before drafting or reviewing any Ikonik weekly report,** then add the new week here once the report is done.

- Detailed write-ups per week: `2026-Www-weekly.md` in this folder (W31 and W39 so far).
- Weeks W32 to W38 were backfilled on 28 Sep 2026 from the master deck `[Ikonik] 2026 - Toggle Weekly Report.pptx` on Jordan's Desktop. They record what the deck said as published and were not re-verified against platforms.
- Workflow and file layout for the deck and the clinic leads log: see "Weekly reporting" in `../CLIENT.md`.

## How to update this log each week

1. Add a row to the scorecard (top of the table) and a section under "Week by week" (top of the list).
2. Write `2026-Www-weekly.md` for the week with the full numbers, creative read and recommendations.
3. Update "Open threads" below: close what got resolved, carry forward what did not, and note how many weeks an ask has been open.
4. After Jordan sends the deck, reconcile this log and the weekly file to the sent version, since his edits carry decisions.

## Method notes that affect comparisons

- Slide 2 per-unit costs: up to W38 the deck divided **total** spend by each count (for example RM80.35 per lead). From W39 each channel's own spend is used (RM5.94 per lead). Do not compare the two directly.
- The prior week is restated from the current clinic log each week, since late entries arrive. Published and restated figures differ (W38: 331 published, 364 restated).
- Meta spend for W38 differs between the Looker dashboard (RM1,810.93) and the log (RM2,311.81).
- Platform enquiries exclude TikTok, whose enquiries appear only in the clinic log.
- The clinic log's spend rows lag by a couple of days; always pull spend from the platforms.

## Week by week

### W39 · 21 to 27 September 2026
Full write-up: `2026-W39-weekly.md`.
- Fewer enquiries (-9.6%), more patients (+8.8% to 174), best booking rate of the month (52.9%), cost per patient down to RM23.09.
- Meta recorded about 70% more calls and chats, but the clinic logged about the same number: three in ten reach the log against half in W38. Calls cost more per patient after the budget rise.
- TikTok LIVE: 44% less spend, 4 more patients, about RM36 per patient against RM61.
- Google: 5 of 89 conversions were new enquiries, about RM79 each.
- Recommended: hold Meta call and WhatsApp budgets until the log gap closes, scale LIVE in steps, retire the prescription-number ads, scale doctor Q&A, dated offers, "it was easy" and life-goal creative.

### W38 · 14 to 20 September 2026
- Malaysia Day (16 Sep) took out a working day; enquiries fell 20% as published, but enquiry to appointment rose to the month's best at the time (48.3% published).
- WhatsApp on tighter audiences: fewer chats, 60% booked against 39%. TikTok LIVE grew enquiries (101) and appointments (26) as spend rose 28% with CPM down 8%.
- Meta lead form halved again; RM158.91 per appointment.
- Creative: Katarak Bahaya best on forms; Begitu Mudah best on WhatsApp ("ease beats aspiration"); Bertahun Tak Nampak (caregiver) best on calls, Ibu Bapa worst.
- Recommended: put the call budget back up, keep WhatsApp tight, hold lead gen at a sixth, open a Shah Alam call ad set, split directions from bookings on Google, keep brand funded.

### W37 · 7 to 13 September 2026
- TikTok DM and lead gen paused; TikTok now LIVE boosting plus Taplink traffic. Meta became the main volume driver: 77 platform enquiries a day against 100.
- Clinic: 415 enquiries, 170 appointments (41.0%), RM22.34 per appointment; calls booked 74%, lead forms 10%.
- Johor and Kelantan call ad set paused and budget moved to Klang Valley; calls became the strongest line. WhatsApp reconnected after the 31 Aug outage.
- Google restarted after the GTM fix; cataract keywords converted near brand's rate. Nine in ten Google conversions are direction clicks.
- Creative: Katarak Bahaya best lead form; Power -1900 best on WhatsApp but fading on forms; 1 Tahun Lepas (career/uniform) best call ad; Puan Normadiah from best to worst.
- Recommended: next budget increase into calls, scale the cataract cluster, retire Dulu Rabun and Puan Normadiah, build cataract keywords. Asked Ikonik for "how did you hear about us" logging and Taplink access.

### W36 · 31 August to 6 September 2026
No weekly report in the master deck. W37's comparison gives RM3,889 spend, 396 enquiries and 156 appointments (39.4%, RM24.93), with 31 Aug only partly logged. WhatsApp ads were down after the 31 Aug integration disconnect, and Google was paused after the website revamp removed GTM.

### W35 · 24 to 30 August 2026
- 145 platform enquiries a day against 200 (73%), RM6.46 blended. TikTok DMs off; Meta carried volume.
- Meta scaled to RM4,316: lead form and calls turned extra money into results, WhatsApp did not (five times the clicks, fewer chats).
- Flags: WhatsApp integration disconnected 31 Aug; GTM missing after the website revamp, so Google paused.
- August month summary and September forecast: 50 appointments a day would need RM38,100 to RM53,200 a month. Recommended September split at RM18,000: Meta RM10,000, TikTok RM6,500, Google RM1,500.
- Creative: Power -1900 cheapest lead (RM7.53); Dulu Rabun wearing out in KL on WhatsApp; Puan Normadiah best call ad (89 calls at RM3.53).
- Asked Ikonik to decide the September target and budget and approve a Meta LIVE ads budget.

### W34 · 17 to 23 August 2026
- Budget shifted to Meta, which overtook TikTok as the biggest line. 147 platform enquiries a day (74%), RM6.00 blended.
- TikTok DM campaign paused (cheap but not converting); Johor and Kelantan dragged TikTok lead gen.
- Meta nearly tripled spend with flat cost per enquiry; WhatsApp became the engine (735 chats at RM2.29).
- RM5,000 brought forward from September into August; another RM5,000 from October flagged for September.
- Creative: Dulu Rabun volume leader on forms and WhatsApp; Begitu Mudah most efficient lead form; Kejap Ye best call ad.

### W33 · 10 to 16 August 2026
- Meta improved the account on a third of the spend; TikTok cost per enquiry rose. 71 platform enquiries a day against 200 (calls not yet counted).
- Meta call ads launched (75 calls in week one). Meta LIVE boosting set up; TikTok whitelisted for LIVE ads.
- TikTok traffic repointed from the homepage to Taplink; video views paused; lead gen expanded to JB, Kelantan, ID and SG, with ID and SG paused on cost.
- Google keyword rebuild and appointment form tracking installed.
- Creative: Dulu Rabun (launched 13 Aug) the new Meta winner; local cut converts at 11.87% against 4.85% for the Jakarta/Medan/Singapore cut.

### W32 · 27 July to 9 August 2026 (cumulative report)

- Google: almost every result was a directions or call click from brand searches.
- Creative: patient testimonials won on TikTok ("15 minit je", Puan Elisha); on Meta, PelaburanBerbaloi cheapest lead and KejapYe best WhatsApp ad.
