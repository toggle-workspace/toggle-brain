### September to date (1 to 27 Sep, clinic log)

1,484 enquiries, 660 appointments, 44.5% booked, RM21.60 per appointment on the by-source spend (RM23.69 on the RM15,638 platform total). Calls and WhatsApp bring 505 of 660 appointments at about RM15 each. TikTok LIVE brings 28% of enquiries but 14% of appointments. Lead forms cost RM60.50 per appointment, about four times a call.

Projection to 30 Sep (last three working days at W39's daily rate): about 1,642 enquiries and 747 appointments on about RM17,375, so RM23.26 per appointment against August's RM29.79. That is roughly August's patient count on a fifth less spend.

## What drove performance

- **Meta's platform numbers and the clinic log diverged.** Meta recorded about 70% more calls and chats than W38 (643 against 378), but the clinic logged about the same number (186 against 190). About three in ten now reach the log, against half in W38. This is the biggest finding of the week and the top next step.
- **Calls got more expensive per patient.** Call spend rose from RM563 (W38 dashboard) to RM1,065.89 and bought 4 more appointments, so cost per call patient went from about RM11 to RM20.
- **TikTok LIVE improved sharply.** LIVE spend fell 44% (RM1,201 to RM669), enquiries fell 28%, and appointments rose from 26 to 30 because 36% booked against 22%. TikTok cost per appointment fell from about RM61 to RM36. CPM (RM1.27 to RM1.16) and CPC (RM0.05) held, so the lower volume came from the budget.
- **Google's conversions are mostly directions.** 84 of 89 conversions were direction clicks; 4 email clicks and 1 booking form were the new enquiries, about RM79 each. Brand took 74 of 89 conversions on 61% of spend. Generic impressions rose 42% with clicks flat at 94, and generic cost per conversion rose from RM7.86 to RM10.18.
