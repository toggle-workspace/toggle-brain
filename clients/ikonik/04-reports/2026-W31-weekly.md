## What drove performance

- TikTok carried the volume. The DMs campaign alone produced 354 of the week's 379 messages.
- Malay language ad sets drove most of the TikTok messages, and testimonial ads drove most of the TikTok form leads. Social proof is the early front runner as a creative angle, on one week of data.
- Meta produced enquiries at RM2.77 against TikTok's RM7.94, on 3% of the week's budget. Two days on a new account, so this is an early read and not a benchmark.
- Google recorded no appointment form submissions but did record 5 calls and directions clicks, so the search traffic is reaching people with visit intent.

## Growth observation raised

The Google zero traces to tracking, not to performance. The website audit (`01-strategy/website-audit-2026-08-04.md`, and blocker 1 in `CLIENT.md`) found that GA4, Meta, TikTok and Google Ads all fire pageviews only, with no lead event on any pixel. Google has nothing to count even when a patient submits the appointment form. Fixing this is the prerequisite for scaling Google spend, and it also affects Meta and TikTok optimization signals.

## What we recommended

**Toggle**
- TikTok: test two new messaging angles against the testimonial angle, and add a qualifying question to the lead form and message template. Keep all five campaigns live, and split the DMs campaign by language once each ad set clears 50 messages.
- Google: confirm the three GTM containers in Tag Assistant, build the appointment form submission event, import it into Google Ads. Hold budgets flat until conversions record. Expand generic keyword coverage after that, since 963 impressions over three days is a thin pool. Add call and location extensions.
- Meta: hold both campaigns unchanged to 14 August so the learning phase completes on a stable budget, then load three new ads per campaign and retire the weakest performer. Report LIVE boosting separately from 3 August.
- Reporting: deliver the live performance dashboard by Wednesday.

**Ikonik**
- Share a TikTok and Meta LIVE calendar for the full month, with topic, description, date and time per session.
- Share the qualifying question to add to the lead form and message template.
- Open question: can Meta LIVE use Messenger and Instagram as messaging destinations alongside WhatsApp? WhatsApp on its own limits live boosting to Monday through Saturday, 8am to 9pm.
