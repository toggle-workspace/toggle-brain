# Ikonik — Style Pack

> Prospect-stage draft (July 2026). Refine after engagement kickoff with client brand assets.

## Voice
- Trust-led, medical credibility first — the livestream surgery content carries the persuasion; copy stays calm and factual, never hype
- Promise-averse client: **never quote traffic numbers**; speak in leads, appointments, patients
- BM/EN mix (BM-leaning for mass LASIK audience, per their own TikTok captions); CN variant for the Chinese-market segment test
- Price anchor allowed: "bebas rabun serendah RM2,500/satu mata"
- All claims KKLIU-compliant (their licence: KKLIU:1537/EXP 31.12.2026) — no cure claims, no superlatives about outcomes

## Visual
- Source material = client's own livestream + surgery footage and existing TikTok posts; we version, we don't originate
- Client brand assets (logo, colours) to be collected at kickoff
- Toggle-facing documents use Toggle design tokens (`clients/toggle/design-system/TOKENS.md`)

## Never
- KOL/influencer framing (audience skews finance/banking)
- View-count or reach as a success metric
- Naming competitor clinics in client-facing creatives (fine in internal strategy docs)
