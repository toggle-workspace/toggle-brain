Why IKONIK Can Win This Market

Vista Eye outspends you and out-branches you. But you hold three assets a multi-branch chain cannot copy — the gap is machinery, not brand.

Live surgery, shown live
The livestreamed procedures are the single strongest trust asset in the category. Chains won't replicate it — corporate and medico-legal caution stops them. In healthcare, the biggest conversion barrier is fear; your content dissolves it before the patient ever messages.
The Prof Muhaya lineage
Rebranded from Prof Muhaya's centre in 2021, IKONIK carries a household-name surgical heritage and a decade-deep patient base — credibility that new-money competitors buy with ad spend, and you already own.
Search-level brand recall
Patients report finding you at the top of TikTok search. That organic pull means paid campaigns start warm — Spark Ads amplify posts that already convert, instead of cold creatives guessing at what works.
The gap, stated plainly: no paid engine guaranteeing lead flow independent of the algorithm · no systematic capture of the attention already earned · no end-to-end tracking from view → lead → appointment → surgery. This proposal builds exactly those three things — and nothing you already do well.

The 6-Month Roadmap

Build → launch → calibrate → scale. Months 1–2 create the machine; months 3–6 are where the compounding happens — the reason this is a 6-month partnership, not 3.

Month 1 · Foundation
  • NDA → GSC + analytics access → traffic forecast & baseline locked
  • LIVE-ads whitelisting request via Marcus (week 1 — lead time matters)
  • Tracking install, sales tracker live, landing page built, campaigns staged
Month 2 · Launch
  • TikTok lead + IM campaigns and Google Search go live
  • Meta retargeting starts as pixels populate
  • First leads flow; weekly sales sync begins; SLA measured
Month 3 · Calibrate
  • LIVE stream ads live (post-whitelisting)
  • First CPL → cost-per-appointment calibration with real close data
  • Doubling target confirmed as a hard monthly number
Month 4 · Optimise
  • Budget shifts hard to winning creative × audience × offer combos
  • Retargeting pools mature; lookalikes from converted patients
  • Landing page CRO from real session data
Month 5 · Scale
  • Media budget scales (RM 9K → up to RM 15K) only where cost-per-appointment holds
  • Keyword + audience expansion; CN-market creative test
  • Livestream calendar aligned with ad pushes
Month 6 · Prove & Plan
  • Full-funnel report: spend → leads → appointments → surgeries, in ringgit
  • Playbook documented — the system is yours, not a black box
  • Renewal decision with data on the table; iSpa + aesthetics expansion scoped
