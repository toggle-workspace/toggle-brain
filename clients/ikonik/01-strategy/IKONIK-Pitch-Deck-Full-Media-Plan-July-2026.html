Why IKONIK Wins — What the Ads Will Amplify

The ads don't invent a story. They put proof you already own in front of people who haven't seen it yet.

Reviews & ratings — patients vouch for you
Your ratings and patient reviews are the differentiation competitors can't buy. In healthcare, a real patient's words outperform any ad claim — so review proof goes inside the creatives: screenshots, star ratings, verbatim testimonials, all KKLIU-vetted.
Reputable doctors, radical openness
Senior specialists who operate live on camera — no other centre in Malaysia lets patients watch the actual procedure before committing. Doctor-led creatives put the faces and credentials patients will actually meet at the centre front and centre.
The Malay community advantage
BM-first content, "bebas rabun" positioning, and accessible pricing have built real trust with Malay audiences — a segment the big chains underserve. The paid plan protects and scales this: BM creative as the lead language, not the translation.
Where we come in: this proof currently reaches only the people the algorithm chooses to show it to. Paid distribution puts it in front of every high-intent prospect in the Klang Valley — daily, measurably, and independent of the For You page.

SEO — Own the Rankings You're Currently Renting

Ads rent the top of Google; SEO owns it. Patients search in stages before choosing a provider — broad location searches, then price and safety questions, then comparison queries right before booking. We want IKONIK at positions 1–3 for all of them.

What we're fixing — found in our audit
  • Duplicate redirects leaking crawl equity
  • Missing schema — no structured data telling Google you're a medical clinic
  • Broken title tags (e.g. the pterygium page) & H1 hierarchy issues
  • FAQ markup absent on treatment pages
  • Core Web Vitals — baselined once GSC / GA4 / PageSpeed access is granted
The 6-week foundation sequence
  • Week 1 — foundation fixes live: redirects, titles, H1s (templating-level, routed to your dev team)
  • Week 2 — medical schema + FAQ markup deployed
  • Week 6 — first re-crawl & progress read against the live baseline
  • Then ongoing: content optimisation against the keyword map, monthly ranking reports
The guarantee
20 keywords · 6 months
20 target keywords (7 short-tail + 13 long-tail, each mapped to a target page — full list next slide) ranked within 6 months of optimisation work beginning.
If we miss the target, the next 2 months of SEO service are FREE — that's how confident the audit makes us.
How it fits the paid plan: paid fills the funnel from day one; SEO makes the same funnel cheaper every month after.
