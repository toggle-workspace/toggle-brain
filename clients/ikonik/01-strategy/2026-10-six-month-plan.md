## Phases

1. **Oct to Nov, fix the leaks.**
   - **Toggle:** the daily check and action rule, call ads in clinic hours only, push weeks, and new ads every two weeks.
   - **Ikonik:** answer calls within three rings, call back within the hour, reply to WhatsApp within five minutes, and share Taplink access.
   - **Success:** half of Meta's calls reach the clinic log (35% in September), at RM22.50 a patient.
2. **Dec to Jan, scale what works.**
   - **Toggle:** move budget to the cheapest sources and push weeks, retarget video and LIVE viewers, add cataract keywords on Google, and launch "LASIK before Ramadan" in mid-January.
   - **Ikonik:** approve offers two weeks ahead, and film "one year later" and family cataract stories.
   - **Success:** 50% of enquiries book (45%), at RM21.50.
3. **Feb to Mar, plan for the festive months.**
   - **Toggle:** push before Ramadan, hold during it, and push after Raya. Move LIVE to after berbuka, and run cataract check-up ads for parents before balik kampung.
   - **Ikonik:** confirm Ramadan hours and Raya closures by mid-January, and share 2025 monthly appointments.
   - **Success:** about 800 patients a month at RM22.50.

- **Totals:** RM138,000 brings 5,750 appointments from 11,880 enquiries, at an average of RM24. That's 958 a month, 30% above the August and September average of 735.
- **Against the RM18,000 plan:** about 940 more appointments for RM30,000 more, or about RM32 each. The deck says so plainly, because each extra ringgit buys less.
- **Why the extra is priced higher than the average:** August spent RM4,160 more than September for only 30 more patients. W39 also showed calls getting more expensive as budget rose.
- **Booking rate path:** 45.5% in October, 47% in November, 48% in December, then 50% from January to March.

**Where the extra RM5,000 goes (Expansion Plan slide):**
- **WhatsApp and calls, +RM3,200:** about 100 more appointments a month at about RM32 each.
- **More TikTok LIVE days, +RM1,000:** about 25 more appointments.
- **Win back unbooked enquiries, +RM400:** 868 enquiries didn't book in September. Toggle retargets them and Ikonik follows up on WhatsApp. At 1 in 20, that's about 45 more appointments.
- **New Google searches, +RM400 from November:** cataract, diabetic eye and kids' myopia. Supporting data: eye disease bookings rose 23%, and NHMS 2023 puts adult diabetes at 15.6%.
- **Test rule:** the last two are tests, and their budget moves to WhatsApp, calls and LIVE if a patient costs more than RM30 after six weeks.
- **Organic growth (SEO and AI search)** sits outside the budget and is scoped separately. It's based on the July audit finding that Ikonik is missing from AI answers to "how much does LASIK cost in Malaysia".

**Dropped at Jordan's call (5 Oct):** a YouTube doctor Q&A test (RM700, moved into WhatsApp and calls) and a Chinese-language ads test before Chinese New Year.
