Where IKONIK stands, and why now

Ten years, 152,000+ patients, 32,000+ laser eye surgeries — and a TikTok channel doing something almost no eye centre in Malaysia does: livestreaming real procedures. The attention engine already works. What's missing is the machine that converts that attention into consultation bookings, predictably, every month:

1 · Attention without capture

Livestreams pull thousands of viewers, but there is no system catching them at the moment of intent. Enquiries live and die inside TikTok comments and DMs — unqualified, untracked, and dependent on whoever checks the inbox.

2 · Organic dependency

All patient flow from TikTok rides on the algorithm. A quiet fortnight of reach means a quiet clinic. There is no paid layer guaranteeing a floor of qualified enquiries regardless of what the For You page decides.

3 · An untracked funnel

From view → enquiry → consultation → surgery, nothing is measured end to end. Without that, no one can say which content actually books patients — so budget and effort can't be pointed at what works.

What this is (and isn't)

This is not a content retainer that replaces what IKONIK's team already does well. It is a lead generation layer built on top of the organic machine that's already working: your livestreams and surgery content become the ad creative; we build the capture, paid amplification, and tracking around them. Every creative stays compliant with Malaysia's Medicine Advertisements Board (KKLIU) requirements — non-negotiable in healthcare, and something we handle as standard.

Roadmap · 1 of 2

Stage 1–2 — build the machine, switch it on

Stage 1 · Foundation — funnel, tracking & creative systemMonth 1

No ads run in Month 1. We build the plumbing first, so from the first ringgit of media spend, every lead is captured and every result is measurable.

  • Kickoff + funnel audit: TikTok account, website, current enquiry handling, front-desk process
  • WhatsApp-first capture flow — click-to-WhatsApp setup, qualification script, response-time SLA agreed with the front desk
  • Conversion-focused landing page for LASIK/refractive enquiries (offer, trust markers, booking CTA) — hosted, tracked, mobile-first
  • Tracking install: TikTok Pixel, Meta Pixel/CAPI, WhatsApp lead logging — one lead sheet, one source of truth
  • Creative mining session: audit livestream archive, identify the 10–15 strongest moments; cut first batch of 6–8 ad creatives
  • KKLIU compliance review of all claims, offers and footage before launch

You receive: a working funnel (page + WhatsApp flow + tracking), first ad creative batch, campaign plan. GATE: launch sign-off before media spend

Stage 2 · Launch & Learn — campaigns liveMonths 2–3

Paid campaigns go live on TikTok and Meta, geo-fenced to Klang Valley. These two months are about finding the winning combination of audience, offer and creative — fast, with small controlled budgets.

  • TikTok Ads: Spark Ads on top-performing organic posts + lead campaigns using livestream-cut creatives
  • Meta Ads: Facebook/Instagram lead forms + click-to-WhatsApp campaigns for the 30–55 segment TikTok reaches less
  • Creative testing cycle: 4–6 new creative variants per month, weekly review, kill losers early
  • Offer testing: free eye assessment vs. price-anchor ("bebas rabun dari RM2,500/mata") vs. livestream-viewer exclusive
  • Weekly optimisation + lead quality feedback loop with the front desk (are leads showing up? qualified?)
  • First monthly performance reports: spend, leads, cost per lead, consults booked

You receive: live campaigns, first flow of tracked leads, and by end of Month 3 a clear read on what a qualified enquiry costs. GATE: Month 3 review sets the scaling plan

Why the livestreams matter so much: in healthcare, the biggest conversion barrier is fear and distrust. IKONIK is the only centre showing the actual procedure, live, unedited. Used as ad creative, that footage does the persuasion work before the patient ever messages — which is why we expect it to outperform any polished brand ad, and why this programme is built around it.

Stage 3–4 — scale what books patients, then lock it in

Stage 3 · Optimise & ScaleMonths 4–5

By Month 4 we know which creatives, offers and audiences produce consultations — not just leads. Budget shifts hard toward the winners.
