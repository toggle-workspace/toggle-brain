# City University Malaysia — Account History and Findings

Reconciled record of the Meta engagement Toggle ran for City University Malaysia between 14 March and 1 July 2026, delivered as a subcontractor to Creative Lead with no direct client relationship. Every figure below is extracted from the six monthly report decks, including the Ads Manager screenshots pasted into those decks. This file is the source of truth for the numbers used in `full-funnel-and-crm-proposal-2026-08-18.pptx`.

## The client

Private university operating as U.C.I Education Sdn Bhd, DU049(B), established 1984. Four locations: Petaling Jaya (Menara City U, main campus), Cyberjaya (plus a separate City University Medical Sciences site), Johor Bahru, and Kota Kinabalu regional centre. The website claims 80+ programmes, 10,000 current students, 40,000 graduates, 60 nationalities, 99.9% graduate employability, a QS 5 Star rating, and a SETARA 2022 "Competitive" rating.

Nine faculties, plus a City Fashion School and a City Animation and Game Design School, plus Open Distance Learning and APEL pathways for working adults. Partnerships promoted on the site as of August 2026: University of Bedfordshire (signed July 2026, 12 programmes), IMI Switzerland hospitality pathway, MINDEF work-based learning, and scholarships of up to RM20 million granted annually.

Lead capture on the public site is a single generic enquiry form (name, a binary undergraduate or postgraduate choice, email, nationality, phone), a Malay-language Dermasiswa B40 JPA form, and three WhatsApp numbers split by undergraduate, postgraduate, and international. There is no programme-level routing and no visible verification step.

## Account structure

Two Meta campaigns only, no other platform.

- `tg_meta_paid_bau_my_20260313_bof_leadgen_prospecting` — instant lead form submissions
- `tg_meta_paid_bau_my_20260313_mof_messages_prospecting` — WhatsApp messaging conversations, paused 14 May 2026

There was no top of funnel, no retargeting, no Google, and no TikTok at any point.

Weakest performers, all four of them static images and three of them Chinese-language:

| Ad | Spend | Leads | CPL |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20260605_c4_cn_future-graduates-business | RM112.88 | 1 | RM112.88 |
| 20260523_c4_en_onefoundation-multipledegree | RM172.15 | 2 | RM86.08 |
| 20260503_c5_cn_ece-stable-career | RM254.22 | 3 | RM84.74 |
| 20260523_c2_cn_5steps-draping-to-runway | RM510.56 | 7 | RM72.94 |

Four findings carried into the proposal:

1. Video outperformed static in every month without exception.
2. Creative that opened with the audience's objection beat creative that opened with a feature list. MBA 12 Months led on exam-time anxiety, and the Business Course graphic led on decision paralysis.
3. Rotating the programme mix reset the learning phase and pushed cost per lead from RM15.68 in April to RM24.05 in May.
4. Chinese-language creative translated from English underperformed throughout. The one Chinese angle with signal was Accounting, at RM30.91 and RM16.23 on two June ads.

## Where the engagement stopped

The July report records a single day of delivery, RM506.85 and 25 leads, then a pause attributed to a "direction change". No party is named in the deck.

The consolidated March to June deck closed with five open questions to City Uni that were never answered: what counts as a good lead, whether low quality clusters in Klang Valley or outstation, what the enrolment targets are, whether a shared tracker could replace the manual sheet, and whether Postgraduate returns for the September and October intake. The same deck recommended conditional logic on lead forms, OTP verification, and a lightweight CRM between Meta Lead Ads and the sales team, to be live by August.

Every report measured cost per lead. None measured cost per application or cost per enrolment, because no outcome data ever came back. That gap is the argument the direct proposal is built on.
