## Notes

- The proposal is written as a fresh start. It does not recommend which programmes to advertise, does not diagnose City Uni's internal process, and does not assume their conversion or enrolment rates. City Uni nominates the programmes.
- Creative supply is owned by the partner agency. The deck carries messaging directions and a placeholder slide for their production plan.
- Every report we produced measured cost per lead. None measured cost per enrolment, because no outcome data came back. That gap is the argument the CRM section rests on.
- Ads can run in English, Bahasa Malaysia and Mandarin. Chinese-language creative translated from English underperformed in the prior engagement, so each language needs a native build.
