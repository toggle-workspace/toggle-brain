# Audience Reference Visuals — Image Prompts

Three reference mockups, one per audience, for slide 6 of the proposal deck. Each finished mockup is 1200 x 1200.

## Division of labour

| Tool | Produces | Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| **Canva AI** (Magic Media) | The photograph only. No text, no layout, no branding. | Prompt A |
| **Claude Design** | The ad layout, the background, the type hierarchy, the on-ad copy and the button. Leaves a marked slot for the Canva photo. | Prompt B |

Image models render long text badly, which is why every word stays out of Prompt A and lives in Prompt B.

**Aspect ratios differ by audience**, because the photo has to fill the slot its layout gives it. The finished mockup is 1200 x 1200 in all three cases.

| Audience | Canva AI aspect | Slot in the layout |
|---|---|---|
| SPM and STPM leavers | Portrait 9:16 | Vertical phone frame, centred |
| Parents | Landscape 5:3 | Top 60% of the canvas |
| Working adults | Square 1:1 | Full bleed behind the copy |

## Workflow

1. Run Prompt A in Canva AI at the aspect in the table above. Generate three or four, pick one.
2. Run Prompt B in Claude Design. It returns the layout with the copy set and an empty image slot.
3. Drop the Canva photo into the slot and export at 1200 x 1200.

## Three standing rules

1. **The copy is directional.** It shows the shape of the ad, not the approved line. Every headline here is a placeholder until City Uni confirms the programmes and any fee or scholarship terms, because the platforms hold the advertiser to what the ad says.
2. **No programme is named.** City Uni nominates the programmes, so every mockup stays programme-agnostic.
3. **English only for the mockups.** Bahasa Malaysia and Mandarin hooks get written natively for their own audiences when creative production starts, rather than translated from these lines.

## 1. SPM and STPM leavers, 18 to 24

**Direction on the deck:** vertical video shot on campus and cut fast, first three seconds carrying a face and a question the viewer is already asking, on screen text throughout, one clear next step.

### Prompt A — Canva AI, portrait 9:16, photograph only

```
Medium close-up of an 18 year old Malaysian student mid-sentence talking straight to
camera, standing on an open university campus walkway with other students blurred behind
her, late afternoon, soft directional daylight from frame left with warm bounce off pale
concrete, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, handheld documentary feel, natural skin
tones, candid and unposed. Vertical 9:16 composition, subject framed from the chest up and
positioned in the lower two thirds, leaving clean uncluttered space across the top third.
Photographic only. No text, no captions, no logos, no watermarks, no graphic overlays,
no borders.
```

### Prompt B — Claude Design, layout and copy

```
Build a 1200 x 1200 square ad mockup that shows how a TikTok ad for a Malaysian university
would look to an 18 to 24 year old school leaver. Do not generate a photograph. Leave the
image area as a clearly marked empty slot with a light grey fill and a thin dashed outline,
labelled "photo slot, 9:16".

Background: flat light neutral grey, #F2F3F6, edge to edge.

Image slot: a vertical 9:16 rectangle centred on the canvas, 1020px tall and 574px wide,
with 24px rounded corners and a soft drop shadow so it reads as a phone screen floating on
the background.

Copy inside the slot, styled as burned-in social captions rather than a designed poster:
- Across the upper third: "After SPM, what now?" Set it in a heavy geometric sans, white,
  roughly 64px, tight leading, two lines maximum, sitting on a semi-transparent dark scrim
  so it stays readable over any photo.
- Near the bottom of the slot: a solid pill button, #4A7BF7, white label at roughly 28px,
  reading "Ask us".

Type should look like phone-shot video captions: heavy, tight, slightly oversized, no
letter-spacing tricks and no drop shadows on the type itself.

Do not add a university logo, a course name, a fee figure or a scholarship claim.
```

## 2. Parents, 35 to 55

**Direction on the deck:** calm and plain, cost, credential and outcome in the first line before any campus atmosphere, static and carousel work as well as video, offer a conversation.

### Prompt A — Canva AI, landscape 5:3, photograph only

```
Medium shot of a Malaysian mother in her late forties sitting beside her teenage child at a
dining table at home, both looking at an open laptop, early evening, warm practical lamp
light from frame right mixed with cool ambient light from a window behind them, 50mm lens,
shallow focus held on the mother, calm and unhurried, an ordinary Malaysian home rather
than a styled set. Landscape 5:3 composition, the pair placed right of centre with clean
uncluttered space on the left. Photographic only. No text, no captions, no logos, no
watermarks, no graphic overlays, no borders.
```

### Prompt B — Claude Design, layout and copy

```
Build a 1200 x 1200 square ad mockup that shows how a Meta feed ad for a Malaysian
university would look to a parent aged 35 to 55. Do not generate a photograph. Leave the
image area as a clearly marked empty slot with a light grey fill and a thin dashed outline,
labelled "photo slot, 5:3".

Layout: a single flat ad still, not a phone mockup.

Top 60% of the canvas, 1200 x 720: the image slot, full bleed to the left, right and top
edges, no rounded corners.

Bottom 40%, 1200 x 480: a solid white panel carrying the copy, with 72px margins on the
left and right.

Copy in the white panel, stacked and left aligned:
- Headline in near-black #0F0F0F, medium-heavy sans, roughly 52px, two lines:
  "Fees, entry routes, and what happens after graduation."
- Supporting line beneath in mid grey #4A4A4A, roughly 30px:
  "Talk to an advisor before you decide."
- A solid green WhatsApp-style button, left aligned, 12px corner radius, white label at
  roughly 28px, reading "Chat on WhatsApp".

Keep the whole thing quiet and legible, closer to a bank or a hospital ad than a student
one. Generous white space, no gradients, no exclamation marks, no decorative shapes.

Do not add a university logo, a course name, a fee figure or a scholarship claim.
```

## 3. Working adults, 25 to 45

**Direction on the deck:** open on the time problem, show the format of study before the content of it, adult and direct, no student life framing.

### Prompt A — Canva AI, square 1:1, photograph only

```
Medium shot of a Malaysian professional in her early thirties working at a laptop at a tidy
desk in her apartment, late evening, one warm desk lamp from frame left with cool blue city
window light behind her, 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, composed and focused rather
than tired or stressed, still wearing office clothes. Square 1:1 composition, subject
placed in the lower right, leaving clean uncluttered space across the top of the frame.
Photographic only. No text, no captions, no logos, no watermarks, no graphic overlays,
no borders.
```

### Prompt B — Claude Design, layout and copy

```
Build a 1200 x 1200 square ad mockup that shows how a Meta ad for a Malaysian university
would look to a working adult aged 25 to 45. Do not generate a photograph. Leave the image
area as a clearly marked empty slot with a light grey fill and a thin dashed outline,
labelled "photo slot, 1:1".

Layout: a single flat ad still with the photograph running full bleed behind the copy.

Image slot: the full 1200 x 1200 canvas, edge to edge, sitting behind everything else.

Over the top 45% of the canvas, lay a soft dark gradient running from roughly 70% opaque
black at the top edge down to fully transparent, so white type stays readable over any
photo.

Copy, left aligned with 72px margins:
- Headline across the upper area in white, heavy sans, roughly 56px, two lines:
  "Work full time. Study around it."
- Supporting line beneath in white at 70% opacity, lighter weight, roughly 30px:
  "See how the schedule actually works."
- Bottom left, 72px up from the bottom edge: an outlined button, 2px white border, 12px
  corner radius, transparent fill, white label at roughly 28px, reading
  "See the entry routes".

Adult and direct in tone. No campus imagery, no student life framing, no bright accent
colours beyond the white type.

Do not add a university logo, a course name, a fee figure or a scholarship claim.
```
