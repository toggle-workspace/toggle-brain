## UNITAR International University

_Education · Malaysia_

**From Moonshot to Milestone: Redefining UNITAR's 40k Lead Acquisition**

### Overview

Established in 1997 as Southeast Asia's first virtual university, UNITAR International University is a pioneer in digital-first education and the first institution in Asia to earn a QS 5-Star Rating for Online Learning. With a focus on accessibility and employability, the university maintains a 98% graduate employment rate by delivering affordable, MQA-accredited programs through its main campus and 13 regional centers across Malaysia.

### Challenge

UNITAR needed a sustainable lead generation engine to fuel aggressive enrollment targets, deliver high volumes of qualified leads, reach prospects across all locations in Malaysia, and keep acquisition costs well below industry benchmarks.

By mid-2024, their campaigns faced rising CPLs (Google RM154, FB RM291, TikTok RM525) and inconsistent media performance. Despite significant investments, campaigns weren't scaling efficiently, requiring a complete re-engineering of their marketing strategy.

### Solution

Toggle implemented a full-funnel performance marketing strategy combining precision targeting, creative storytelling, and disciplined media operations.

**#1. Marketing Messaging Framework**

A structured messaging playbook aligned to the student decision journey:

- Top of Funnel (TOF): Myth-busting, aspirational, curiosity-driven content ("Is Traditional Education Holding You Back?", "Does University Prestige Really Matter?")
- Middle of Funnel: Reassurance messaging highlighting UNITAR's unique value proposition ("UNITAR: Designed for the Real World", "Learn, Apply, Get Hired")
- Bottom of Funnel: Urgency and conversion-driven CTAs ("One Click Away — Complete Your Application", "Don't Let This Opportunity Slip Away")

**#2. Media Planning & Channel Strategy**

- Google SEM + PMax → high-intent leads (students actively searching for degrees/universities)
- Meta (Facebook/Instagram) → scalable mid-funnel engagement using creative storytelling and lead forms, boosted by campaign consolidation for stronger learning signals
- TikTok → TOF and awareness building with strong emotional/relatable hooks, designed to shift perceptions among younger audiences

**#3. Platform Hygiene & Optimisation**

- Facebook CAPI (Conversions API): server-side conversion signals for stronger attribution and improved campaign learning
- Campaign Consolidation: reduced fragmentation across Meta campaigns, improving delivery efficiency and reducing audience overlap
- Audience Targeting Optimisation: shifted from broad, inefficient targeting to layered intent + lookalike audiences
- Spend Pacing Controls: smarter daily pacing avoided budget burn while scaling spend past RM11 million/month at stable CPLs

**Services delivered:** Performance Marketing · Creative Production · Reporting & Analysis · Website Tracking

### Results

| Metric                                                   | Result                |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Qualified leads generated                                | 32,000+               |
| CPL reduction YoY                                        | 47%                   |
| Market impression share (Education & Training, Malaysia) | 77%                   |
| Period                                                   | June – September 2025 |

### Key insight

A full-funnel messaging matrix (TOF to A3) is essential for bridging the gap between curiosity-driven leads and high-intent conversions. Channel diversification — leveraging Google for immediate demand, Meta for nurturing, and TikTok for future demand — created a sustainable ecosystem for massive scaling. The synergy between continuous creative rotation and a shift toward layered audience intent proved to be the primary driver in surpassing the most ambitious enrollment targets.

## Kith and Kin

_Real Estate · Kuala Lumpur & Selangor_

**How a real estate agency increased lead volume by 392%**

### Overview

Kith and Kin is a reputable real estate agency specialising in development projects across Kuala Lumpur and Selangor, with a primary focus on a newly launched development in Dutamas, near the Mont Kiara district, targeting both local and expatriate buyers.

### Challenge

Before working with Toggle, Kith and Kin relied solely on organic methods and traditional cold-calling to generate leads, with no paid advertising presence. Toggle's objective was to increase qualified lead volume while keeping CPL at a sustainable level.

### Solution

Upon analysing the market landscape, Toggle identified a significant gap in paid advertising from competitors around the Dutamas area. The approach primarily focused on Meta Ads, deploying targeted lead generation and message-specific campaigns. A series of compelling ad creatives across gifs, images, and video formats effectively showcased the project's unique selling points while maintaining an optimal CPL.

**Services delivered:** Performance Marketing · Creative Production · Reporting & Analysis · Conversion Optimisation

### Results

| Metric                                  | Result   |
| --------------------------------------- | -------- |
| Lead volume increase                    | +392%    |
| Conversion rate (leads to deals closed) | 10%      |
| Cost per lead                           | RM35     |
| Period                                  | 3 months |

### Key insight

A mix of lead generation and message-specific campaigns, combined with engaging ad formats (gifs, images, videos), helped capture attention and inform the audience at multiple touchpoints. Continuous refinement of messaging tailored to the target audience proved critical in enhancing campaign performance and lead quality.

## Great Eastern Singapore

_Insurance · Financial Services · Singapore_

### Overview

Great Eastern Singapore is one of the region's leading insurance providers, offering a comprehensive range of life, health, and general insurance products. While the brand has strong recognition, maintaining dominance in organic search is critical for capturing high-intent users actively researching insurance plans online.

### Challenge

Despite strong brand presence, Great Eastern faced several SEO challenges:

- Key product-related search terms such as "insurance plan" were underperforming
- Average keyword rankings were stagnating around position #4
- Meta titles and descriptions lacked optimised keyword targeting and semantic depth
- Search snippets were not fully optimised for CTR, reducing potential organic traffic gains
- Competitors were aggressively optimising their SEO strategies

### Solution

Toggle implemented a focused, data-driven on-page SEO optimisation strategy:

**#1. Metadata Optimisation** — Refreshed title tags to improve keyword prominence and alignment with search intent. Rewrote meta descriptions to enhance clarity, keyword density, and CTR appeal. Ensured semantic alignment between metadata and on-page content.

**#2. Keyword-Content Alignment** — Enhanced keyword-to-content relationships across priority landing pages. Improved internal keyword structuring to reinforce topical authority. Optimised headings and content hierarchy to improve crawlability and context signals.

**#3. Search Relevance & SERP Performance** — Strengthened semantic keyword coverage to improve ranking potential. Optimised snippets to better match user search intent. Focused on high-impact product category pages with the greatest commercial value.

**Services delivered:** On-Page SEO Optimisation · Metadata Restructuring and Enhancement · SERP Performance Optimisation · Content Keyword Refinement · Ongoing Performance Tracking and Ranking Analysis

### Results

| Metric                              | Result   |
| ----------------------------------- | -------- |
| Average keyword ranking improvement | #4 → #1  |
| Organic clicks increase             | +16%     |
| Period                              | 2 months |

### Key insight

Small but strategic on-page optimisations can drive significant ranking improvements when focused on high-value keywords. Well-structured metadata plays a critical role in both search visibility and click-through performance. In highly competitive industries like insurance, even incremental SEO gains translate into meaningful business impact.

Singlife is a leading digital-first insurance provider in Singapore, offering life, health, and investment-linked insurance solutions. Operating in a YMYL (Your Money Your Life) category, content quality and authority signals play a decisive role in organic search rankings.

### Challenge

Despite strong brand presence, Singlife faced several organic search challenges:

- Core insurance keywords ranking around position #3, limiting maximum visibility and click capture
- Competing financial institutions strengthening long-form, authoritative content to dominate top positions
- Content required deeper alignment with user intent
- E.E.A.T. (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) signals needed enhancement to meet evolving Google algorithm standards for YMYL industries
- Competitive differentiation in search results was increasingly difficult

### Solution

Toggle implemented a comprehensive on-page content optimisation strategy centred on authority building, intent alignment, and structured relevance:

**#1. Intent-Aligned Content Expansion** — Developed keyword-rich, semantically structured content aligned with high-intent search queries. Expanded topical coverage to address informational, comparison-based, and transactional intent. Improved depth and comprehensiveness of priority insurance pages to outperform competing content.

**#2. Strengthening E.E.A.T. Signals** — Enhanced credibility markers within content to reinforce expertise and trustworthiness. Improved clarity, factual accuracy, and structured explanations of insurance-related topics. Optimised content presentation to align with Google's expectations for YMYL industries.

**#3. Structural & Relevance Optimisation** — Refined heading structures (H1–H3) to improve semantic hierarchy and crawlability. Improved internal linking to reinforce topical authority and contextual relationships. Optimised on-page elements to strengthen keyword prominence without over-optimisation.

**Services delivered:** Comprehensive On-Page Content Optimisation · Content Structuring and Authority Reinforcement · Keyword Expansion and Semantic Enhancement · E.E.A.T. Signal Strengthening · Ongoing Ranking and Performance Analysis

### Results

| Metric                              | Result   |
| ----------------------------------- | -------- |
| Average keyword ranking improvement | #3 → #1  |
| Organic clicks increase             | +23%     |
| Period                              | 3 months |

### Key insight

In YMYL industries, authority and trust signals are just as important as keyword optimisation. Comprehensive, intent-driven content consistently outperforms shallow optimisation tactics. Even a ranking improvement from #3 to #1 can generate substantial traffic gains in competitive verticals. A well-structured content hierarchy and strong semantic alignment improve both search engine crawlability and overall user experience.

## CIMB Malaysia

_Banking · Financial Services · Malaysia (YMYL)_

### Overview

CIMB Malaysia is one of the region's leading banking institutions, offering retail banking, credit cards, loans, wealth management, and corporate financial services. As a major financial brand within a YMYL category, search engine visibility plays a crucial role in customer acquisition, product awareness, and long-term brand authority.

### Challenge

Despite strong brand presence, CIMB faced several SEO challenges:

- Product and category pages underperforming due to limited keyword depth and insufficient semantic coverage
- Lack of strong topical authority reducing competitiveness for high-value financial keywords
- Existing blog content contained redundancies and outdated material, diluting overall content quality signals
- Internal approval processes within a large financial institution slowed content publishing cycles
- Delayed deployment reduced the speed of SEO impact in a fast-moving, competitive banking landscape

### Solution

Toggle repositioned the SEO strategy toward a content-led authority model:

**#1. Systematic Content Pruning** — Conducted a comprehensive audit of existing blog content. Removed, consolidated, or optimised redundant and underperforming articles. Improved overall content quality signals to strengthen domain authority.

**#2. High-Intent Content Development** — Identified keyword gaps across priority banking and financial topics. Developed high-intent, keyword-focused articles targeting informational and commercial queries. Strengthened semantic keyword coverage to improve ranking potential.

**#3. Strengthening Topical Authority** — Aligned content creation with CIMB's core financial product pillars. Built structured internal linking frameworks to reinforce content clusters. Focused on long-term authority building rather than isolated keyword wins.

**#4. Workflow & Stakeholder Collaboration** — Collaborated closely with internal teams to streamline approval processes. Established clearer publishing workflows to reduce deployment delays. Prioritised high-impact financial topics to accelerate measurable SEO gains.

**Services delivered:** Strategic Content Audit and Blog Pruning · Keyword Gap Analysis and Authority Mapping · Development of High-Intent Financial Articles · Internal Linking and Content Clustering Optimisation · Cross-Functional Collaboration to Improve Publishing Efficiency · Ongoing Performance Monitoring and SEO Refinement

### Results

| Metric                        | Result                                            |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Organic impressions increase  | +52.6%                                            |
| Organic clicks growth         | +34.5%                                            |
| Keyword position improvements | Double-digit gains across multiple priority terms |
| Period                        | 12 months                                         |

### Key insight

In highly competitive financial sectors, building topical authority is more impactful than isolated keyword optimisation. Systematic blog pruning can significantly enhance overall site quality signals and ranking potential. Cross-functional collaboration is critical when working within large organisations with complex approval workflows. Sustainable SEO growth in YMYL industries requires a long-term authority-building strategy rather than short-term tactical wins.
