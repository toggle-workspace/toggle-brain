📲 The Sales Bot — Team Guide

A simple guide to the Toggle sales bot on Telegram. Two minutes to set up, then it keeps your leads from going cold — and you update everything in a few taps. No more hunting through the spreadsheet.

What the bot does for you

  • Reminds you every Sunday about your leads that have gone quiet.
  • Lets you update them in taps — pick the stage, mark warm/cold, add a note. The tracker updates by itself.
  • Lets you add a new lead from your phone the moment you leave a meeting.

You never open the spreadsheet. You just tap buttons in Telegram.

1 Join the bot (once, ~2 min)

  1. Install Telegram if you don't have it (free, App Store / Play Store).
  2. Open this link: https://t.me/Togglenudgebot
  3. Tap Start (or type /start).
  4. The bot shows a list of names. Tap your name.
  5. Done ✅ — you're in. The bot now knows which leads are yours.
Can't see your name in the list? Tell Zaid — it just means your name isn't on any lead in the tracker yet. He'll fix it in a second.

2 When the bot nudges you (every Sunday)

Each Sunday morning the bot sends you the leads that have gone quiet. For each one:

  1. It asks "What's the stage now?" → tap one:
    Not startedIn progressBlockedCompleted
  2. Then "Warm or cold?" → tap Warm or Cold (or Skip).
  3. Then "Add a note?" → type a quick note (e.g. "client wants to revisit after Raya"), or send /skip.

That's it. The tracker updates and the lead's "last contact" date is set to today automatically.

Not now? Tap 💤 Snooze to be reminded later, or 🔕 Not mine if a lead was assigned to you by mistake.

3 Add a new lead (anytime — e.g. right after a meeting)

In the chat with the bot:

  1. Type /newlead
  2. Bot asks for the client / company name → type it (e.g. "Acme Sdn Bhd").
  3. Bot asks who the PIC is → tap a name, or type a new one.
  4. Bot asks for a note → type it (e.g. "met at expo, wants Q3 proposal"), or send /skip.
  5. Bot says "Added ✅" — the lead is now in the tracker, dated today.

From then on it's a normal lead and the bot will remind whoever you assigned it to.

Assigned it to a teammate? If they're on the bot, they get an instant ping with the client name and your note — no waiting for Sunday.
Started /newlead by mistake? Just send /skip to stop.

Quick answers

Do I need to install anything besides Telegram?
No.
Does it cost me anything?
No — it's free.
Will it be slow?
No. Replies come back in a couple of seconds.
It says my name is "already linked to another account."
Someone else's phone is registered under your name. Tell Zaid — he'll unlink it and you can /start again.
I registered but didn't get a Sunday nudge.
Either you have no quiet leads (great!) or your name on the leads doesn't match the name you tapped. Type /stale to check, or ask Zaid.
