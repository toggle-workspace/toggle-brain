# Paid-media audit, TikTok x Toggle workshop attendees

Top-level audit of every attending brand's observable paid activity, run as
pre-workshop sales intelligence.

> **INTERNAL ONLY.** None of this may reach the client-facing pathway pages in
> `sites/tiktok-workshop-offerings/`. Those pages are read by the people
> described here, on their own phones, at the event. The findings below are
> blunt by design; the pages are not. That separation is enforced in the site's
> build script and must not be crossed by hand.

---

## Method, and what it can and cannot tell you

| Source | Status for Malaysia | Used? |
|---|---|---|
| **Meta Ad Library** | Live, real data, MYR-denominated | ✅ Every count below |
| **Google Ads Transparency Centre** | JavaScript shell; no data via fetch. Its internal endpoint returns `{}` without an advertiser ID | ❌ Not retrievable |
| **TikTok Commercial Content Library** | **EEA, Switzerland and UK only.** Malaysia is not covered | ❌ Not available |

Three consequences worth holding onto:

1. **Every Google row below is "unknown", not "zero".** The Transparency Centre
   renders fine for a human, just not for a fetcher, so it is a manual browser
   check if you want it: `adstransparency.google.com/?region=MY&domain=<domain>`.
2. **TikTok paid activity is not verifiable for any Malaysian brand.** TikTok's
   library exists as an EU Digital Services Act compliance surface, not
   voluntary global disclosure.
3. **The Ad Library never exposes spend** for commercial ads, only for political
   and issue ads. No spend figure appears anywhere in this document, and any
   that circulates elsewhere is self-declared. See "Corrections" at the end.

Counts are `estimated_total_count` from the Ad Library, which varies with the
country filter and is an estimate rather than a ledger. Page-ID-level queries
were used wherever a page could be identified, since keyword search materially
understates volume, HE Medical returned 10 on a keyword search and 164 on a
page-ID query.

Ad snapshot pages would not load, so destination type (lead form vs WhatsApp vs
landing page) and format mix (static vs video vs carousel) are **inferred from
titles and CTAs**, not observed. They are labelled where they appear.

## The five findings worth opening a conversation with

### 1. Common Ground is paying to show placeholder text, since August 2023
Verified directly at page level. **7 of 15 active ads** on `CommonGround.work`
(page `1051442348287602`) carry the literal headline `{{product.name}}`, an
unrendered catalogue token. Creation dates on the broken set:

`2023-08-18` · `2025-06-03` · `2025-08-07` ×3 · `2026-01-09` · `2026-04-24`

So a broken dynamic/catalogue setup has been shipping placeholder copy to
prospects for close to three years, across at least four separate build
sessions, and nobody has caught it. All spend is Malaysia-only; nothing found
for the Hive or The Cluster in MY or SG, so seven of their eight markets have no
observable paid presence.

The working ads are competent. *"Premium KLCC Private Offices from RM1,099/mo"*,
*"Celebrate Merdeka with Up to 50% OFF Private Offices"* and *"Ambition Lives Here |
Common Ground Jaya One"* all land, which makes the contrast sharper rather than softer.

**Use it like this:** open the Ad Library on your phone in front of them. This is
the single most demonstrable finding in the entire set.

### 2. HM Aerospace has two ads in its lifetime, one of them blank
Page `104973511737519`, queried with `ALL` status, returns exactly two ads:

- `Commercial Pilot License`, created 2026-08-17
- `HM Aerospace |   |   |   |   |`, created 2026-08-10, **five empty title fields**

That is the entire paid Meta history of a business selling programmes at roughly
RM300k per cadet.

Meanwhile **Layang Layang Flying Academy** (`717287595022836`) is running an
active MY ad created 2026-08-17: *"👇 DM us or click Learn More to secure your
spot for the September 2026 intake before it's full."* Deadline urgency plus a
DM funnel, aimed at the same intake.

Curiosity worth confirming in person: the two ads were created **one second
apart** (epoch 1786936870 and 1786936871), which suggests the same agency or the
same automated tool is running both accounts. If true, their agency is doing
better work for their competitor.

### 3. TIME is losing its own brand feed to its resellers
The brand page `Time Internet` (`198416760199558`) runs 21 active ads covering
both consumer (*"Kabel Besar, Untung Besar | TIME Fibre Home Broadband"*) and
enterprise (*"Work Faster. Scale Smarter."*), so there is no one-cycle gap.

But a reseller swarm is louder and cheaper-looking on the same terms:

- `TIME FIBRE Internet` (`108551564995352`), 13+ active ads, all titled
  identically *"TIME 600MBPS RM99"*
- `TIME Authorized Distributor MY` (`237745724859614`), *"600MBPS ONLY RM99"*
  repeated four times in one title, and **billed in USD**, i.e. an offshore-run
  account
- `Time Fibre - 最快宽频` (`105205465231734`), Chinese-language price ads
- Multi-brand agents running *"Unifi / Maxis / TIME / CelcomDigi Authorised
  Agent…"*, so Unifi resellers are advertising into the same feed

**The pitch:** TIME pays to build the demand its own channel harvests, on price
messaging it does not control, in a feed where its brand sits next to a
competitor's reseller.

### 4. Ayu Borneo has never switched an ad off
12 active ads on page `107961894739051`, and every ad the page has ever run is
still running. The oldest was created **2026-03-10** and is still live five
months later. Titles are essentially blank, the only text is *"Chat with us"*,
*"Ayu Borneo Family Wellness Centre Malaysia"*, *"www.ayuborneo.my"*. No offer,
no price, no service named, and no location text anywhere.

Their own TikTok states **24-25 outlets**. So an estate that size is being fed by
a single undifferentiated national click-to-WhatsApp campaign with no geo-split
(inferred from the absence of location text and of near-duplicate creative sets).

This is the cheapest cost-per-acquisition win available to anyone in the room,
and it needs no new creative to demonstrate, only splitting what exists by
catchment.

### 5. LactoDay's exposure is compliance, not performance
The best-run account of the growth tier: 15 active ads, seven created in the 48
hours before the audit, including their first English-language batch
(*"Always last on her own list?"*, *"3 seconds. That's the whole routine."*).
Chinese stock runs back to March 2026.

The risk sits in the positioning, not the media buying. The page itself is named
**LactoDay I 女神私密养护益生菌**, 私密养护 positions the product as intimate
or feminine care, which is an implied health indication carried by the page name
rather than by a single reviewable ad. Paired with *"差别不在数字，在活着抵达"*,
a strain-survivability claim, that is the surface exposed if NPRA or a competitor
complains. No disease-cure claims were found.

**This is the conversation no platform rep will have with them**, and it is the
clearest demonstration of vertical-specific expertise in the set.

Note the sister business: **Beaute Mori** (`505531909577924`) has run **one ad
ever**, around 2025-05-23, none active. A distribution house with real pharmacy
shelf presence and effectively no paid history.

## The rest, briefly

**HE Medical.** 164 active ads, essentially nothing paused, running back to
mid-June 2026 in an even EN/BM/ZH split. Volume and translation discipline are
genuinely there; creative diversity is not. Seven-plus near-identical variants of
*"Book private consultation with our doctor"*, alongside sharper work that proves
they can do better (*"Non-Surgical Girth Enhancement | Immediate Results"*,
*"30 分钟男士提升方案 | 快速且安全"*, and a Bangsar South branch launch in
Chinese). Recruitment ads run off the same page. Pipe-separated repeated titles
indicate dynamic/Advantage+ creative with duplicated fields (inferred). No
TikTok account at all, for a category whose audience lives there.

**Parcel Daily.** The healthiest testing discipline in the whole set: ~10
distinct concepts on a 1-2 week refresh cadence across three languages
(*"Hantar Parcel Serendah RM5"*, *"Compare All Courier Rates in One Platform"*,
*"新用户优惠：首 3KG 只需 RM5"*). The gap is strategic rather than operational: every ad sells a discounted first parcel, so they are buying one-off senders
while the high-lifetime-value e-commerce seller goes barely addressed. Nothing
runs under the AXVEL entity name.

**ICMS.** Five ads, all created since 2026-07-22, which reads as a first real
Meta push rather than a sustained programme. The football differentiator *is*
being used, in Malay: *"Jadikan Minat Bola Sepak Sebagai Kerjaya"*. Also
*"Level Up with ICMS MBA"* and *"Bina Kerjaya Dalam Penguatkuasaan"*. A
`study.icms.edu.my` microsite exists. Search trap to avoid: "International Council of Malaysian
Scholars" shares the acronym, and `@icmscampus` is a
different institution in Sydney.

**Destinasi TV.** Exactly one ad in the library's history, created 2026-08-09,
with no link headline, consistent with a boosted organic post rather than a built
campaign. Important: a keyword search returns ~73 ads, but the other ~72 are homestays,
travel agencies and rival broadcasters whose copy merely contains the word
"destinasi". They are not sponsors advertising on Destinasi TV either. They are
just keyword collisions. Follower counts were not machine-readable; the million-plus figure is
claimed, not measured.

**JOMhotel.** Zero entries across four query shapes, active or historical. The
TikTok account still sits on the pre-rebrand handle **@justtonite_malaysia**, ten
months after the October 2025 rename, so brand equity is split across two names.
No Visit Malaysia 2026 reference in any creative.

**Supervisor.my.** No paid presence anywhere, and the site carries a genuinely
differentiated, quantified claim ("a minimum of 15% savings on renovation costs",
1M+ sq ft supervised, financial-institution client list) that is doing no work.
It does not surface for generic renovation-supervision queries, where TÜV SÜD and
interior-design firms dominate. **Pitching them social-first would be the wrong
call**, this is a search and LinkedIn problem.

**The Earnest Co.** No ads under either name. A B2B/export posture (MD2 and
Morris pineapples, GlobalGAP/MyGAP in progress) with a "Get a Quote" form and a
free-consultation CTA already on the site. Nothing drives traffic into a funnel
that already exists.

**Damia Nash.** No ads by her, and none featuring her as talent surfaced.
Recent TikTok content is hijabi fashion and outfit inspiration, so the
positioning reads fashion-adjacent as much as beauty. No product, shop or
link-in-bio funnel found. Flag: `instagram.com/damia_nash` is a private account with 44 followers and an
unrelated bio. Almost certainly a different person squatting the handle, not her.

**Wild Rice Media / Rizqey.** No Ad Library entries, no Malaysian domain, no
registered entity, no social presence, as of 2026-08-18. ("Wild Rice" hits are a
Singapore theatre company and a UK film house.)

**AceBrix.** A clean zero, not keyword noise. No site, no entity, no socials.
Whatever exists is personal-profile activity by the founder.

## Corrections to `tiktok-rsvp-prospect-research.md`

1. **Every TikTok spend figure in that document is self-declared on the RSVP
   form, not verified.** Malaysia is not in TikTok's ad library, so there is no
   way to corroborate any of it. Parcel Daily's ">RM4k/mo on TikTok" and HE
   Medical's ">RM20,000/mo" budget signal are claims made by attendees, and
   should be treated as intent signals rather than facts in any pitch.
2. **Parcel Daily's read should flip.** The research characterises them as
   "already spending with weak organic", implying poor execution. Their Meta
   account is the best-tested one in the growth tier. Their problem is *what* the
   creative sells, not *how well* it is run. Pitching them on basics would land
   badly.
3. **Ayu Borneo has ~24-25 outlets, not 5-8** (per their own TikTok). The
   client-facing pathway page has been corrected so it no longer implies a small
   estate.
4. **Destinasi TV's follower count is unverified.** The million-plus figure could
   not be confirmed from any machine-readable source.

## Suggested next actions

1. **Manual Google pass.** The Transparency Centre works in a browser. Fifteen
   domains, ten minutes, and it closes the one gap in this audit.
2. **Screenshot the Common Ground and HM Aerospace ads before the event.** Both
   are live now and both could be fixed at any moment. A screenshot in your
   pocket is worth more than a description on the day.
3. **Decide the Supervisor.my position before you speak to them.** They are the
   one attendee for whom the workshop's core subject is the wrong answer, and
   saying so plainly is a stronger move than selling them TikTok.
4. **Re-run this audit the week of the event** if anything here is going into a
   deck. Ad Library state changes daily, and the two most quotable findings are
   both one bug-fix away from disappearing.
