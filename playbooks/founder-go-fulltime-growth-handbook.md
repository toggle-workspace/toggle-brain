## Part A — The go-full-time admin & compliance checklist (Malaysia)

> Compliance figures verified 2026-06-20 against KWSP/PERKESO/LHDN summaries
> (sources at the bottom). **Confirm exact brackets on the official portals
> before filing** — rates and ceilings change.

### A1. Personal financial runway (do this before you quit)
- [ ] **6+ months of personal expenses** banked, separate from business cash.
      Going full-time means losing your salaried income *and* employer EPF/SOCSO.
- [ ] **EPF voluntary self-contribution** — as a business owner you have no
      employer EPF. Use **i-Saraan / Self-Contribution** to keep building your
      retirement + dividend. Decide a monthly amount and automate it.
- [ ] **Personal SOCSO** — register under the **Self-Employment Social Security
      Scheme (SKSPS)** so you're covered for injury/invalidity once you no
      longer have employer coverage.
- [ ] **Private medical + income-protection insurance** — you lose any group
      coverage the moment you go solo.

### A2. Business entity (decide before registering as an employer)
- [ ] **Sole Proprietor / Enterprise** (SSM, cheap, fast) **vs Sdn Bhd**
      (limited liability, separates personal assets, looks credible to bigger
      ASEAN clients, cleaner for hiring + raising). **For an agency that intends
      to hire and scale → Sdn Bhd is the default recommendation.**
- [ ] Register / confirm the entity with **SSM**.
- [ ] Open a **dedicated business bank account** — never commingle.
- [ ] Engage a **bookkeeper / accountant** (monthly). Cheapest insurance you'll buy.

### A3. Register as an EMPLOYER (the EPF & SOCSO piece you asked about)
Trigger: the moment you have **one employee** (and once you incorporate Sdn Bhd,
you as a director/employee may count). Do all four:

- [ ] **EPF / KWSP** — register as an employer via the **i-Akaun (Employer)**
      portal (or a KWSP branch) within **7 days** of hiring your first employee.
      You get an employer reference number.
- [ ] **SOCSO + EIS / PERKESO** — register the business and employees via the
      **ASSIST Portal** (assist.perkeso.gov.my). SOCSO and EIS are registered
      together.
- [ ] **LHDN employer tax file (No. E)** — register as an employer with LHDN;
      this is what you use to remit monthly **PCB/MTD** (income tax withholding)
      on staff salaries. Do this via **MyTax / e-Daftar**.
- [ ] **HRD Corp** — **mandatory once you hit 10+ Malaysian employees** (levy on
      monthly payroll; commonly **1%** at 10+, with an optional **0.5%** band for
      5–9 staff in covered sectors). Register within 3 months of crossing the
      threshold. *Confirm your sector's exact rate.*

### A4. 2026 statutory contribution rates (employer's monthly cost of a hire)
Budget **~14.95% on top of gross salary** for a typical local hire under 60:

| Scheme | Employer | Employee | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **EPF** | **13%** (salary ≤ RM5,000) / **12%** (> RM5,000) | 11% | Below age 60, citizen/PR. |
| **SOCSO** | **1.75%** | 0.5% | Wage ceiling **RM6,000**. |
| **EIS** | **0.2%** | 0.2% | Wage ceiling **RM6,000**. Ages 18–60. |
| **PCB/MTD** | — | per tax table | You withhold + remit for the employee. |

- [ ] **All contributions due by the 15th** of the following month. Late = penalties.
- [ ] Run payroll through software (e.g. payroll.my, or your accountant) so
      EPF/SOCSO/EIS/PCB are auto-calculated and the contribution files generated.

### A5. Tax & invoicing readiness
- [ ] **SST — Service Tax:** digital advertising / marketing services are
      taxable. Register once your taxable turnover crosses the threshold
      (**RM500,000** / 12 months for the relevant category) and charge **service
      tax** on invoices. *Confirm the current rate + your service category with
      your accountant — this changed recently and is easy to get wrong.*
- [ ] **e-Invoicing (LHDN MyInvois)** — Malaysia's e-invoice mandate is rolling
      out by turnover band. Check which phase your revenue puts you in and get
      your invoicing flow compliant.
- [ ] Standardise **contracts / MSAs + SOWs**, payment terms (Net 30 default per
      `clients/_TEMPLATE`), and a deposit policy. Get **Professional Indemnity
      insurance** before signing bigger retainers.

> **The founder's trap:** treating EPF/SOCSO/PCB as "later." They're a hard
> ~15% loading on every salary and a legal obligation from employee #1. Price it
> into your rate card *now* (Phase 0.1) or your margins will lie to you.

## Part B — The 90-day lead engine (get business conversations fast)

The objective is not "revenue in 90 days" — it's **a repeatable flow of qualified
conversations**, the leading indicator that revenue follows. Target: **5+ new
qualified conversations / week by Day 90.**

### B0. Foundation — niche + offer (Week 1–2)
Everything compounds off these two.

- **Niche down (Iman Gadzhi / David C. Baker).** "We help everyone" converts
  nobody. Toggle's strongest proof is **higher-ed in Malaysia** (UNITAR, IJN
  University College, City University, Sunway TES). **Lead with that vertical.**
  Specialisation is the single biggest lever on both close rate and price.
- **Build the Grand Slam Offer (Hormozi, *$100M Offers*).** Maximise the value
  equation:
  > **Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort)**
  - Dream outcome: "Fill your intake without burning budget on ads that don't convert."
  - Likelihood ↑: case studies, a guarantee, your **diagnose-first** method.
  - Time delay ↓ / effort ↓: done-for-you, fast onboarding, a 14-day first win.
  - This *is* Toggle's edge — the agency profile already sells
    **system diagnosis before spend**. That's a Godfather offer the
    "run more ads" crowd can't match.

### B1. The Core Four channels (Hormozi, *$100M Leads*)
Run all four; don't wait for one to be perfect.

1. **Warm outreach — your highest-ROI channel, do it Week 1.** You have a 50+
   relationship base and past clients. DM/call/email everyone who knows you with
   a *specific* offer (a free Growth System Audit, below). Warm always converts
   highest; most founders skip it out of ego. Don't.
2. **Free content (1-to-many warm).** Already a P1 internal todo
   (`cockpit/todos/_internal.md`): ship the Hormozi/Galloway/Marketing
   Millennials batch across **LinkedIn / IG / Shorts**. Document the diagnose-first
   method publicly. **Give away the "what," sell the "how + done-for-you."**
3. **Cold outreach (Dream 100).** Chet Holmes' play: pick the **100 dream
   accounts** in your niche (Malaysian/ASEAN universities + colleges, then your
   second vertical). Hit them relentlessly with value, not pitches. Iman
   Gadzhi's mechanic: a personalised **Loom audit** of their funnel/ads as the
   opener — "results in advance."
4. **Paid ads (1-to-many cold).** You're a paid-media agency — eat your own
   cooking. Run a small budget driving to the lead magnet. Doubles as a live
   case study and credibility proof.

### B2. The lead magnet that fits Toggle perfectly
Sabri Suby's "Halo Strategy" + Hormozi's lead magnet: **give massive value
upfront.** Toggle's diagnose-first positioning makes this almost free to deliver:

> **"The Toggle Growth System Audit"** — a free teardown of a prospect's growth
> system (footfall conversion, sales first-response time, lead-form bounce, CRM
> gaps) — exactly the out-of-scope benchmarks the agency profile already
> champions. It *demonstrates* the method instead of describing it, and surfaces
> the real bottleneck → the paid engagement sells itself.

### B3. Chet Holmes' Buyer's Pyramid (why content + audits work)
Only **3%** of your market is buying now; **~7%** are open. The other 90% aren't
in-market *yet*. Education-based content + the free audit capture the 90% so
you're the obvious choice when they enter the 3%. Cold-pitching only fights over
the 3% everyone else is also chasing.

### B4. Pricing & sales discipline (Blair Enns / Win Without Pitching)
- **Stop free-pitching.** The audit is your value demo; full strategy is paid.
- **Value-based pricing**, not hours. Tie fees to the client's growth outcome.
- **Raise prices as proof accumulates** (Hormozi: most agencies are underpriced
  and under-niched). Your filled rate card (Phase 0.1) is where this lives.

## Part C — Build the machine so it's not just you (the scale lever)

Going full-time is step one; the trap is *becoming the bottleneck*.

- **Dan Martell — Buy Back Your Time / Replacement Ladder.** Offload your
  lowest-$/hour work first, in order: **admin → delivery → marketing → sales →
  leadership.** Your job is to climb to where only *you* can add value (offer,
  positioning, key sales, hiring). Each statutory hire (Part A) is a rung —
  which is exactly why EPF/SOCSO costs must be in the rate card.
- **Productise (Hormozi).** Turn the diagnose-first method into named, fixed-scope
  offers (Audit → Roadmap → Build → Retainer). Productised = teachable = scalable.
- **Systematise delivery.** The `playbooks/` + `generators/` already exist — feed
  them so a new hire delivers to your standard without you in the room.
