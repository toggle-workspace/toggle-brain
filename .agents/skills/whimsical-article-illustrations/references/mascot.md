# Mascot IP — Inky

> **This is the swap file.** To make a company-flavored version, replace this single file's character (name, look, personality, jobs) and update the character block in `references/prompt-template.md`. Everything else in the skill stays the same. See `MASCOT-SWAP.md` at the repo root.

## Character definition

**Inky** is the fixed visual IP of this illustration style — the English counterpart of Ian's original 小黑 ("Little Black").

Inky appears in every image by default. Inky is NOT a mascot sticker, not cute decoration — it's an absurd worker seriously helping the system run.

## Look

- A small, solid-black creature.
- White dot eyes.
- Thin legs, occasionally thin arms.
- The body can be a cylinder, a black bean, a black box, a funnel, a shadow, a hole, or the black block inside a machine.
- Slightly irregular, hand-drawn outline.
- Blank, dazed, calm, serious expression.

## Personality

- Very earnest, doing something slightly absurd.
- Like a low-key system operator.
- Dry humor, never mugging for the camera.
- A bit clumsy, but not dumb.
- Looks like it's genuinely in charge of one job inside a whiteboard sketch.

## Common duties

Give Inky the core action:

- Hauling material.
- Pulling lines to converge information sources.
- Stuck inside a breakpoint.
- Working a "judgment" lever inside a machine.
- Becoming a sorting funnel.
- Slicing open the "material fish."
- Stamping/handing off copy.
- Leading a handoff path by a string.
- Holding a warning sign over a pit.
- Reaching out of a hole but failing to catch the content.
- Off to the side hauling bricks, building a bridge, opening a door, sorting, taking notes.

## Bans

- Don't draw Inky as an over-cute mascot.
- Don't draw a children's-cartoon character.
- Don't give Inky elaborate clothing, emoji faces, or sparkly eyes.
- Don't let Inky just stand in a corner watching.
- Don't let Inky steal the structural expression.
- Don't draw Inky too commercial, too round, too polished.

## The test

If you remove Inky and the core metaphor still fully holds, Inky was too decorative. Rewrite the prompt so Inky becomes the action subject.
