# Style DNA

## In one line

Pure white, minimal, hand-drawn, generous white space, restrained, whimsical, product-sketch feel, handwritten notes, structure that's clear but never a manual.

Like someone who has spent years in AI, product, design, and dev tools sketching a quick explainer on a blank sheet of paper.

## Must

- 16:9 horizontal body illustration.
- Pure white background: no beige, warm gray, paper texture, gradient, shadow, grain, or vintage-paper feel.
- Black hand-drawn line art, mostly: thin lines, slight wobble, not mechanical, not vector, not heavy outlines.
- Lots of white space: subject occupies ~40%–60% of the frame, at least 35% empty, ideally one whole quiet zone.
- Sparse handwritten labels: at most 5–8 spots, each ideally 1–4 words.
- One image tells one core action, structure, state, or metaphor.
- Let structure express itself naturally — don't write the structure-type name on the image.

## Color

- **Black**: main line art, character, frame lines, structure, main text, main objects.
- **Red**: key call-outs, problems, emotional beats, critical warnings, results.
- **Orange**: main flow, path, arrows, automation direction, the A→B movement relationship.
- **Blue**: supplementary notes, mental state, system state, second-layer explanation, AI/assistant/automation hints.

Blue isn't required in every image. Keep color restrained — under rather than over.

## Never

- No commercial illustration.
- No PPT infographic.
- No formal flowchart.
- No course slide.
- No cute cartoon poster.
- No children's illustration.
- No complex architecture diagram.
- No polished flat illustration.
- No techy UI.
- No real app screenshots.
- No complex backgrounds, gradients, shadows, or textures.
- Don't explain every node.
- No top-left type title like "Workflow / System Architecture / Common Pitfalls / Roadmap."

## Aesthetic direction

Be whimsical, creative, interesting, clean, wildly imaginative.

Don't be cute, childish, over-complex, or stiff.
