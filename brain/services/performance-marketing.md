## Scope of work

The three tables below are the source for the `/scope-of-work` generator. Full format spec:
`templates/scope-of-work/README.md`. In short, `W0` is the start date and `W3` is three weeks after
it, anything else prints as written, `key` rows appear on the client's key dates page, and a
recurring row can name its first occurrence with `(first: W5 = label for the key dates page)`.

### In scope

| Deliverable | How much | Type | Typical timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Confirm access to your accounts and check what is already tracking | Every ad platform and analytics property in your quotation | once | W0 |
| Set up conversion tracking | Meta Pixel, GA4 and the Google Ads tag on the pages that matter | key | W1 |
| Plan the campaign structure and the audiences | [N] campaigns across [N] platforms | once | W1 |
| Write the ad copy and brief the creative we need | [N] ad variations per campaign | once | W2 |
| Build and launch the first campaigns | [N] campaigns, [N] ad sets | key | W2 |
| Review and improve the campaigns | Budget, audience, bid and placement changes | recurring | Every week |
| Check your budget pacing so the month lands where you planned | All live campaigns | recurring | Every week |
| Test new audiences, placements or bidding | [N] tests a month | recurring | Every month |
| Send your performance report | 1 report covering spend, results and cost per result | recurring | Every month, by the 5th working day (first: W5 = Your first performance report) |
| Run your review call | 60 minutes | recurring | Every month |

### Not in scope

- The ad budget itself. You pay Meta, Google and the other platforms directly.
- Video and photo shoots. We quote production separately.
- Landing page design and build. We quote that separately.
- Changes to your website beyond the tracking code.
- Calling, emailing or following up the leads the ads bring in.
- Setting up or managing your CRM.
- Any platform that is not named in your quotation.
- Product feed and catalog management for online stores, unless your quotation lists it.

### What we need from you

| What we need | When we need it |
|---|---|
| Admin access to every ad account, business manager and analytics property | W0 |
| A payment method loaded on each ad platform | W0 |
| Your offer, your pricing, and the questions your sales team hears most | W0 |
| A developer, or CMS access, so we can place the tracking code | W0 |
| Approval of new ads within three working days of us sending them | Ongoing |
| Your sales or enrollment numbers, so we can tie ad results to revenue | Every month |
