# Social Media Management

Run the organic side of your social accounts: the calendar, the posts, the captions, and the replies.

- **Who it's for:** Businesses whose customers check the profile before they enquire, and find it empty or months out of date.

## Scope of work

Format notes are in `brain/services/performance-marketing.md`.

### In scope

| Deliverable | How much | Type | Typical timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agree the content pillars and what each platform is for | [N] platforms | key | W1 |
| Deliver your first content calendar | 1 calendar covering [N] posts | key | W2 |
| Deliver the calendar for the coming month | 1 calendar covering [N] posts | recurring | Every month, [N] working days before the month starts |
| Write the captions and hashtags | 1 set per post | recurring | Every month |
| Schedule and publish the posts | [N] posts a month on each platform | recurring | Every month (first: W3 = First posts published) |
| Reply to comments and direct messages | Within [N] hours, Monday to Friday | recurring | Every working day |
| Send your social report | 1 report covering reach, engagement and follower growth | recurring | Every month, by the 5th working day (first: W6 = Your first social report) |

### Not in scope

- Paid social ads and the budget behind them. That work sits under Performance Marketing.
- Finding, briefing and paying influencers or creators.
- Handling complaints, refunds and support questions beyond a first holding reply.
- Crisis communications and public statements.
- Filming at your events, stores or premises.
- Any platform not named in your quotation.
- Posts in a language not named in your quotation.
- Replies outside Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm.

### What we need from you

| What we need | When we need it |
|---|---|
| Admin access to every platform we manage | W0 |
| One person we can ask when a comment needs a real answer from your business | W0 |
| Your offers, promotions and product news, at least [N] working days before you want them posted | Ongoing |
| Approval of the content calendar within three working days | Ongoing |
