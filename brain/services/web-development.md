### Not in scope

- Writing the copy for the pages. We quote copywriting separately.
- Photography, video and illustration.
- Hosting, domain and SSL fees. You pay those directly to the provider.
- Paid plugins, themes and third-party licenses. You pay those directly.
- Maintenance, updates and new pages once the 30 day bug fix window closes.
- Any page beyond the number in your quotation.
- Online store, payment or booking functions, unless your quotation lists them.
- Moving old blog posts or products across from an existing site, unless your quotation lists it.
- Mobile app development.
