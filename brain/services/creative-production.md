# Creative Production

Produce high-performing visuals and video assets designed to capture attention and drive results. Covers the creative brief and the creative itself.

- **Who it's for:** Businesses running paid media or social who need a steady supply of assets, not a one-off campaign.
- **Related case studies:** [UNITAR](../case-studies/unitar.md) · [Kith & Kin](../case-studies/kith-and-kin.md)

## Scope of work

Format notes are in `brain/services/performance-marketing.md`.

### In scope

| Deliverable | How much | Type | Typical timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Write the creative brief | 1 brief per campaign | key | W1 |
| Present the first concepts | [N] concepts | key | W2 |
| Produce the static assets | [N] assets a month, in the sizes each platform needs | recurring | Every month (first: W3 = First static assets delivered) |
| Edit the short videos | [N] videos a month, up to [N] seconds each | recurring | Every month (first: W3 = First videos delivered) |
| Write the copy that runs with each asset | 1 set of copy per asset | recurring | Every month |
| Resize approved assets for every placement in your media plan | All placements in the plan | recurring | Every month |
| Make the changes you ask for | 2 rounds per asset | recurring | Every month |

### Not in scope

- Shoots with a crew, studio, location or talent. We quote production separately.
- Talent, model and voiceover fees, and the licenses to keep using their footage.
- Stock photo, video and music licenses.
- Logo design, brand identity and brand guidelines.
- Print, packaging and out of home artwork.
- 3D, full animation, and motion work beyond simple text and logo movement.
- A third round of changes on an asset. We quote that as new work.

### What we need from you

| What we need | When we need it |
|---|---|
| Your logo files, fonts and brand guidelines | W0 |
| Product photos, footage, or samples we can shoot ourselves | W1 |
| Approval of the creative brief before we start on concepts | W1 |
| Feedback collected into one list per round, sent by one person | Ongoing |
