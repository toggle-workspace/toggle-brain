# Generative Engine Optimization

Get your business named when someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude or Google AI Overviews for a recommendation.

- **Who it's for:** Businesses in a category buyers research before they shortlist, where being left out of the answer costs you the meeting.

## Scope of work

Format notes are in `brain/services/performance-marketing.md`.

### In scope

| Deliverable | How much | Type | Typical timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agree the prompts we will test, written the way your customers ask | [N] prompts | once | W2 |
| Run the first test across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and Claude | [N] prompts on 4 engines | key | W2 |
| Give you the baseline: where you appear, where you do not, and what is said about you | 1 document | key | W3 |
| Correct your company facts wherever the models read them | Your site, Google Business Profile, directories and review sites | once | W4 |
| Shape your pages so a model can quote them | [N] pages a month | recurring | Every month |
| Rerun the prompt test and report what changed | [N] prompts | recurring | Every month, by the 5th working day (first: W6 = Your first GEO visibility report) |

### Not in scope

- A guaranteed mention. The models decide what they say, and their answers change without notice.
- Paying an AI company for placement. No such product exists on these engines today.
- Replying to or managing your reviews.
- Removing a negative mention from a model's answer.
- Creating or editing a Wikipedia page. Wikipedia does not allow paid editing on a client's behalf.
