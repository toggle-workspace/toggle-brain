# Search Engine Optimization

Help clients find you online with SEO that strengthens search presence and brings consistent, high-quality traffic.

- **Who it's for:** Businesses whose customers search for the problem before they search for the brand.
- **Related case studies:** [Great Eastern](../case-studies/great-eastern-sg.md) · [Singlife](../case-studies/singlife.md) · [CIMB Malaysia](../case-studies/cimb-malaysia.md)

## Scope of work

Format notes are in `brain/services/performance-marketing.md`.

### In scope

| Deliverable | How much | Type | Typical timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crawl your site and run the technical audit | Up to [N] pages | key | W2 |
| Research the keywords worth ranking for | [N] priority keywords | once | W2 |
| Give you the fix list, ordered by what moves rankings first | 1 document | key | W3 |
| Apply the technical fixes | Up to [N] fixes a month | recurring | Every month (first: W4 = First round of technical fixes applied) |
| Rewrite page titles and meta descriptions | [N] pages a month | recurring | Every month |
| Improve the content on your existing pages | [N] pages a month | recurring | Every month |
| Link related pages to each other | Across the pages we work on | recurring | Every month |
| Watch Search Console for errors and ranking drops | Every indexed page | recurring | Every week |
| Send your SEO report | 1 report covering rankings, traffic and the work done | recurring | Every month, by the 5th working day (first: W6 = Your first SEO report) |

### Not in scope

- Writing new blog articles or landing page copy. We quote content separately.
- Buying backlinks or paying for links. We do not do it, and it puts your site at risk of a Google penalty.
- Redesigning or rebuilding your website.
- Translating pages into another language.
- Developer work beyond what your CMS lets us change.
- Google Ads and any other paid search work.
- A guaranteed position for any keyword. Google decides rankings, and no agency can promise one.

### What we need from you

| What we need | When we need it |
|---|---|
| Access to Google Search Console and Google Analytics | W0 |
| CMS admin access, or a developer who can apply our fixes | W0 |
| The products, services and locations you want to be found for | W0 |
| Approval of page changes within five working days | Ongoing |
