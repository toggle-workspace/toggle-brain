# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) — Toggle Solutions

**Purpose.** The 10-point profile of the client Toggle *wins with and keeps*. Used for lead
qualification, sales prep, pipeline scoring (`Sales/sales-pipeline.md`), and proposal targeting.
This is the **retainer / growth-partner** ICP — the DIY founder crowd belongs to Toggle Bespoke
(`brain/positioning/bespoke-product.md`), not here.

> **Sweet spot in one line:** An established, cash-flowing, old-economy operator selling a
> physical or consumer product with clean advertising rules, run by an owner who knows their
> business but not their CAC — in Malaysia, ready to scale across ASEAN.

Every point below is anchored to evidence already in the brain. Green flags qualify; red flags
disqualify.

---

## The 10 points

### 1. Established old-economy operator — not a startup
**Green:** 7+ years trading, real operations — retail footfall, distributors, a sales team, an
inbox of inbound, a CRM (however messy). **Red:** pre-Series-A app, "we're still finding the model."

*Why:* Toggle's entire edge is diagnosing the system *before* selling ads — footfall conversion,
sales first-response time, CRM gaps (`brain/positioning/agency-profile.md`). That diagnostic only
has surface area on a business that actually operates. Every proven case is an incumbent, not a
startup (UNITAR est. 1997; Kith & Kin developer; established consumer brands).

### 2. Proven product-market fit + healthy, self-funded cash flow
**Green:** the product already sells; the ask is "scale what works." Profitable or founder-funded.
**Red:** VC-dependent burn, or still hunting for PMF.

*Why:* Toggle scales a working engine — it doesn't invent demand or fix a broken product. Kualesa
and Al Hidayah were **already selling**; Toggle multiplied ROAS/orders on top. VC-funded startups
also cut the agency first when the runway tightens — structurally high-churn.

### 3. Physical / consumer product with clear unit economics (FMCG & similar)
**Green:** FMCG, F&B, consumer goods, retail, homeware, apparel, pet, beauty (non-claims),
publications, education products — anything with a real AOV, margin, and LTV. **Red:** intangible,
bespoke-quote, or "it depends" pricing where ROAS can't be computed.

*Why:* Toggle is judged on revenue, not vanity metrics (`brain/positioning/differentiators.md` #1).
That claim only lands when the maths is legible: Al Hidayah 11.5x ROI, EduKids 6x ROAS
(`brain/case-studies/`). Clean unit economics = provable, compounding wins.

### 4. Category with no marketing-regulatory drag
**Green:** you can advertise it freely on Meta / TikTok / Google today. **Red:** finance, insurance,
banking, health claims, pharma, vice — anything gated by BNM / MAS / MQA / platform ad policy.

*Why:* Toggle's regulated clients (CIMB, Great Eastern, Singlife) are all **SEO-only** — the paid
performance engine can't run where compliance blocks it (`brain/geos/malaysia.md`,
`brain/geos/singapore.md`). Regulated categories cap Toggle to its lowest-leverage service. Avoid.

### 5. Owner-led buyer who is NOT marketing/tech-savvy
**Green:** the decision-maker is a founder / GM / MD who knows their product cold but can't define
CAC, ROAS, pixel, or CAPI — and wants to *hand the whole thing to one trusted partner*. **Red:**
in-house CMO/growth team, or a technical founder who wants a tool + one hire and will DIY with AI.

*Why:* This is the exact fit for "everything under one partner" and "senior operators, not junior
account managers" (`brain/positioning/differentiators.md` #2–3). The savvy DIY founder is Toggle
**Bespoke's** customer, not Solutions' — routing them to a retainer is a mismatch that churns.

### 6. Under-built or broken marketing — big upside from a low base
**Green:** relies on organic / referrals / cold-calling, has no proper tracking, or is burning spend
with no system. **Red:** already running a mature, well-instrumented in-house paid operation.

*Why:* Toggle's biggest, most sellable results come from 0→1 or fixing broken setups: Kith & Kin had
**no paid presence at all** → +392% leads; UNITAR had rising CPLs → 47% CPL cut, 77% impression
share. The larger the before/after delta, the stronger the case study and the easier the ROI story.

### 7. Real, retainer-viable budget with runway for 6+ months
**Green:** can commit a monthly retainer **plus** meaningful pass-through ad spend, and stay the
course past the learning phase. **Red:** one-off RM2–3k "let's test it" money that can't outlast
optimisation.

*Why:* The model is monthly retainer + separately-billed ad spend (`brain/pricing/rate-card-my.md`).
Established, cash-flowing players sustain this; thin-funded startups can't — and short budgets
produce high effective CAC and early churn (the exact failure mode to design out). The
performance-only / revenue-share model (`brain/process.md`) also fits best where volume is real.

### 8. Malaysia-first, ASEAN-expansion-ready — not SG-first
**Green:** HQ or core market in Malaysia; ambition to expand across SEA. **Red:** Singapore-first or
SG-only.

*Why:* Malaysia is home turf — channel and language fluency (BM/EN/中文/Tamil, TikTok Shop),
`brain/geos/malaysia.md`. ASEAN expansion is a proven growth vector: Kualesa scaled into SG, Brunei,
Philippines, Dubai. SG appears in the book **only** as SEO-only YMYL work and is a hard, low-margin
entry market — pursue it as expansion, not as the beachhead.

### 9. A definable KPI and a funnel Toggle can move end-to-end
**Green:** one hard success metric (leads, orders, ROAS, footfall, enrolments) and a bottleneck that
spans acquisition, conversion, or retention. **Red:** no measurable goal, or a goal Toggle can't
influence (e.g. purely offline B2B relationship sales).

*Why:* Toggle runs three connected pillars — acquisition, conversion, retention
(`brain/positioning/agency-profile.md`). It needs an actual funnel to work on. EduKids is the
template: paid + CRO + a dead website taken to RM14k/mo at a 5.5% conversion rate.

### 10. Results-over-contracts buyer who wants a long-term partner
**Green:** happy to start on a pilot / audit and let performance earn the retainer; values a senior
partner over the cheapest vendor; thinks in years. **Red:** agency-hopping on price, wants a
90-day guarantee, no interest in a relationship.

*Why:* Toggle retains through results, not lock-ins — trial → audit → partnership
(`brain/process.md`). Old-economy, relationship-driven owners fit this rhythm and compound into
multi-year accounts (high account LTV), which is where agency economics actually work.

## Anti-ICP — disqualify fast (mirrors the failure modes)

| Red flag | Why it fails Toggle |
|---|---|
| **Fintech / financial services** | High CAC + BNM/MAS/platform ad limits → paid engine can't run (see #4). |
| **Pre-PMF startup** | Toggle would be forced to do product/PMF work it isn't built to sell (see #1–2). |
| **Tech-savvy founder going "one marketer + max AI"** | Wants a tool, not a partner → Toggle **Bespoke**, not Solutions (see #5). |
| **SG-first / SG-only** | Tough, low-margin entry market; only ever SEO-only in the book (see #8). |
| **Thin, one-off budget** | Can't survive the learning phase → high effective CAC, early churn (see #7). |
| **Marketing-regulated product** (health claims, pharma, vice, gambling) | Compliance caps Toggle to SEO; kills the ROAS story (see #4). |
