# Proof gaps

What our case evidence can and cannot carry, written after the deck rebuild on 2026-08-26. Yi Yang's read was that the case studies are weak. Half of that is true and half of it was a presentation problem.

**The presentation problem, now fixed.** We had nine documented cases and the old deck used five, without the method behind any number and without the two strongest arguments we own. Mindvalley (business lead cost from $100 to $10 on $500,000 managed) and Kith and Kin (lead volume up 392% at RM35) were both missing. The rebuilt deck carries all nine, each with the method and a transfer line, filterable by vertical.

**The real problem.** Nine cases of outcomes, and almost no third-party evidence that any of it happened. That is what costs us the accounts we lose to larger agencies.

---

## What we can prove today

| Claim | Strongest evidence | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| National scale lead generation in higher education | UNITAR, past RM11m a month, 32,000+ leads, 47% lower cost per lead across three years | High. Long duration, large budget, sustained result. |
| Search authority in regulated categories | CIMB, Singlife, Great Eastern. Two insurers taken to position one with the same model. | High. Repeated, which reads as method rather than luck. |
| Direct to consumer commerce across ASEAN | Al Hidayah 11.5x, EduKids 6x on TikTok, Kualesa across five markets | Medium to high. Strong numbers, smaller budgets. |
| Hard currency and Western audience media | Mindvalley, $500,000 managed, five channels, three revenue lines | High on substance, aging. The account ended in March 2022. |
| Finding a cheap auction before the media plan | Kith and Kin, RM35 cost per lead in real estate | Medium. One account, three months. |

## What we cannot prove, in priority order

**1. Nobody but us says any of it is true.** Every case is written by Toggle, in Toggle's voice, with no client name attached to a quote, no logo permission recorded, and no client contactable as a reference. The profile claims 50+ testimonials rated five out of five, and none of them appear in a case file. This is the single largest gap and the cheapest one to close.

**2. No fast moving consumer goods case.** Colgate-tier brands buy brand outcomes measured through lift studies and media mix modeling, alongside television and out of home. We have no case in that shape at all, and our nine cases are all direct response. Pitching an FMCG brand on lead cost is answering a question they are not asking.

**3. No case that survives procurement.** Large accounts run vendor assessment: named team allocation, service levels, escalation paths, data processing terms, insurance, financial standing. None of our cases document how an engagement was run, only what it produced.

**4. Two verticals are sold but unproven.** `brain/positioning/agency-profile.md` lists business to business software and healthcare. There is no case for either. The deck's slide 11 now says this out loud, which is the honest interim answer, and a case is the real one.

**5. Three services are sold but unproven on their own.** Web development, conversion optimization and CRM or retention appear inside other cases, never as the case. A prospect buying a CRO sprint has nothing to read.

**6. Everything is 2025 or older.** Eight of nine cases are 2025 and Mindvalley is 2022. Nothing shows what we did this year. By the fourth quarter this reads as an agency describing its past.

**7. No failure is documented anywhere.** We have no written account of an engagement that did not work, what we learned, or a client we handed back. Slide 7 claims we walk away when the diagnosis says the problem is not marketing. That claim needs one real story behind it or it should come out of the deck.

## What to collect, ranked by effort against value

| Action | Effort | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Get three named client quotes with permission to publish, from UNITAR, Al Hidayah and Kith and Kin | Low. Three emails. | Closes gap 1, the largest one, this week. |
| Write one engagement as an operating story: team, cadence, escalation, what broke and how it was handled | Low. One afternoon. | Closes gap 3 and gives us the walk-away story for gap 7. |
| Publish a 2026 case from live work | Medium. Depends on a result landing. | Closes gap 6. Pick the account most likely to produce a number this quarter. |
| Screenshot evidence in every case: platform reporting, Search Console, the store dashboard | Medium. Access permitting. | Turns nine claims into nine checkable claims. |
| Run one CRO or CRM engagement as a standalone paid pilot and write it up | High. Needs a client. | Closes gap 5 and creates the entry product for accounts not ready for media. |
| Win one FMCG or one business to business software account, priced as a pilot | High. Sales problem, not a documentation problem. | Closes gaps 2 and 4. Until then slide 11 handles it honestly. |

## The rule this creates

A number goes in the deck only if it is in a file under `brain/case-studies/`. A file goes in that directory only with the method written down and the period stated. When a case gains client permission, a quote, or verifying evidence, note it in the file and update this page, because slide 11 of the deck is generated from what is written here.
